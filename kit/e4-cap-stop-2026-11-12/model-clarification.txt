The clarified protocol satisfies the main model: actual binding clock, supplied-state capture, synchronous view generations, completed history publication, and protection across all Discard waits. Keep three concrete qualifications before patching.

1. A read-only reservation peek needs exact-owner provenance, beyond matching baseline. The singleton currently records only{id,baseline,minting} (project.ts188). For the no-allocation capture, use it only when the supplied state is still the exact live private owner and baseline, the workspace binding agrees, and the singleton baseline matches. A departing prev capture must fall back to that document's existing reservation fields rather than inspect the next owner's singleton.

Preserve authoritative absence: when a matching peek has minting===false, do not merge an old mintingReserved:true back from the document. When no trusted reservation exists, preserve absence without allocating, releasing, normalizing or remembering device IDs. Keep normal allocating source/Save writers unchanged.

2. Atomic restore must preserve the existing account-authoring gate. Current sample restoration calls guarded setSampleValue; direct useTemplateStore.setState({selectedParts,sampleData}) bypasses the wrapper at templateStore.ts220. Perform the atomic update through a narrow store seam that preserves this authorization behavior. Selection can remain independently allowed as before; paused-account sample writes must remain refused. Clock/cue updates must also leave the published receipt consistent with the state actually accepted.

3. Generation-only fallback is invalidation, not persistence. It is appropriate for legacy/external owners lacking a retained envelope: advance only the existing bound owner's generation, with no envelope/reservation creation. It cannot establish that a no-binding, no-receipt selection was durably saved. The supported editor path is sound because EditorFoundation obtains its session before presenting selection controls; preserve that lifecycle and prove the first selection publishes the binding's actual nonzero opening clock. Without a valid binding or receipt, leave the view absent—never synthesize time zero or borrow another owner's time/history.
