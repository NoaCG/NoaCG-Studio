What's in a package

  • <name>.ograf.json: the OGraf v1 manifest (the entry point)
  • graphic.mjs: the Graphic Web Component, commented for reading
  • GUIDE.md: where the fields, the look and the motion live
  • FIELDS.md: the data contract, every field, type and default
  • lib/gsap.min.js + fonts/*.woff2: self-contained, no network

What is OGraf?

OGraf is the EBU's open standard for HTML broadcast graphics, one format any OGraf renderer can load. Try a package in the community OGraf DevTool.

Open the studio to change one, or read where NoaCG is taking the standard.

Made with NoaCG Studio, free and open source broadcast graphics: create, control, and put them on air from your browser. The starters are free to use and modify in your productions.
