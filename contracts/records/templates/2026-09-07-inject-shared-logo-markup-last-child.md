# templates/inject-shared-logo-markup-last-child

Rule: `templates/inject-shared-logo-markup-last-child`. Recorded 2026-09-07 on `claude/templates-contract-migration` at 0e351ca6.

The original contract records the rationale and constraints below. Source: src/templates/AGENTS.md, lines 215-227. - **shared/logoSlot.ts** - the shared OPTIONAL logo slot every `logo: 'optional'` design inherits   unless it hand-authors its own (`designHasLogoSlot`). The capability, the two arrangements and   the mark-is-not-a-picture rule are under "Fields & images" below; the measurements that settled   them - and every recorded exception - are **`docs/LOGO_SLOT.md`**. Three rules bind anything   that injects markup into a design it cannot see: **inject as the LAST child** (a first child   renumbers every `nth-child` selector the design wrote about its own children - lt02 put the name   under its underline), **cap the mark's height rather than fixing it** (a fixed height hands a   portrait crest the power to set the strap's height through its own aspect), and **the clear   space is the mark's own MARGIN, never the box's `column-gap`** (a track is charged its gap even   when nothing is in it, so an empty slot shifted the words 26px). The gate is   `e2e/catalog/mark-height.spec.ts`, which measures every mark-capable lower third against a   SQUARE and a PORTRAIT mark - they fail differently - and holds the lt49/lt53 exceptions from   both sides, so a stale entry fails rather than excusing a design it no longer describes.
