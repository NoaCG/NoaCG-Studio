Preserve lifecycle `calls`, ambient `loop` motion, and measured `dynamic` builders when converting legacy animation. Use `convertToDataRegion(template, refine?)` to author steps the legacy region cannot represent, and derive lifecycle step counts from the resulting data rather than hard-coding them.
