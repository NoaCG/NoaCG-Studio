Give a kit ONE Style family and never re-resolve it into another. Its starter is about ten graphics from its own library that satisfy the core six in that family, gated by `validatePacks`. Impose a kit palette only on designs whose family the palette is drawn for, and never give a kit a light-paper palette, because a card that sets its type over the picture loses it.
