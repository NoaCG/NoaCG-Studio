Distinguish engineering verification, readiness for workflow review and actual owner acceptance. Before requesting owner testing, prove a usable end-to-end task in the real UI, including ordinary controls needed to finish it; report missing essentials as unfinished workflow, and let agents catch routine defects. Keep technical slices small, but group their review around useful outcomes rather than asking the owner to test every partial control.
