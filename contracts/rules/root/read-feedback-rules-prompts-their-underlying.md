Read feedback, rules and prompts for their underlying intent and the outcome the user wants, not their literal wording. Explain any departure from a rule's wording in the pull request; invariants stay binding. Choose the simplest effective solution and challenge unnecessary complexity. Keep interfaces self-explanatory with minimal visible copy, and never turn an explanation given to you into UI text.
