End a completed or waiting turn with a tiny wrap-up somebody can read on a phone: what changed and whether it is verified, the exact action needed or "nothing", and the next step. Do the obvious safe continuation rather than asking permission for it. Put a question last on screen and add no prose after it, and run no extra scans just to produce the wrap-up.
