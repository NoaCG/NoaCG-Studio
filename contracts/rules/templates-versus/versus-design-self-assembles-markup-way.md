A versus design SELF-ASSEMBLES its markup, the way a scoreboard does, rather than taking the info-card contract. The presets in `vsPresets.ts` (`vs-slam`, `vs-glide`) animate edges-meet slides and a VS pop with real keyframes only, and write their data through `convertToDataRegion`.
