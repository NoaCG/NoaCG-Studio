A numbered sibling family becomes ONE PART PER SIBLING, labelled by the word that sibling carries - the quiz's option rows and the alert category's severity blocks are recognised the same way. One shared class matching four elements has nowhere to put four different values, and the level machine gives each block its own opacity track.
