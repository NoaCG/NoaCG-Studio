`taxonomy.ts` is the DISCOVERY facet registry and is PURE DATA - programme families and formats, the graphic categories and their groups, structures, field semantics, capabilities, placements, the per-preset motion table, style-family labels and the search aliases. Derivation lives in `src/templates/templateMeta.ts` and the browse engine in `src/templates/search.ts`. Each programme format carries the VERBATIM workbook `sheetName` that `packs.ts` uses.
