A look carries SHAPE as well as colour - radius, blur, edge, lift, accent weight and glow, the kicker face and both trackings, the heading weight - as additive-optional brand tokens. `setIf` writes only a token the RECEIVING design already declares, so a look can never graft a variable onto a design that consumes none. The numeric-font variable is deliberately NOT captured, because it is DERIVED from the typeface in use. A carried label face goes through `ensureFontFace`.
