Each non-tabular face also declares `numericFallbackId` - the bundled sibling its numbers are set in instead, sharing a style family so the voice survives. The pairings are made BY HAND: registry order gives a scoreboard the wrong width, and a fallback stack gives a condensed display face a substitute that only exists at one weight. Mono is the last resort, for an imported face with no pairing.
