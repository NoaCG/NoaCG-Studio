`OCCASIONS` is the MOMENT a graphic is for, a closed vocabulary on the show's OWN CLOCK, and each value declares the PHRASES a confused person types. Those phrases are folded into `ALIASES` automatically, so the declaration IS the search behaviour and a phrase cannot fail to reach its occasion. The axis is a clock, so a value that is not a MOMENT - a form, a show type, a look - has nowhere to sit, and `src/templates/templateMeta.ts` gates admitting one. Which designs carry which moment is declared in `src/templates/meta.ts`, not here.
