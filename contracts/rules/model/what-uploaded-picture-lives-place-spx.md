What an uploaded picture is FOR lives in ONE place, so the SPX card, the video card and both prompt builders cannot drift: `asset` (use it as it is - the only purpose that BUNDLES, plus the fixed or swappable `AssetBinding`), `layout` (make one like this), `mood` (take the look and feel) and `plate` (make it work over this). The labels and hints live here too, because they are the words the user reads on every surface.
