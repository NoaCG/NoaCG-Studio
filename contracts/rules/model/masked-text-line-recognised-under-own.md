A masked text line is recognised under ITS OWN graphic's prefix - the host's, or an INSERTED graphic's namespaced one (a `data-gfx` root carrying its own `-box`, from `blocks/templateInsert.ts`). Such parts carry `inserted: true`, which is how they can be full registry parts - selectable, animatable, named by their field titles - while `countLines`, the count the preset emitters size choreography from, keeps counting the host's lines only.
