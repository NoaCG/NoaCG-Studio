Scope 'all' is the whole graphic adopting the donor's full choreography plus the chosen or donor step duration and ease, SKIPPING press-revealed layers whose entrance belongs to their press; it also swaps the target phase's step `calls` and `dynamics` for the donor's, both scaled to the settled duration. Per-layer applies never touch `calls` or `dynamics` - those are step-level, not layer motion.
