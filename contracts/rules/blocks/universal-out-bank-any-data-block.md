`motionPresets.ts` is the UNIVERSAL in and out bank for ANY data-block graphic - catalog, wizard import, agent-door scaffold, hand-made - authored directly as DATA with two keyframes per property, no emitter and no importer, and applied by the pure `applyMotionPreset`. It is NOT a category bank: `presetRegistry`'s banks are the catalog's tuned choreographies and need the structure contract they were written for, while a universal motion moves THE GRAPHIC AS ONE UNIT, which is what lets it promise to work everywhere.
