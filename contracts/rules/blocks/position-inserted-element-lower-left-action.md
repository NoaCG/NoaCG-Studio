Position an inserted element with `positionForNewElement` in the lower-left action-safe area, tag it `data-gfx` and style it with `textCssRule`. A block groups under a hierarchical `path` and names the `primaryTab` to reveal after applying. Animation blocks come in two tracks: CSS (`@keyframes` plus a class on the element) and GSAP (a tween injected into `play()`/`stop()`).
