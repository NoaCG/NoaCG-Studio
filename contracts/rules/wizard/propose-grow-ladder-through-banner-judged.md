Propose the grow ladder through `proposeBannerGrowth` for a banner judged by itself: a rectangle with room before the margin whose STACKED bound lines sit ON it (`lineSitsIn`), whatever their anchor. A pair sharing one baseline argues neither way; no stacked line, a plate of figures, a centred line on a bar touching a scoreline, a full-frame backplate or a quiz behaviour keep shrink and the step asks. Never judge the whole graphic.
