Default a banner to the whole grow ladder through `proposeBannerGrowth` where the artwork is unambiguous: a rectangle with room before the margin whose STACKED bound lines sit ON it (`lineSitsIn`) - start-anchored lines on any banner, centred or right-set ones only on a strip. A pair sharing one baseline argues neither way; no stacked line, a plate of figures, two figures on the graphic, a full-frame backplate or a quiz behaviour keep shrink and the step asks.
