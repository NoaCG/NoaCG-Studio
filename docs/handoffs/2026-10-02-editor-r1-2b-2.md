# Continue after R1.2b.2: typography and fit

Branch `claude/editor-r1-2b-2-typography`, begun at fetched `origin/main` `7d83ced0`, which is the
anchor correction to R1.2b.1 (PR #609, deployed). The spec, reproduction, decisions and verification
live in [the R1.2b.2 receipt](../research/editor-r1-2b-2/README.md). Start from updated `origin/main`
in a new worktree.

What landed before this branch:

- **The anchor moves only the pivot** (owner answer, 2026-10-01): typing, Center anchor and the
  Anchor tool write `--base-anchor-x/y` and `transform-origin` alone; the Anchor tool maps the drag
  through the parent so the crosshair stays under the pointer. The
  [R1.2b.1 receipt](../research/editor-r1-2b-1/README.md) records the answer, the changed tests and
  the decision that Center follows the same rule.

What R1.2b.2 settled:

- **Owner decisions, 2026-10-02**: the everyday type set (weight from the font's own range,
  alignment, line and letter spacing, Long text: Shrink to fit, Wrap, Run on, with its width) on
  created and imported text; and on a text box the side handles resize while the corners scale.
- **Where it applies**: placed lines take all of it; catalog lines take weight and spacing, keeping
  alignment and fit with their design; SVG text takes weight and letter spacing, keeping alignment
  and fit with its import's fit ladder. `typeReasons` in `artworkEdits.ts` says which and why.
- **Canvas resizing** is for boxes with their own width and height (`resizableTextBox`); an imported
  slot with only a maximum width keeps its Width typed and its handles scaling.
- **The preview refits placed text** after a stylesheet swap, as the graphic's `update()` does.

Carry separately (recorded as limits in the receipt):

- An imported SVG's fit ladder does not refit after a style change in the editor preview.
- A value too long at the fit's 55% floor runs past its slot, by the fit's design.
- Italic, all caps, shadow, outline and typing on the canvas (an R1.2b follow-up in the artwork
  brief) are not built.
- Owner looks for R1.2a.4, R1.2a.5, R1.2a.6, R1.2b.1 (answered on the anchor; the handles still
  wait for his read) and now R1.2b.2 are desktop items in `docs/acceptance/owner-queue/`.
