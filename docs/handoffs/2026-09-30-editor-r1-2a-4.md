What R1.2a.4 settled: a flag is the boundary between two cues on one ruler. Add Step at the playhead
splits the cue there, Delete joins two cues, and a Step flag drag is a join then a split, so every
key and bar keeps its absolute time; each is one undo and refuses atomically with its reason where
the runtime could not play the result exactly. The shared cut is `holdAt`, `cutTracks` and
`cutBars` in `src/blocks/animEdit.ts`, which Set Out now uses too; `splitCue`, `joinCues` (with
`unsplitAt`) and `moveStepFlag` sit beside it, and `applyStep` (`src/blocks/editorSteps.ts`) is the
registry's step operation. Add Step then Delete is byte-identical, with two documented
normalizations that play the same. G02 editing on a flag, the flags as controls
(`src/components/editorFoundation/StepFlag.tsx`) and the owner's 2026-09-30 one-key Out hold
(`noacgBuildExit`'s `early` branch; the R1.2a.3 body upgrades by hash) are in.

Next bounded phase, R1.2a.5, under the same owner contract:

- Cross-cue key moves: dragging a key or a bar body across a flag changes its cue, with the same
  repartition (`joinCues`/`splitCue` show how a key and a bar move between cues exactly).
- Redefine Set Out crossing from a Next cue, which lifts the R1.2a.2 guard in `moveOutBoundary`
  (`src/blocks/editorOut.ts`). With Out from an earlier step interrupted (R1.2a.3), motion moved
  into Out no longer plays from an earlier step; what remains is the authored exit at the last step.
- Owner decision 2026-09-30, answering whether Out keys move with the flag: the exit belongs to
  the Out flag. It keeps its own timing relative to the flag and starts the moment Out is
  pressed, whichever way the flag moves; no motion is ever cut. Out later moves the exit with the
  flag and the graphic holds longer (replacing `CROSSES_OUT_KEY`); Out earlier into still air
  moves it with the flag with no pause; Out earlier into unfinished motion carries that motion
  into Out, where it plays first and the exit starts the moment it ends. Recorded in
  `docs/EDITOR_REBUILD_PLAN.md` (D01, D02) and the register; built in R1.2a.5
  ([spec](../research/editor-r1-2a-5/README.md)).
