# Continue after R1.2a.4: cross-cue key moves

Worktree `fervent-banach-cc3a1d`, branch `claude/editor-r1-2a-4-steps`, began at fetched `origin/main`
`26488f9d0` (contains R1.2a.3, PR #564, live as `d0642028`). The spec, reproduction, verification
and limits live in [the R1.2a.4 receipt](../research/editor-r1-2a-4/README.md). Confirm the branch's
landing and live `/version.json` before starting from updated `origin/main` in a new worktree.

What R1.2a.4 settled: a flag is the boundary between two cues on one ruler. Add Step at the playhead
splits the cue there, Delete joins two cues, and a Step flag drag is a join then a split, so every
key and bar keeps its absolute time; each is one undo and refuses atomically with its reason where
the runtime could not play the result exactly. The shared cut is `holdAt`, `cutTracks` and
`cutBars` in `src/blocks/animEdit.ts`, which Set Out now uses too; `splitCue`, `joinCues` (with
`unsplitAt`) and `moveStepFlag` sit beside it, and `applyStep` (`src/blocks/editorSteps.ts`) is the
registry's step operation. Add Step then Delete is byte-identical, with two documented
normalizations that play the same. G02 editing on a flag, the flags as controls
(`src/components/editorFoundation/StepFlag.tsx`) and the owner's 2026-09-30 one-key Out hold
(`noacgBuildExit`'s `early` branch; the R1.2a.3 body upgrades by hash) are in.

Next bounded phase, R1.2a.5, under the same owner contract:

- Cross-cue key moves: dragging a key or a bar body across a flag changes its cue, with the same
  repartition (`joinCues`/`splitCue` show how a key and a bar move between cues exactly).
- Redefine Set Out crossing from a Next cue, which lifts the R1.2a.2 guard in `moveOutBoundary`
  (`src/blocks/editorOut.ts`). With Out from an earlier step interrupted (R1.2a.3), motion moved
  into Out no longer plays from an earlier step; what remains is the authored exit at the last step.
- Out moving later refuses while the exit's first keys sit at time 0 (`CROSSES_OUT_KEY`, R1.2a.1):
  Out keeps its keys at their absolute times. On most graphics with an authored exit that means
  the Out flag can only move earlier, into settled air. Consider whether Out keys should move with
  the flag when it moves later; that changes R1.2a.1's rule and needs the owner.

Carry separately, unchanged by R1.2a.4:

- A Step cannot go inside a clip-path reveal: string tracks have no exact split, and the wizard's
  default lower third and the info cards reveal their box by `clipPath`, so Add Step refuses
  during their entrances (it works in the settled air after them and inside the info cards'
  Steps). An exact split of a `clipPath` inset would lift this.
- Refusals name a layer and property by code (`.info-card-box clipPath`), as Set Out's always
  have. The operations in `animEdit.ts` see no layer labels; the editor could map the selector to
  its label before showing the reason.
- The owner look is filed as
  [a desktop item](../acceptance/owner-queue/2026-09-30-editor-step-authoring.md); its answer may
  reshape R1.2a.5 (whether a moved flag should carry the Step's reveal with it).

  value at the old flag (a cut never removes a track, which is what keeps Add Step then Delete

  a join or drag needs it (card26-29 are the catalog's only machine-less stepped designs). A layer
