# Continue after R1.2a.5: cross-cue moves and the Out flag

Worktree `editor-r1-2a-5-cross-cue-196874`, branch `claude/editor-r1-2a-5-cross-cue-196874`, began
at fetched `origin/main` `cfaa6019f` (contains R1.2a.4, PR #572, `ea2d63f9`, and the Steps-as-buttons
backlog item, `3de997ca`). The spec, reproduction, verification and limits live in
[the R1.2a.5 receipt](../research/editor-r1-2a-5/README.md). Start from updated `origin/main` in a
new worktree.

What R1.2a.5 settled, under the owner's 2026-09-30 decision that the exit belongs to the Out flag:

- **The Out flag.** Set Out, the Out flag drag and its nudge move Out; the exit keeps its own
  timing from the press. Later, the graphic holds longer. Earlier into still air, the exit moves
  with the flag. Earlier into unfinished motion (the entrance or the last Step's cue), that motion
  is cut as Add Step cuts and moved into Out, stored as `carried` on the Out step; it plays first
  and the exit starts as it ends. Carried motion rejoins its cue before any later move, so any two
  moves equal one. `moveOutBoundary` and `rejoinCarried` are in `src/blocks/editorOut.ts`; the
  R1.2a.2 Next-cue guard and `CROSSES_OUT_KEY` are gone.
- **Out from an earlier step** skips `carried` (`noacgBuildExit`'s `early`), so the exit starts at
  the press. The R1.2a.4 interpreter body is frozen by hash
  (`ANIM_INTERPRETER_BEFORE_CARRIED_HASH`) and upgrades once on write.
- **Keys across flags.** `moveKeys` and `moveTrack` (`src/blocks/animEdit.ts`) treat a track as
  one curve on the ruler, cut by the flags: a key a cut wrote is the flag's, rejoined where the
  move reaches it and cut anew after, so a drag and its reverse give the source. Keys keep their
  order, stay between the start of In and the end of Out, and on a flag stay on the side they came
  from. The registry's `key.move` (`applyKeyMove` in `src/blocks/editorAnimation.ts`) is one undo;
  the timeline drags, nudges by frame and shows the refusal live while held.
- **Bars across flags.** `moveLayerSpan` moves the whole run of bars a layer's visibility
  continues through (`visibleRun`, `moveLayerAcross`). A cue without bars is spelled out as the
  cue before left the layer, in the operations and on the timeline.

Carry separately:

- The limits in the receipt: Out set exactly at a bar's end then moved later, a layer blinking
  during carried motion into an empty exit, an exit that starts by holding the carried motion's
  last value, byte identity with differing default eases or emptied layers, a Linear key authored
  on a flag between collinear Linear neighbours, and the editor's Out playhead and the OGraf stop
  duration from an earlier step (both overstate by `carried`).
- A Step cue a frame or two long, which Set Out inside a Step now makes easy, lets its flag label
  and the Out flag's overlap on the ruler (the flag labels have no room rule).
- Catalog rows animate `yPercent`, which this editor does not author yet, so their bar bodies
  refuse to move ("Another source channel owns this transform"); their keys move, and a drawn
  layer's bars move freely.
- The owner looks for R1.2a.4 and R1.2a.5 are desktop items in `docs/acceptance/owner-queue/`.
  Their answers may reshape how a moved flag treats a Step's reveal.
- Steps as control buttons ([backlog](../backlog/steps-as-control-buttons.md)) stays possible: a
  Step is still a cue with a stable name, and no operation renames one.
- Unchanged from R1.2a.4: a Step cannot go inside a clip-path reveal; refusals name layers by
  selector; legacy `hides` refuse wherever a move would shift them; machine graphics refuse all
  animation authoring here; the `e2e/editor-keys.spec.ts` memory-pressure flake
  ([finding](../backlog/editor-keys-canvas-drags-fail-under-memory-pressure.md)).
