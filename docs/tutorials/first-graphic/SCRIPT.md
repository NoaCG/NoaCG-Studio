# Script: your first graphic

**Everything under a beat heading is spoken, word for word. Nothing else in this file is.**

Twelve beats, about 560 words, roughly four minutes at a calm pace. One voice throughout. The
frame named under each heading is what is on screen while those words are spoken;
`INSTRUCTIONS.md` says what happens inside it.

Two words to get right if the voice is synthetic: HJK is said as three letters, "hoo-yoo-koo" in
Finnish or "aitch jay kay" in English, and Ilves rhymes with "hill-ves".

---

## Beat 1 - three doors
`step-1-import-door.png`

This is NoaCG Studio. You have a graphic you drew yourself, and in about four minutes it will be
on air with somebody typing into it.

Open the studio and it asks how you want to start. The door we want is Import graphic. Your own
artwork, no AI.

## Beat 2 - the drop zone
`step-2-drop-zone.png`

Here is where the file goes. Before you use it, read the line above it: need help exporting SVG.

Open it once, because these three rules decide whether the import works at all. Name your layers,
because those names become the labels your operator reads. Keep your text as text, and do not
convert it to outlines. One artboard, at the size you want on air.

Get those right in your design app and everything after this is easy.

## Beat 3 - what it found
`step-3-what-it-found.png`

Drop the file in. This one is a football scoreboard from the NoaCG practice library, and you can
download the same file and follow along.

NoaCG reads it straight back to you. The artwork is 1920 by 1080, and it found seven text layers.
Check that number against what you drew. If it is lower than you expected, some of your type was
turned into outlines on the way out of your design app, and it is worth exporting again.

It also says the walk just got shorter. Five steps now, not six. Your text is already exactly
where you put it, so there is nothing to erase and nothing to place.

## Beat 4 - fields
`step-4-fields.png`

This is the step that turns a drawing into something an operator can drive.

Every text layer you drew is a row here, and every row is already on. The layer name is what your
operator will read, so this is the moment to fix anything you called Rectangle 3. The words under
it are what the field starts as.

Now look at what NoaCG worked out on its own. The two scores are plain figures, so they came in as
numbers. The clock offers to be a countdown. The crest is a picture your operator can swap.

Anything you meant as part of the drawing, untick, and it goes back to being artwork.

## Beat 5 - animation
`step-5-animation.png`

Animation is how the graphic comes on and how it goes off. Something sensible is already chosen,
so if you have no opinion yet, keep walking.

## Beat 6 - finish
`step-6-finish.png`

Finish has two name boxes and they are not the same box.

The first one names the graphic. Call it Match scorebug. The second one names a new production,
which is the show you are going to run it in. Call it Saturday Match. Fill both. Leave them empty
and they take the same name, which is confusing a week later.

Then press add to the production.

## Beat 7 - the dashboard
`step-7-production.png`

This is the playout dashboard, and it is where the show is run.

Preview on the left is what you are checking. Program on the right is what your audience sees, and
it is empty, because nothing is on air yet. Your graphic is already waiting in the rundown as a
cue.

## Beat 8 - take
`step-8-on-air.png`

Press Take.

That is your drawing, on air.

## Beat 9 - the scores act at once
`step-9-score-bumped.png`

Now the part a scoreboard exists for. The plus and minus buttons act on air the moment you press
them. Nothing to confirm, no second press.

HJK score twice. And that away goal was offside, so take it back.

## Beat 10 - typing waits
`step-10-typed-not-on-air.png`

Typing is different, and this is the one thing to take away from the whole video.

Type a new team name into the cue. Nothing happens on air. Read the line under the fields: one
change not on air yet. Your program is still showing the old name, and it will keep showing it.

## Beat 11 - update
`step-11-updated.png`

Press Update, and the typed change goes live.

So: numbers act on their own press, typed values wait for Update. An operator who does not know
that airs the previous name and blames the graphic.

## Beat 12 - out
`step-12-off-air.png`

Out plays the graphic off. Nothing on air.

That is the whole road. Your own drawing, five steps, and four buttons. What you draw next is up
to you, and the practice library has a file for every kind of graphic, made to be taken apart.
