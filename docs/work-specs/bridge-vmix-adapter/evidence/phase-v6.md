# V6 rules for timed vMix clip playback

V5 played or held a selected vMix file and reported its clock. V6 lets the operator trim it, control how it ends, and set its fades and level. This phase covers one already-supported media cue on a vMix player. Folder playback, Play next and sequence execution belong to V7.

A trimmed Take, faded Take, gain setting, live change to Clear and faded Out each returned `unsupported` and sent zero new vMix functions. The adapter advertised only `state` for the target. ## Rules

1. A trimmed Take first confirms the list is stopped before selecting the appended occurrence. Native Loop is off for a Bridge-managed boundary. After `SelectIndex`, XML must show that exact occurrence before a seek, and a fresh read must confirm the seek before `Play`. Success requires a `Running` reading of the new occurrence at or after the in point. A delayed, ignored or wrong selection or seek cannot cause the Bridge to play an unconfirmed occurrence. Prefix cleanup remains after observed playback and keeps the latest confirmed occurrence if cleanup partly fails. In Program, every sampled new frame is at or after the in point. If paused selection exposes earlier frames, a black cover starts before selection and remains until the seek is confirmed.
2. The clock reports the selected occurrence's measured effective segment in seconds. Missing or malformed timing cannot attest a position or cause a timed command. For a trimmed clip, Hold pauses at its out point, Loop seeks to its in point, and Clear removes the list at its out point. An untrimmed Hold holds the native last frame and native Loop stays under vMix control. An untrimmed Clear removes the list when the owned clip reaches `Completed`; if it has a fade, the Bridge plans it to finish at the end before removing the list. Paused time does not count toward an automatic clip boundary.
3. A faded Take starts from alpha zero and volume zero, then advances alpha once per frame toward 255 and sends one `SetVolumeFade` to the cue level for the fade duration. Out with a fade and a timed Clear with a fade reach alpha and volume zero at the end of their fade, before removal. A Clear armed inside the fade window uses the remaining time. Use the current playback frame convention. A later Take restores its own alpha and level, including after cancellation or partial completion of an earlier fade.
4. A Take applies its own gain, defaulting to unity. At or below unity its fader endpoint is `100 × gain^(1/4)` and it sends `SetGain 0`. Above unity the fader endpoint is 100 and it sends `SetGain 20 × log10(gain)`. For a fade-in, the endpoint is sent by `SetVolumeFade` from a fader of zero. No stale gain survives on the next confirmed Take.
5. A live ending change acts on the held occurrence without playing it again. A trimmed ending replaces the Bridge's pending Hold, Loop or Clear, while an untrimmed ending uses native Loop and arms or disarms Clear. Pause suspends boundary work and Resume schedules it from a new observation. Successful Out or Clear retires the occurrence. A late action aimed at an expired instance cannot affect its replacement, even for the same file.
6. An automatic step is bound to the Bridge session's exact port, player, generation and held occurrence. It uses the same serialized mixer turn and scope checks as player actions. It checks its generation after waiting for the turn and rechecks current XML under the turn before sending; the exact occurrence, setup and timing must still authorize the step. A conclusive native takeover retires the ownership, including a same-path occurrence changed by an outside writer if observable under the V5 rules. The other player and another port keep their own clocks. An unavailable read grants no send. A refused or unknown write grants no follow-up, and the Bridge never resends that write by itself. Closing the adapter removes pending work.
7. The vMix `/status` target capabilities advertise `end`, `fade`, `trim` and `level`. The cue editor and request path use those capabilities to expose Clear, fades, trim and level and pass the saved values to the existing vMix player verbs. Missing capabilities keep the older Bridge path usable. CasparCG and vMix overlays keep their existing paths.
