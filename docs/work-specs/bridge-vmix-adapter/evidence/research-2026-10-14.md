# Research, 2026-10-14: clips as vMix inputs

The planning session for the re-scoped spec. vMix 29.0.0.49 (Trial) on the development laptop,
driven only over the TCP API on 127.0.0.1:8099 by a small probe client (one command at a time,
`SUBSCRIBE ACTS` on, every state read from `XML`). Media made with ffmpeg in the session's scratch
folder: `clipA_red.mp4` (4 s, 1280x720, red, a 440 Hz tone), `clip B blue.mp4` (3 s, blue, a
space in the name), `still_green.png`, `sting.wav` (3 s). Pictures are vMix's own
`SnapshotInput` of the list input (1920x1080), read as the mean colour of the frame.

## The second probe, the same evening: trim, fades and level

After the owner asked for the CasparCG clip controls in vMix too. vMix started fresh, a new list
with Auto Play, Pause, Restart, Play Next and Loop off, and `counter10.mp4` (10 s, a test pattern
with a counter, a 440 Hz tone). Program pictures are vMix's `Snapshot`, read as the mean colour.

| # | Done | vMix answered |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | `SetPosition 2000`, `MarkIn`, `SetPosition 5000`, `MarkOut` on the list, then `SetPosition 0` and `Play` | `FUNCTION OK` for both marks, no mark in the XML, and the clip played from 0 through 5 s and on: marks do nothing on a Video List. `MarkReset` changed nothing visible either |
| 18 | The list on Program, `SetAlpha 128`, then `SetAlpha 0` | the Program picture's mean went from (119,129,133) to (64,62,67), then to black (2,0,2) with alpha near 0; the alpha is not in the XML |
| 19 | 26 `SetAlpha` calls in a row, 0 to 255 | 21 ms for all 26, so one call per frame is cheap |
| 20 | `SetVolume` 100, 50, 25, 10 while playing | the XML `volume` read 100, 6.25, 0.39 and 0.01, and the meter fell in the same ratio (0.125, 0.0078, 0.00049): the gain is (fader / 100) to the fourth power, so a level of L dB at or below 0 is fader 100 x 10^(L/80) |
| 21 | `SetVolumeFade Value=0,1000` | `volume` 0.0027 after 0.3 s and 0 after 1.3 s: vMix fades the sound itself |

So trim is the Bridge's job (start at the in point with `SetPosition`, act at the out point), a
picture fade is the Bridge stepping `SetAlpha` once per frame, a sound fade is `SetVolumeFade`, and
a level is `SetVolume` by the measured curve. vMix's `SetGain` (0 to 24 dB) is documented for a
level above 0 dB and not measured. Left as before: the same backup and restore, SHA-256 as before.

## What it settles for the plan

- A Video List is a stable vMix input whose file NoaCG can change: the clips player. Take is
  `SelectIndex` of an item and `Play`; Out is `ListRemoveAll`, which leaves black.
- Hold, Loop and the next file are vMix's own: `Completed` on the last frame (rows 6, 7, 5).
- With Auto Play, Pause and Restart off, the player plays what NoaCG took whichever way the vMix
  operator cuts (row 12), the way a CasparCG channel plays whatever the vision mixer shows.
- An audio-only player needs `AudioAutoOff` and `AudioOn` (rows 15 and 16).
- A read right after `SelectIndex` can still show the old item (row 8), so a read-back waits.
- `ListAdd` refuses a missing file with `ER` (row 3), which names the file to the operator.

## Not measured

The Video List's own Loop with Auto Next on (the whole list), `SetGain`, how exactly the Bridge
can stop at an out point, the first-frame delay of a large file, UNC paths, the operator's own
preset saved with the players and opened again, and any edition but the Trial.

forced close (SHA-256 `4af6e979…` and `9396e6e6…`, as before; `last.vmix` keeps its 2026-10-01
time). The background service was untouched; vMix's NDI helper exited with it. The media and the
probe client stayed in the session's scratch folder.
