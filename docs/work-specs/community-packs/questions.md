# Community packs: questions for the owner

Behaviour choices in [`spec.md`](spec.md) that change what a maker, a visitor or an admin sees or
can do. Most important first. Each has a recommendation the spec already follows, so an answer of
"yes" changes nothing and any other answer names the decision to revise (in brackets).

1. **Where does a maker submit a pack?** Recommendation: a quiet **Submit a pack** button on the
   Community packs shelf in the wizard, with the maker's own submissions (status, Withdraw,
   Update) under **Your packs** on the same shelf. The alternative is "Submit to Community
   packs..." in a production's menu on Home > Productions. Reason: packs are got and given in one
   place, and Home stays the maker's library with no community UI. [D8, AC-6]

2. **What can be submitted?** Recommendation: a whole production the maker owns, one graphic is
   fine, with at least one prepared cue per graphic; no single-graphic submit from the Graphics
   library, no team productions, no production installed from the shelf. Reason: one format and
   one installer, and every pack arrives with a ready first cue. [D7, AC-6, AC-7]

3. **Where does the NoaCG admin review?** Recommendation: on the same shelf. An admin sees
   "Waiting for review" with Install to try, Approve and Not accepted (reason required), and Take
   down on live cards. The alternative is the existing Moderation dialog in the account menu.
   Reason: the admin judges a pack where users will meet it and tries it with the same Install.
   [AC-8, AC-9]

4. **What happens to installed copies when a maker updates a pack?** Recommendation: nothing. An
   approved update replaces the shelf card; whoever wants it installs it as a new production.
   Reason: an update that arrives mid-show could change what is on air. [D11, AC-11]

5. **Who sees approved community packs?** Recommendation: everyone who opens the shelf, signed in
   or not, as with the NoaCG seeds. Reason: installing needs no account today, and an approved
   pack is CC BY 4.0 anyway. [D3, AC-8]

6. **Can a maker withdraw at once?** Recommendation: yes. Withdraw takes the pack off the shelf
   (or out of the review queue) immediately with no review, and installs already made stay.
   Reason: the offer is the maker's, and the CC BY grant to people who installed it cannot be
   revoked anyway. [D6, AC-10]

7. **How does a maker hear the decision?** Recommendation: only through the status under Your
   packs (In review, Live, Not accepted with the reason, Taken down with the reason); no email in
   this version. Reason: there is no mail path for it, and a maker checks the shelf they submitted
   from. [D14]

8. **Whose name is on the card?** Recommendation: the account's display name, shown on the sheet
   before sending; no separate maker-name field. Reason: the CC BY attribution should match the
   account that granted the licence. [D15]
