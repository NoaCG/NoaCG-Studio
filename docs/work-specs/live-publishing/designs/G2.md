# G2: The honest light and held Takes

Design gate for phase 5 (W3, E1, O2, O3, O7, S4, D1, D4, AC-3) of `../spec.md`. It covers every
point the spec lists for G2: per-output, per-graphic reports of received and ran commands, pending
and failed states; never banking past held work; deduplication; reconnect, restart, cancellation
(Out, All out, newer Take, deletion) and queue limits; replaying held history out of sight before
showing its final state, keeping data, events and Next steps; a demanded graphic loading ahead of
background preparation and its 15 s spacing; the three reported versions (stored, prepared, on
air); and unknown never green. Written against `main` at c90317d8. Nothing here is built yet; the
build is the next pull request.

## Terms

- **Output.** One `/output` page (`src/output/main.ts`), named by its instance id
  (`liveInstanceId`, the `id` of its Presence entry). A CasparCG layer is one too.
- **Command.** A log row whose `t` is `update`, `play`, `stop`, `next`, `event` or `snap` (the
  renderer commands, `livePath.ts:267`). `cue`, `staged`, `live` and `ping` are status rows.
- **Lifecycle command.** `play`, `snap` or `stop`. A **Take** is a `play` or `snap`; an **Out** is
  a `stop` (All out writes one `stop` per graphic the head says is on).
- **Position.** A row's `seq` on the numbered log (protocol 2, migration 0071). Each output keeps
  the highest position it has read (`lastAppliedSeq`, `main.ts:948`). An output that follows the
  old log by row id (protocol 1: a production still holding rows from before 0071, which the
  prune removes within 14 days) has no contiguous position and reports no run (section 6 words
  it).
- **Hosted.** The output's stage has a current frame for the graphic (`stage.graphics`, or a
  published package's layer). **Released**: that frame's document has loaded, or was released on
  a fallback face (`stage.ts` `release`); only then does a command reach the document.
- **Ran.** A command was posted into its graphic's released document (`postPreviewCmd`), or sent
  as a call to a package's Graphic. A command the stage still keeps (a frame's queue before it
  releases, W1's place-hint wait in `placeOnOutput.ts`, capped at 2 s, or a package waiting for
  its base) has not run yet. This is the most an output can see. No browser can prove the
  broadcast picture (E1); words below never claim one.
- **Settled.** A command is settled on an output when it ran, or was read past by a rule below
  (a superseded entrance, a cancelled hold, a deleted graphic, a command with nothing to post).
  A settled command is never run later.
- **Held.** A command the output has received and not settled, kept in order to run later
  (section 3). **Failed**: a command the output has given up on, with a reason (section 3).
- **Today.** The output claims a command before running it (`main.ts:816`), moves its cursor and
  banks it in its reports whatever happened (`main.ts:948,686`), drops a command for a graphic it
  does not host (`stage.ts:947`) while still reporting that graphic (`main.ts:1009`), and its
  stage only posts or queues (`stage.ts:420`); a frame added by a publish takes its queued
  commands with their entrances whenever it loads. The page says it cannot confirm a picture
  (`playoutStatus.ts:192-199`), reads "Connected" in green from the outputs' READY entries alone,
  and a change waiting for an on-air graphic reads amber (`readiness.ts` "Waiting for clear").

## 1. What an output reports (the run report)

Every output keeps, beside its cursor, an exact account of what it has not settled:

- `p`: its **cursor**, `lastAppliedSeq`. Every command at or below `p` is settled on this output,
  except those of the graphics listed in `h`. It never waits for a held graphic, so other
  graphics keep playing.
- `h`: per graphic with unsettled or failed commands, `[from, kind, why?]`: `from` is the
  position of its oldest unsettled command; `kind` is `loading` (held, its graphic is being
  loaded), `late` (held, its surviving Take reached this output more than 5 s ago, S4),
  `placing` (handed to the stage, waiting in the stage: W1's place-hint wait or a package's base,
  each capped) or `failed` (with `why`: `unpublished`, `load`, `script`, `limit` or `typed`). A
  graphic in `h` has settled everything of it before `from` and nothing from `from` on.
- `e`: the first 8 characters of the epoch it follows, so a page never compares positions across
  a republish.

So for graphic `g` on output `o`: **ran(o, g) = h[g] ? h[g].from - 1 : p**. Received is anything
at or below the cursor; pending is `loading`, `late` and `placing`; failed is `failed`.

**Each command handed to the stage gets exactly one outcome, by identity.** `main.ts` hands the
stage each command with its position (`stage.apply(graphic, msg, at)`), and the stage answers
once per command through `onOutcome(at, outcome)`: `posted` (it reached the document or the
package's Graphic: ran), `skipped` (nothing to post by an existing rule: an update with no data
and no look, a package command with no OGraf call; settled) or `failed` with a reason (a typed
action this build cannot read, `typed`; a package's waiting list past `WAITING_KEPT`, `limit`; no
frame, which section 3 makes unreachable, `load`). A command waiting in the placer, a boot frame's
queue or a package's waiting list has no outcome yet and is that graphic's `placing` entry. Every
way a stage lets go of such a command answers it: `stage.remove` and a replaced frame answer
`skipped` for each command still in the frame's queue, the placer or the package's waiting list
(today `stage.ts:1151-1158` drops them silently), and the frame's release answers `posted`. A
watchdog closes the class: a command with no outcome `FRAME_LOAD_MS` (20 s) after it was handed
over is answered `failed` with `load` and leaves the stage's queue. So no entry can stay `placing`
for good. The stage's own `state` asks
(`stage.ts:996`) are not commands and get no outcome. Nothing is counted: a dropped command can
never be mistaken for a later posted one.

**Two channels, no migration.**

1. **On every report row** the output writes, as `state.run = { o, p, h, e }`
   (`control_output_report_seq` stores `state` as sent, `0071_command_sequence.sql:516-555`).
   Report rows reach every production page through the log it already follows
   (`sessionWire.ts:279`). Today a graphic's report is a trailing debounce: each change resets an
   800 ms timer (`scheduleReport`, `main.ts:641-692`), so a graphic changing faster than that
   never reports until it stops. Now it fires 800 ms after the last change **or 2 s after the
   first unreported change, whichever is sooner**, so a data feed or clock cannot starve it. `run`
   is part of the byte-identical comparison, so each command of a graphic that may report
   (section 2) leads to a report of it within 2 s. A command settled with no report of its own (a
   graphic the output does not host, R4; a deleted graphic; a cancelled hold; a command of a
   failed graphic) schedules, within the same 2 s ceiling, a report of one hosted, released,
   settled graphic, the one whose bank is oldest: a re-bank at the cursor, which is a true
   baseline for it (the path `REBANK_AFTER` already takes). Only an output that hosts no such
   graphic leaves it to Presence.
2. **In the Presence entry**, as `ready.run` (same shape). The entry already goes through the
   page's Presence budget (one call per 10 s, `presenceGate.ts`), coalesced to the newest state;
   the output touches it whenever `p` or `h` moves (the gate coalesces that to one call per 10 s). It is the backstop: a report row that failed
   to write, a held or unhosted graphic that writes no report, an output with no command since it
   booted, all reach the page within 10 s, and it tells a page that this output speaks W3 at all.
   `h` in the entry lists at most 12 graphics; when more are unsettled, `p` in that entry is
   lowered to the smallest unlisted `from` minus 1, so the entry never claims more than is true.
   A report row always carries the full `h` (bounded by the graphics an output hosts or holds).

**Write volume.** Report rows per output: one per changing graphic per 800 ms of quiet, as
today, plus at most one per graphic per 2 s while it changes continuously (today none until the
change stops), plus one for a command that leaves data and state byte-identical (a repeated
update, an Out of a graphic already out), plus at most one re-bank per 2 s for commands settled
with no report of their own. The burst cap leaves `live` rows out
(`0071_command_sequence.sql:460-468`), so reports never refuse a Take; a report takes the head
lock for one row write, as today, never inside a press. The build measures press to sent with
two outputs and a feed writing to an on-air graphic every 200 ms against today's figure, with
the phase 2 harness (section 9).

**Readers.** `run` is one more member of `state`. Recovery reads only `groups`, `awards`,
`appearance`, `typed`, `steps`, `on`, `ograf` and `held` (`outputRecovery.ts:218`,
`reportedTyped`, `reportedLook`, `ografRestore`), and an older page's `machineState` keeps state
whole and reads `groups` (`ProductionPage.tsx:963`), so `run` changes no reader. The new page
strips `run` before noting machine state, so it causes no re-render of the event buttons. A
report with `run` and no machine state is no longer `null`; `reportedSnap` already reads
`groups` only, and `onWithoutPose` (`main.ts:1112`) reads `state.groups` the same way.

## 2. Reporting only the truth, and never banking past held work

- **Only a hosted, released graphic with nothing unsettled writes a report of itself.** Today an
  output reports every graphic a command reached, hosted or not (`main.ts:1009`); a graphic it
  does not host is reported with no machine state and banked, which can overwrite another output's
  good bank of it and, through the timed-cue wire (`cueArmWire.ts:314`), make pages believe it
  aired. Now a graphic in `h`, or not hosted, or hosted and not released, writes no report: no
  scheduled report, no forced re-bank (`REBANK_AFTER`, `main.ts:706`; the `typedRefused` rule,
  `main.ts:677-680`, generalised), and no report on a republish (`onEpoch`). Its bank stays where
  some output that ran it put it (that output's report banks it for every output, `main.ts:949`,
  which is right: it ran it). When no output did, it has no report: `planOutputRecovery`
  (`outputRecovery.ts:55`) then starts from the lowest bank of the graphics that have one, so a
  reboot reads that graphic's older rows by the restart rule in section 4. Banks are per graphic (`alreadyInSnapshot`), so the other
  graphics bank at the cursor as today. A graphic's report scheduled before it became held is
  dropped when it fires (asked at the moment of writing, as `typedRefused` is today).
- The run report of the other graphics carries `h`, so the page learns of held work within 2 s
  whenever anything else on that output reports, and within 10 s from Presence otherwise.
- **Deduplication.** `applied.claim` (`main.ts:816`) stays where it is: a command is claimed when
  it is received, and a held command owns its claim. The numbered follower hands each position
  once (`seqFollow.ts`), and a refill re-reading the window behind the cursor finds every row
  already claimed. A held queue is keyed by position, so a row can never be held twice. A reboot
  starts with no claims and replays from the banks, which this section keeps below every held
  command.

## 3. Held Takes (O7, S4, D1)

### When a hold starts

- A **Take** (`play` or `snap`) of a graphic that is not hosted and released starts a hold of
  that graphic. Not hosted means the stage has no frame for it (not in the version this output
  holds); hosted and not released means a frame a publish added is still loading
  (`swap.ts` `stage.add`), or a boot frame is still loading after the stage came back on air.
- From then until the hold ends, **every command for that graphic** (update, event, Next, snap,
  Out, another Take) joins its held queue in position order, and nothing of it reaches the stage.
  `applyCommand`'s bookkeeping (merged data, clocks, typed gate, steps, on-air sets) runs when a
  held command is applied, not when it is received, so it sees the commands in the same order as
  a graphic that never waited.
- A command that is not a Take, for a graphic that is not hosted and released and holds nothing
  (not hosted, or a frame a publish added that is still loading), is settled at once: its data and event values go into the graphic's merged data (as `applyCommand` does
  today before the stage), and nothing posts. When that graphic is later hosted, the first thing
  its frame receives is that merged data (the swapper's `data(graphic)` already hands a joining
  frame its held values; a loading frame gets them as its first command at release). So a data
  feed writing to a graphic no output hosts never holds anything and never turns the light amber.
  Only the boot puts commands in a frame's own queue.
- A command that is not a Take, for a graphic whose hold has **failed**, is settled the same way:
  its values are kept, nothing posts.
- A held queue is never a reason to wait for anything else: other graphics, the follower's cursor
  and the reports of other graphics carry on.

### Cancellation comes first

Before a hold loads anything, and again when its graphic releases, its queue is read for its
final lifecycle command. A queue whose last lifecycle command is an **Out** (an Out, or All out's
`stop`) is **cancelled**: it needs nothing loaded and nothing shown. A cancelled hold starts no
demand, never fails, and settles at once when its graphic is not hosted (its data stays as the
graphic's held values, R4) or at release when it is (case 1 below).

- **A newer Take** of the same graphic joins the queue; the earlier entrance is superseded. The
  5 s of case 2 is counted from the newest surviving Take's arrival.
- **A Take of another graphic on the same layer** is that graphic's own command; the page already
  sends the Outs of on-air layer peers with it (`graphicVerbs.ts:745`), and each lands in its own
  graphic's queue.
- **Deletion.** A published version that no longer has the graphic (the swapper's `remove`, a
  demand answer without it once the queue is cancelled, or a payload without it read during a
  boot's catch-up, which is history) drops its held queue and settles its commands as cancelled,
  with no failure. A held graphic counts as off air for the swapper's removal, so the removal is
  not kept waiting by a Take that never aired. It never appears afterwards.

### Loading the demanded graphic (ahead of background preparation)

- A live hold whose queue ends in a Take, of a graphic the stage does not host, starts a
  **demand**: the output reads the published payload at once (`controlOutputResolve`, retried as
  `untilAnswered` does), not waiting for a Presence prepare request and not waiting for the
  preparer's 15 s spacing (`prepare.ts:36`). At most one read is in flight; a hold that starts
  while one is in flight is answered by the next read that starts after the hold began (one
  queued read covers every hold since).
- When the answer holds the graphic, the output adds that graphic to the stage at once
  (`stage.add`, the version it came from passed for its package base and its sounds), records its
  digest as the one it holds (so the swapper never adds or builds it again), and hands the payload
  to the preparer as its next run's answer when no newer request is waiting, so the output reads
  the payload once, not twice. A preparation already running is not waited for and not
  cancelled: the demanded frame loads beside it, never behind the swapper's one-at-a-time builds.
- When the answer does not hold the graphic and the queue still ends in a Take, the hold **fails**
  with `unpublished`: a Take nothing can air (today it airs nothing and the light stays green).
  When the queue has been cancelled meanwhile, it is a deletion (above).
- A demanded frame that has not released `FRAME_LOAD_MS` (20 s) after it was added fails with
  `load`; one whose document reports a script error while loading fails with `script`. The frame
  is kept, and the graphic's next Take uses it.
- A graphic that is hosted and not released needs no demand: its frame is loading already.

### When the graphic releases

The held queue is applied in order through `applyCommand`, as a batch, with
`supersededAnimations` (`seqFollow.ts:333-358`): an entrance a later Take or Out of the same
graphic in the queue replaces is not animated; data, events, Next steps and exits all apply.

**Hidden** below means the frame's own hidden flag: the stage sends the document `offair` and
`sound-quiet` together (`composeDocument.ts:606-612`), so neither picture nor sound reaches air,
while its frame rate stays as on air. Clearing the flag restores each to the stage's own setting
(`setVisible`, `setSoundQuiet`), so a frame's settle never shows or unmutes a stage the boot or a
refill holds off air or quiet, and the boot's `setVisible(true)` never shows a frame in its own
settle. A package's layer is hidden with `setOffAir`, and its Take and Next steps are sent with
`skipAnimation: true` (a member of the `playAction` params `ografCallFor` builds,
`ografContract.ts:292`), OGraf v1's own way to land an action at once (NoaCG's exported graphics
settle and keep their sound quiet for it, `src/export/targets/ograf.ts:1918`).

1. **Cancelled.** Nothing of the queue reaches the document except its data: the frame receives
   one `update` with the graphic's merged data, and every command settles. A frame that was
   loading with nothing in its stage queue never receives a `play`, so the graphic is never seen.
   A boot frame, whose stage queue already holds the boot's history, is always released hidden
   (case 3) and ends off air.
2. **On time.** The newest surviving Take reached this output (its own clock, `Date.now()` when the
   row was received) at most 5 s before the release, and the frame holds no boot history: the
   queue is applied as live commands are, entrance included. AC-3's "first seen once loaded, with
   its entrance".
3. **Late.** Otherwise (a Take older than 5 s, or a boot frame's history in its queue) the frame is
   hidden first, the boot's queue runs, then the held queue; the frame is watched until it stands
   still (two answered state asks in a row with the same playhead, `catchUp.ts` `stoodStill`, at
   most `CATCH_UP_TIMING.capMs`, 6 s), then it shows its final state, without its entrance, as
   after a restart (S4). The cap always ends it: whatever goes wrong, the flag is cleared at the
   cap, as `airWhenSettled` does for the stage. Commands for the graphic that arrive during the
   settle are applied to the same hidden frame and are part of the final state.

### Limits and failures

- At most 200 commands are held per graphic and 1,000 per output (the stage's own `WAITING_KEPT`
  is 200). Before the per-graphic limit is reached, consecutive `update`s in the queue are merged
  into one (merged as `applyCommand` merges data, clock fields included, so the final values are
  the same) and superseded entrances are dropped. If the queue is still over the limit, the
  graphic fails with `limit` and its queue is dropped.
- A held command waits as long as the graphic is loading: `loading`, then `late` after 5 s, then
  `failed` with `load` at 20 s.
- A failed graphic's queue is dropped at the failure and nothing of it airs. Its `failed` entry
  stays in `h` until **any later Take or Out of that graphic settles on this output, All out (a
  `stop` of it), or its deletion** (above); a `typed` entry follows E1.1 instead (below). Each of these is in the operator's hands: a failed
  Take moved the head to "on" (`control_head_effect`), so All out includes it even after the
  graphic is gone from the rundown.

### Typed data and packages

- The typed gate (`typedOutputGate`) runs when a held command is applied, so a refused envelope is
  refused exactly as it is today and the graphic is held by E1.1. A graphic E1.1 holds is reported
  in `h` as `failed` with `typed` from its refused command for exactly as long as `typedRefused`
  holds it, and leaves `h` when a native update clears it, as E1.1 says; a Take or Out does not
  clear a `typed` entry (the gate still lets a `stop` through to it, `ografTyped.ts:650`, as today,
  and the output's READY entry keeps naming the refusal in red).
- A published package's commands already wait in order while its base is asked for
  (`waitingOgraf`, `stage.ts:596`): those are `placing` until posted. A package not yet mounted
  follows the same hold rules, with `setOffAir` and `skipAnimation` for its late case. Its answers
  stay the existing package outcome words (`ografOutcome.ts`).

## 4. Reconnect, restart and late frames

- **Reconnect.** Nothing held is lost: the queue lives in memory and the follower's refill hands
  only rows not yet read. A report row that failed to write while the network was down is not
  retried; the next report (any graphic) and the next Presence entry carry the current `p` and
  `h`, and a Presence rejoin re-sends the entry (`livePath.ts`, "A rejoin starts with no entry").
- **Restart.** The held queues are gone with the page, but no bank passed them (section 2), so
  the boot's catch-up replays them, hidden, as it replays everything (`main.ts` boot recovery).
  One gap is closed with it: a graphic the head says is on and that **no report banks** (a Take
  no output ran, then the output reloaded) is today read below `followFrom` only when it may air
  typed data (`main.ts:1068-1072`, `mayAirTyped`), and is otherwise played again with no data
  (`onWithoutPose`), so its template's defaults air. Now every such graphic, typed or not, reads
  its own rows below `followFrom` within the existing `unreportedPages` bound and replays them
  hidden, so it comes back with the values the operator sent; past the bound it takes its head
  cue's published values from the payload when that cue is published; a head cue that is not
  published leaves it as today (played with no data), and the debug line names it ("keep what
  their next Take sends", `main.ts:1071`, is reworded to say which).
  A catch-up Take of a graphic the boot payload does not hold is a deletion when that payload
  lacks it (above); a catch-up Take of a graphic whose frame has not released when the stage
  comes back on air is a late frame (next line). A restart shows what is on air.
- **Late boot frames.** Today a boot frame that releases after the stage came back on air (the
  6 s cap of `airWhenSettled`) runs its queued history on air, entrances and sounds included. Now
  such a frame is hidden with its own flag from the moment the stage comes back on air until it has
  released, run its queue and stood still (case 3), so a restart never plays history on air.
- **Republish** (a new epoch, `onEpoch`): held queues stay (they are commands owed to air); their
  `from` reads 0 in the new epoch until they settle, and `e` changes, so a page never compares
  positions across the two logs.

## 5. The three versions, and an updated graphic on air (O3, D1)

Each output already reports in its READY entry: `v`, the version every graphic of which it holds
(on air), and `chg`, the newer version it is taking: `chg.v` (stored: the newest published version
it has read), `chg.s`/`n`/`of` (prepared: how many of the changed graphics are built and checked),
`chg.w` (graphics whose prepared frame waits because they are on air here). G2 adds one member:

- `chg.b`: the graphics whose change is still being built or waiting to be built (at most 6
  names, with `chg.of - chg.n` the count). So for each graphic a page can say whether this output
  has the new design stored only, prepared, or on air.

What the page says (section 6 gives the tones):

- A graphic whose new design is **prepared and waiting** because it is on air: green, "Scorebug:
  new design at next Take" (O3). Today that reads amber "Waiting for clear".
- A graphic whose new design is **still being built** on some output: amber "Updating outputs",
  and if it is taken meanwhile it plays its previous design (the swapper only swaps a ready frame,
  `swap.ts` `beforePlay`) and the panel line says "Scorebug: playing its previous design on
  <output>, the new one is loading" (D1).
- An output that has **not read** the published version yet (behind, today's "Behind: showing
  v12"): amber "Updating outputs".
- A **failed** change: red, as today ("Change failed: Frost Quiz (script error)").
- `holds()` (`swap.ts:217`) is unchanged: false while any prepared frame waits, which is why the
  output reports `waiting` rather than holding the version. The O3 green words go only to the
  graphics listed in `chg.w` of a `waiting` report for the published version. A `waiting` report
  with no `w` list (the whole-page reload for another stage resolution, `prepare.ts:176,186`, which
  takes the new design only once everything is off air, sometimes with `why: 'unreachable'`)
  keeps today's amber words ("Behind: showing v12", "Page not reachable").

## 6. The light (E1, O2, D4)

The page's judgement lives in a plain module, `src/control/runConfirm.ts` (Node-tested), and
`playoutStatus.ts` turns it into the light's tone and words.

### What the page tracks

- **Owed positions.** The positions every expected output must reach, each with the time this
  page learned of it: every command row it follows on the log, whoever wrote it, with its
  graphic; its own send answers (`seq_to`, at once, before the row arrives), with their graphics;
  and, with no graphic, the head's `seq` the page resolves when it opens and again at each new
  epoch (the **seed**, so an output that is behind reads as unconfirmed on a page opened after the
  press, or on a second operator's page).
- **Run reports.** Per output id, the newest `run` it has heard, from report rows and from the
  Presence entry, the newest heard winning within one epoch (a report row or entry with a lower
  `p` than one already heard is older and is ignored; at an equal `p` the later one's `h` wins),
  with the `h` that came with it.
- **Expected outputs.** `rememberOutputs`, never forgotten automatically (D4).

### Confirmed

Per output `o`, judged per graphic, with its run report in the current epoch:

- A position `c` of graphic `g` is **confirmed** when `ran(o, g) ≥ c`. A held or failed graphic
  never blocks the confirmation of another graphic's commands.
- A position `c` of graphic `g` that `o` has received (`c ≤ p`) and that `h[g]` covers
  (`h[g].from ≤ c`) is judged by that entry, not by its age: `loading`, `late` and `placing` read
  amber with their own words (so S4's late Take is named as such), `failed` reads red.
- A position `o` has **not received** (`c > p`) is judged by age, from when this page learned of
  it, oldest first, so a stream of newer commands never resets it: grey under 5 s, amber from
  5 s, red from 15 s. The seed has no graphic and needs `p ≥ c` only; since an output that has
  had no command for a while confirms it only through its Presence entry (one call per 10 s), the
  seed reads grey under 15 s, amber from 15 s and red from 30 s. While this page's Presence is
  not joined, an unconfirmed seed stops at amber "<output> cannot confirm" and never turns red:
  a quiet output has no other way to confirm it.

Green needs every owed position confirmed by every expected output (R13). **The worst tone
wins** (red, then amber, then grey, then green); within a tone the first row of the table below
that applies gives the words.

| A position the output has not received, from 5 s (the seed: 15 s) | amber | Waiting for <output> | "<output> has not confirmed <graphic> (<n> s)" |
| An expected output with no `run` (built before this phase) | amber | Reload <output> when nothing is on air | "It cannot confirm what it runs. Reload it when nothing is on air, or press Check now." (E2's words) |
| An expected output following the old log (protocol 1) | amber | <output> cannot confirm | "It follows this production's older log, which cannot confirm what it runs. It confirms again once the rows from before the numbered log are pruned (within 14 days) and it reloads." |
| A position the output has not received, under 5 s (the seed: 15 s) | grey | Confirming… | "<graphic>: sent, waiting for <output>" |
| Not published | grey | Not published | as today |
| No output known | grey | No output | as today ("Not connected") |
| Everything confirmed, every output ready, a prepared design waiting on air | green | <graphic>: new design at next Take | as section 5 |
| Everything confirmed, every output ready | green | Ready | "Ready for playout · <n> of <n> outputs" |

Reasons in words: `unpublished` "it is not published", `load` "it did not load in 20 s",
`script` "it has a script error", `limit` "too many commands while it loaded", `typed` E1.1's
existing reason.

- **Green is only ever "every owed position confirmed by every expected output".** The Presence
  fallback (the heartbeat line when Presence is down, `describeReadiness` `fallbackView`) can no
  longer read green while a position is owed and unconfirmed; report rows still confirm while
  Presence is down. A page that cannot reach the server at all keeps today's stale words.
- **"Connected" becomes "Ready"** in the header (O2). "CasparCG ready" before a publish stays.
- **The late Take, after it appears.** Once its queue has run, the graphic is confirmed and the
  light returns to green; the output's next entry carries `lt: [{ g, ms }]` for 60 s, and the
  panel lists "<graphic> appeared on <output> without its entrance (loaded after <n> s)" for that
  time, so the operator can see what happened.
- **The cue's mark (O7).** A cue whose graphic is `loading`, `late` or `placing` on any output
  shows a "Loading" mark on its rundown row until it is confirmed or fails. A mark, not a
  control.
- **PROGRAM's tag** (`programAir`): today "logged 14:02:11" says the log took it and "not
  confirmed here". It gains `ran`: "ran 14:02:11" once every expected output has confirmed the
  last command this page sent, with the title "Every output reported running it. A browser cannot
  prove the broadcast picture." Until then it stays "logged".
- **Timing constants**, in `runConfirm.ts`: 5 s to amber for a command (15 s for the seed) (a report row fires within 2 s of a
  change, plus a round trip; the build measures it with the phase 2 harness), 15 s to red (the
  same as `NOT_ANSWERING_MS`; 30 s for the seed), S4's 5 s, `FRAME_LOAD_MS` 20 s. The page measures its ages on its
  own clock from when it learned of the position; the output decides S4's entrance on its own
  clock from when the Take reached it, as S4 says.

## 7. Compatibility and order

- **No migration.** Both channels exist; `state` is stored as sent. No server function, policy or
  table changes. The build is one app release.
- **Old outputs, new pages.** An open output built before this phase reports no `run`. New pages
  judge it unknown: amber "Reload <output> when nothing is on air" (E2's words), never green. It
  keeps playing exactly as today. It moves to the new build by itself the way it already does: on
  the next prepare request (a publish, or Check now) once nothing is on air there
  (`prepare.ts` `newBuild`, `reloadWhenClear`).
- **New outputs, old pages.** An old page ignores `state.run`, `ready.run`, `chg.b` and `lt`
  (`readOutputReady` reads only the members it knows). A new output's reports of graphics it did
  not run stop, which removes a false "aired" from old pages' timed cues; a new output's change
  waiting on air still reports `chg.s: 'waiting'` with `w`, which old pages word as today.
- **Production copies (phase 4).** G2 reads the published payload and the log, as today, whichever
  way the `production_copies` switch is set; nothing here reads or writes a production record.
- **Chromium 71.** Everything the output runs is ES2017: no optional chaining, no nullish
  coalescing, no `Array.prototype.at`, no `Object.fromEntries` in `src/output/` and the modules it
  imports; the pure hold module is checked by the existing output-reader check
  (`scripts/check-output-reader.mjs`).
- **D1's number.** G2 does not depend on a new graphic loading in 5 s. It removes the wait before
  loading starts (the demand skips the Presence request and the 15 s spacing, which phase 2 found
  to be 22 to 25 s of the 30 to 33 s on the large fixture throttled); the download itself is still
  the whole payload until phase 7. Whatever the load takes, S4 decides what airs, and the light
  says it. The owner's D1 decision before phase 6 does not change this design.

## 8. Rules

Observable lines; the build PR repeats them under **Rules** and tests them.

- R1. Each output reports, on every report row and in its Presence entry, `run = { o, p, h, e }`;
  for every graphic `g`, every command of `g` at or below `ran(o, g)` (`h[g].from - 1` when
  listed, else `p`) is settled on that output (the only direction a page reads). Every command handed to the stage gets exactly one outcome by its position (posted,
  skipped or failed), on removal and replacement too, and at the latest 20 s after it was handed
  over (`failed`, `load`); one with no outcome yet is listed. An entry that cannot list every
  unsettled graphic lowers `p` so this still holds. An output on protocol 1 reports no run.
- R2. An output writes a report of a graphic only when it hosts that graphic, its frame has
  released, and the graphic has nothing held or failed; such a graphic gets no forced re-bank and
  no republish report either. So no report this output writes banks a command it has not
  settled. A report fires at most 800 ms after a graphic's last change and at most 2 s after its
  first unreported change; a command settled with no report of its own leads within 2 s to a
  re-bank of one hosted, released, settled graphic, when the output hosts one.
- R3. A Take of a graphic that is not hosted and released starts a hold; from then every command
  of that graphic waits in its held queue, in position order, once each, until the hold ends; no
  command of it reaches the stage meanwhile; other graphics are not delayed.
- R4. A command that is not a Take, for a graphic that is not hosted and released and holds
  nothing, or whose hold has failed, is settled at once; its data and event values become that
  graphic's held values, which its frame receives first when it is hosted or released.
- R5. A live hold whose queue ends in a Take, of a graphic the stage does not host, reads the
  published payload at once, without the Presence request or the 15 s spacing; with the graphic
  in it, the graphic is added to the stage at once beside any running preparation and the payload
  is not read again for that preparation; without it, the hold fails `unpublished`.
- R6. A held queue whose last lifecycle command is an Out is cancelled: it starts no demand, never
  fails, and gives the document only its merged data; the graphic is never seen and every command
  settles. A deletion (a published version without the graphic, a cancelled hold's demand answer
  without it, or a payload without it read during a boot's catch-up) drops the queue and settles
  its commands with no failure; it never appears afterwards.
- R7. On release, a queue whose newest surviving Take reached the output at most 5 s earlier (its
  own clock), with no boot history in its frame, runs in order with its entrance; otherwise the
  frame is hidden (no picture and no sound) while the queue runs and until it stands still (at
  most 6 s), then shows its final state with no entrance. Data, events, Next steps and exits apply
  in both cases; only an entrance a later Take or Out of the same queue replaces is skipped.
  Clearing a frame's hidden flag never shows or unmutes what the stage holds off air or quiet.
- R8. A hold that is not cancelled fails (`unpublished`, `load` at 20 s, `script`, `limit` over
  200 per graphic or 1,000 per output after merging updates) and drops its queue; the failure
  stays in `h` until a later Take or Out of that graphic settles on that output, All out stops
  it, or it is deleted. A `typed` entry is not one of these: it lasts exactly as long as
  `typedRefused` holds the graphic (E1.1).
- R9. A boot frame that releases after the stage returned on air runs its queued history hidden
  and shows only its final state; a restart replays held commands from the banks; a graphic the
  head says is on that no report banks replays its own rows below `followFrom`, typed or not,
  within `unreportedPages`, else takes its head cue's published values.
- R10. A command is claimed once when received; a held command owns its claim; no command runs
  twice on one output, and a refill never re-holds a row.
- R11. Each output's READY change report names the graphics whose change is still building
  (`chg.b`); a graphic in `chg.w` of a `waiting` report for the published version reads green
  "<graphic>: new design at next Take" (a `waiting` with no `w` keeps today's amber);
  one still building reads amber "Updating outputs" and plays its previous design if taken.
- R12. A page owes every command row it follows and its own send answers, each with its graphic,
  and the head position it resolves at open and at each new epoch, with none; an output confirms a
  position of graphic `g` when `ran(o, g)` in that epoch is at or past it, and the seed when `p`
  is; a received position its `h` covers is judged by that entry.
- R13. The light is green only when every owed position is confirmed by every expected output,
  every expected output reports a run, and nothing else is amber or red; a position not received
  is judged by the oldest one's age: grey under 5 s, amber until 15 s, red after (the seed 15 s
  and 30 s, never red while Presence is not joined); `loading`, `late` and `placing` are amber, `failed` is red, and the worst tone wins; an output without `run` is amber "Reload <output> when nothing is
  on air", one on protocol 1 amber "<output> cannot confirm". No output is forgotten automatically.
- R14. Report rows add `state.run` and nothing else to what recovery reads; the page strips it
  before noting machine state; old pages and old outputs keep working as section 7 says.

## 9a. Decisions

- **Report rows carry `run`, Presence backs them.** Presence alone is one call per 10 s per page,
  which would keep the light non-green for up to 10 s after every press; a client broadcast needs
  a new policy (no migration is authorised in this phase); the report row already exists, follows
  every command, and reaches every page.
- **Grey "Confirming…" for the first 5 s after a press.** E1 rules out green while unconfirmed;
  amber is "attention" and would flash on every Take; grey is "nothing is wrong and nothing has
  reported yet", which is exactly the moment.
- **A live Take of a graphic no output can host turns red** ("<graphic> did not play: it is not
  published"). Today it airs nothing and the light stays green, which is the owner's "everything
  looks green" case. A cancelled one, or one read in a restart's history, is a deletion instead.
- **Only a Take starts a hold**; data for a graphic nobody hosts is kept and handed to its frame
  later, so a feed never turns the light amber.
- **Protocol 1 outputs read amber "cannot confirm"**: their log has no contiguous position to
  confirm against, and the prune ends that state within 14 days.

## 10. Limits

- The demanded graphic still arrives with the whole published payload (phase 7 sends only what
  changed), so on a large production a held Take can wait for that download; S4 decides what
  then airs.
- A report row that fails to write is not retried; the Presence entry confirms within 10 s, and
  until then the light is grey, then amber, then after 15 s red, which is true: the page does not
  know.
- A package's own sound during its late case is the Graphic's: NoaCG's exported graphics quiet it
  for `skipAnimation`, a foreign package may not.
- The light confirms what each output ran, never the broadcast picture (E1); the PROGRAM title
  says so.

No behaviour choice the owner has not made was needed. The light's tones and words are O2's,
E1's and E2's; the held Take's entrance rule is S4's; the "Loading" mark is O7's; the previous
design on a Take of a graphic still loading is D1's; outputs never forgotten is D4's. The defaults
chosen here (the 5 s and 15 s ages, the grey "Confirming…", the 2 s report ceiling, the 60 s note
after a late Take, red for a live Take of an unpublished graphic, amber for protocol 1) are
section 9a's, for the review to challenge.
