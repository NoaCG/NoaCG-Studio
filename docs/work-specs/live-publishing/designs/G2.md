# G2: The honest light and held Takes

Design gate for phase 5 (W3, E1, O2, O3, O7, S4, D1, D4, AC-3) of `../spec.md`. It covers every
point the spec lists for G2: per-output, per-graphic reports of received and ran commands, pending
and failed states; never banking past held work; deduplication; reconnect, restart, cancellation
(Out, All out, newer Take, deletion) and queue limits; replaying held history out of sight before
showing its final state, keeping data, events and Next steps; a demanded graphic loading ahead of
background preparation and its 15 s spacing; the three reported versions (stored, prepared, on
air); and unknown never green. Written against `main` at c90317d8. Nothing here is built yet; the
build is the next pull request.

## Terms

- **Output.** One `/output` page (`src/output/main.ts`), named by its instance id
  (`liveInstanceId`, the `id` of its Presence entry). A CasparCG layer is one too.
- **Command.** A log row whose `t` is `update`, `play`, `stop`, `next`, `event` or `snap` (the
  renderer commands, `livePath.ts:267`). `cue`, `staged`, `live` and `ping` are status rows.
- **Lifecycle command.** `play`, `snap` or `stop`. A **Take** is a `play` or `snap`; an **Out** is
  a `stop` (All out writes one `stop` per graphic).
- **Position.** Where a row sits in the log the output follows: its `seq` on the numbered log
  (protocol 2), its row `id` on the old log (protocol 1). Each output already keeps the highest
  position it has read (`lastAppliedSeq`, `lastAppliedId`, `main.ts:943-948`).
- **Hosted.** The output's stage has a current frame for the graphic (`stage.graphics`, or a
  published package's layer). **Released**: that frame's document has loaded, or was released on
  a fallback face (`stage.ts` `release`); only then does a command reach the document.
- **Ran.** A command was posted into its graphic's released document (`postPreviewCmd`), or sent
  as a call to a package's Graphic. A command the stage still keeps (a frame's queue before it
  releases, or W1's place-hint wait, `placeOnOutput.ts`, capped at 2 s) has not run yet. This is the most an output can see. No browser can prove the
  broadcast picture (E1); words below never claim one.
- **Settled.** A command is settled on an output when it ran, or was read past by a rule below
  (a superseded entrance, a cancelled hold, a graphic the published version lacks that no Take
  named). A settled command is never run later.
- **Held.** A command the output has received and not settled, kept in order to run later
  (section 3). **Failed**: a command the output has given up on, with a reason (section 3).
- **Today.** The output claims a command before running it (`main.ts:816`), moves its cursor and
  banks it in its reports whatever happened (`main.ts:948,686`), drops a command for a graphic it
  does not host (`stage.ts:947`) while still reporting that graphic (`main.ts:1009`), and its
  stage only posts or queues (`stage.ts:420`); a frame added by a publish takes its queued
  commands with their entrances whenever it loads. The page says it cannot confirm a picture
  (`playoutStatus.ts:192-199`), reads "Connected" in green from the outputs' READY entries alone,
  and a change waiting for an on-air graphic reads amber (`readiness.ts` "Waiting for clear").

## 1. What an output reports (the run report)

Every output keeps, beside its cursor, an exact account of what it has not settled:

- `p`: its **settled position**. Every command at or below `p` is settled on this output, except
  the graphics listed in `h`. This is the cursor (`lastAppliedSeq`, or `lastAppliedId` on
  protocol 1); it never waits for a held graphic, so other graphics keep playing.
- `h`: per graphic with held or failed commands, `[from, kind, why?]`: `from` is the position of
  its first unsettled command; `kind` is `loading` (held, its graphic is being loaded),
  `late` (held, its first surviving Take reached this output more than 5 s ago, S4), `placing`
  (handed to the stage and waiting in W1's place-hint measurement, at most 2 s) or `failed`
  (with `why`: `unpublished`, `load`, `script`, `limit` or `typed`). A graphic in `h` has settled
  everything before `from` and nothing from `from` on.
- `e`: the first 8 characters of the epoch it follows (protocol 2), so a page never compares a
  position across a republish. `k`: `s` (seq) or `i` (row id), the kind of position.

To know `placing`, the stage counts per graphic the commands it has posted into a document
(`posted(graphic)`, a new read-only map beside `replies`), and `main.ts` keeps the position of each
command it handed to the stage until it is posted; the oldest one not yet posted is that
graphic's `from`.

So for graphic `g` on output `o`: **ran(o, g) = h[g] ? h[g].from - 1 : p**. Received is anything
at or below the cursor; pending is `loading`, `late` and `placing`; failed is `failed`.

**Two channels, no migration.**

1. **On every report row** the output writes, as `state.run = { o, p, h, e, k }`
   (`control_output_report_seq` and `control_output_report` store `state` as sent, migration
   0071 section 6). Report rows reach every production page through the log it already follows
   (`sessionWire.ts:279`). Today an output reports a graphic 800 ms after a command reaches it
   (`scheduleReport`, `main.ts:1009`), and skips the write when data and state are byte-identical;
   `run` is part of that comparison, so every command now produces one report row per output
   within the same 800 ms debounce. That is the confirmation path: about a second after the press.
2. **In the Presence entry**, as `ready.run` (same shape). The entry already goes through the
   page's Presence budget (one call per 10 s, `presenceGate.ts`), coalesced to the newest state.
   It is the backstop: a report row that failed to write, an output with nothing to report (no
   command since it booted) and the names of held graphics all reach the page within 10 s, and it
   tells a page that this output speaks W3 at all. `h` in the entry lists at most 12 graphics;
   when more are unsettled, `p` in that entry is lowered to the smallest unlisted `from` minus 1,
   so the entry never claims more than is true. A report row always carries the full `h` (it is
   bounded by the graphics an output hosts or holds).

**Write volume.** One report row per command, per graphic, per output, inside the existing
debounce, against today's one per changed graphic per output: the only new rows are for commands
that leave data and state byte-identical (a repeated update, an Out of a graphic already out). The
burst cap leaves `live` rows out (`0071_command_sequence.sql:460-468`), so reports never refuse a
Take. No report is sent sooner than today; a report still takes the head lock for one row write,
as today, 800 ms after the press, never inside it.

**Readers.** `run` is one more member of `state`. Recovery reads only `groups`, `awards`,
`appearance`, `typed`, `steps`, `on`, `ograf` and `held` (`outputRecovery.ts:218`,
`reportedTyped`, `reportedLook`, `ografRestore`), and an older page's `machineState` keeps state
whole and reads `groups` (`ProductionPage.tsx:963`), so `run` changes no reader. The new page
strips `run` before noting machine state, so it causes no re-render of the event buttons. A
report with `run` and no machine state is no longer `null`; `reportedSnap` already reads
`groups` only, and `onWithoutPose` (`main.ts:1112`) reads `state.groups` the same way.

## 2. Reporting only the truth, and never banking past held work

- **Only a hosted, released graphic with nothing unsettled writes a report of itself.** Today an
  output reports every graphic a command reached, hosted or not (`main.ts:1009`); a graphic it
  does not host is reported with no machine state and banked, which can overwrite another output's
  good bank of it and, through the timed-cue wire (`cueArmWire.ts:314`), make pages believe it
  aired. Now a graphic in `h`, or not hosted, or hosted and not released, writes no report: no
  scheduled report, no forced re-bank (`REBANK_AFTER`, `main.ts:706`; the `typedRefused` rule,
  `main.ts:677-680`, generalised), and no report on a republish (`onEpoch`). Its bank stays where
  some output that ran it put it, or at the start of the log when none did, so a reboot of any
  output replays its held commands. Banks are per graphic (`alreadyInSnapshot`), so the other
  graphics bank at the cursor as today.
- The run report of the other graphics carries `h`, so the page still learns of held work within
  a second whenever anything else on that output reports.
- **Deduplication.** `applied.claim` (`main.ts:816`) stays where it is: a command is claimed when
  it is received, and a held command owns its claim. The numbered follower hands each position
  once (`seqFollow.ts`), the fast road's copy on protocol 1 is dropped by the claim, and a refill
  re-reading the window behind the cursor finds every row already claimed. A held queue is keyed
  by position, so a row can never be held twice. A reboot starts with no claims and replays from
  the banks, which section 2's rule keeps below every held command.

## 3. Held Takes (O7, S4, D1)

### When a hold starts

- A **Take** (`play` or `snap`) of a graphic that is not hosted and released starts a hold of
  that graphic. Not hosted means the stage has no frame for it (not in the version this output
  holds); hosted and not released means a frame a publish added is still loading
  (`swap.ts` `stage.add`), or a boot frame is still loading after the stage came back on air.
- From then until the hold ends, **every command for that graphic** (update, event, Next, snap,
  Out, another Take) joins its held queue in position order, and nothing of it reaches the stage.
  `applyCommand`'s bookkeeping (merged data, clocks, typed gate, steps, on-air sets) runs when a
  held command is applied, not when it is received, so it sees the commands in the same order as
  a graphic that never waited.
- A command that is not a Take, for a graphic that is not hosted and holds nothing, is settled at
  once: its data and event values go into the graphic's merged data (as `applyCommand` does
  today before the stage), and nothing posts. When that graphic is later hosted, the first thing
  its frame receives is that merged data (the swapper's `data(graphic)` already hands a joining
  frame its held values). So a data feed writing to a graphic no output hosts never holds
  anything and never turns the light amber.
- A held queue is never a reason to wait for anything else: other graphics, the follower's cursor
  and the reports of other graphics carry on.

### Loading the demanded graphic (ahead of background preparation)

- A hold of a graphic the stage does not host starts a **demand**: the output reads the published
  payload at once (`controlOutputResolve`, retried as `untilAnswered` does), not waiting for a
  Presence prepare request and not waiting for the preparer's 15 s spacing (`prepare.ts:36`).
  At most one demand read is in flight; a hold that starts while one is in flight is answered by
  the next read that starts after the hold began (one queued read covers every hold since).
- When the answer holds the graphic, the output adds that graphic to the stage at once
  (`stage.add`, the version it came from passed for its package base and its sounds), records
  its digest as the one it holds (so the swapper never adds or builds it again), and tells the
  preparer the version it saw, which the preparer takes in its turn as today. A preparation
  already running is not waited for and not cancelled: the demanded frame loads beside it. Today's
  swapper builds changed graphics one at a time; a demanded add never queues behind them.
- When the answer does not hold the graphic, the hold **fails** with `unpublished`.
- A demanded frame that has not released `FRAME_LOAD_MS` (20 s) after it was added fails with
  `load`; one whose document reports a script error while loading fails with `script`. A frame
  that releases later is still used for the graphic's next Take (the failure is reported, the
  frame is kept).
- A graphic that is hosted and not released needs no demand: its frame is loading already. A boot
  frame that has not released when a live Take starts its hold already holds the boot's history
  in its stage queue; its release is then always case 3 below (history never plays on air), with
  the boot's queue first and the held queue after it.

### When the graphic releases

The held queue is applied in order through `applyCommand`, as a batch, with
`supersededAnimations` (`seqFollow.ts:333-358`): an entrance a later Take or Out of the same
graphic in the queue replaces is not animated; data, events, Next steps and exits all apply.

1. **Cancelled.** The queue's last lifecycle command is an Out (an Out, All out's `stop`), or the
   queue holds no Take any more (section "Cancellation"): nothing of it reaches the document
   except its data. The frame receives one `update` with the graphic's merged data, nothing else;
   every command in the queue is settled. The graphic is never seen, even briefly: its document
   never received a `play`, so it stays in the template's invisible start state.
2. **On time.** The last surviving Take reached this output (on its own clock, `Date.now()` when
   the row was received) at most 5 s before the release: the queue is applied as live commands
   are, entrance included. AC-3's "first seen once loaded, with its entrance".
3. **Late.** Otherwise the frame is put **off air** first (the document's own `offair`, which keeps
   its frame rate, `composeDocument.ts:608`; a package's `setOffAir`), the queue is applied, the
   frame is watched until it stands still (two answered state asks in a row with the same
   playhead, `catchUp.ts` `stoodStill`, at most 6 s, `CATCH_UP_TIMING.capMs`), then it comes on
   air in its final state, without its entrance, as after a restart (S4). The cap always ends it:
   whatever goes wrong, the frame comes back on air at the cap, as `airWhenSettled` does.
   Commands for the graphic that arrive during that settle are applied to the same hidden frame
   and are part of the final state.

The stage keeps a per-frame hidden flag beside its whole-stage `offAir` (`stage.ts:531`); a frame
is off air while either is set, so the boot's `setVisible(true)` never shows a frame in its own
settle, and a frame's settle ending never shows a stage the boot holds off air.

### Cancellation

- **Out and All out** while held: the stop joins the queue; the queue ends cancelled (case 1)
  unless a later Take follows it.
- **A newer Take** of the same graphic: joins the queue; the earlier entrance is superseded. The
  5 s of case 2 is counted from the newest Take's arrival.
- **A newer Take of another graphic on the same layer** is that graphic's own command; the held
  graphic's queue is not touched (layers are the stage's, not the hold's, as today).
- **Deletion**: a publish that no longer has the graphic (the swapper's `remove`, or a demand
  answer without it after it was added) drops its held queue; its commands are settled, reported
  as cancelled (they leave `h` with no failure). A held graphic counts as off air for the
  swapper's removal, so the removal is not kept waiting by a Take that never aired.
- A **failed** graphic's queue is dropped at the failure and nothing of it airs. Its `failed`
  entry stays in `h` until a later Take or Out of that graphic settles on this output (a Take
  starts a new hold, or runs at once if the graphic is now hosted and released).

### Limits

- At most 200 commands are held per graphic and 1,000 per output (the stage's own `WAITING_KEPT`
  is 200). Before the per-graphic limit is reached, consecutive `update`s in the queue are merged
  into one (merged as `applyCommand` merges data, clock fields included, so the final values are
  the same) and superseded entrances are dropped. If the queue is still over the limit, the
  graphic fails with `limit` and its queue is dropped.
- A held command waits as long as the graphic is loading: while the output keeps reading the
  payload, its `h` says `loading`, then `late` after 5 s, then `failed` with `load` at 20 s.

### Typed data and packages

- The typed gate (`typedOutputGate`) runs when a held command is applied, so a refused envelope is
  refused exactly as it is today and the graphic is held by E1.1. A graphic E1.1 holds is reported
  in `h` as `failed` with `typed` from its refused command, and leaves `h` when a native update
  clears it, as E1.1 says.
- A published package's commands already wait in order while its base is asked for
  (`waitingOgraf`, `stage.ts:596`). The same hold rules apply to it: it is held while its base is
  pending or its layer has not loaded, and its late case puts its layer off air with `setOffAir`.
  Its answers stay the existing package outcome words (`ografOutcome.ts`).

## 4. Reconnect, restart and late frames

- **Reconnect.** Nothing held is lost: the queue lives in memory and the follower's refill hands
  only rows not yet read. Report rows that failed to write while the network was down are not
  retried; the next report (any graphic) and the next Presence entry carry the current `p` and
  `h`. Presence rejoins re-send the entry (`livePath.ts`, "A rejoin starts with no entry").
- **Restart.** The held queues are gone with the page, but no bank passed them (section 2), so
  the boot's catch-up replays them, hidden, as it replays everything (`main.ts` boot recovery).
  A graphic the catch-up commands that the payload does not hold is held and demanded as in
  section 3; since the catch-up is history, its release is always case 3 (late), or case 1.
  A restart that happens while a graphic is on air on other outputs and loading here shows what
  is on air once loaded.
- **Late boot frames.** Today a boot frame that releases after the stage came back on air (the
  6 s cap of `airWhenSettled`) runs its queued history on air, entrances included. Now such a
  frame is held off air with its own flag until it has released, run its queue and stood still
  (case 3), so a restart never plays history on air.
- **Republish** (a new epoch, `onEpoch`): held queues stay (they are commands owed to air); their
  `from` reads 0 in the new epoch until they settle, and `e` changes, so a page never compares
  positions across the two logs.

## 5. The three versions, and an updated graphic on air (O3, D1)

Each output already reports in its READY entry: `v`, the version every graphic of which it holds
(on air), and `chg`, the newer version it is taking: `chg.v` (stored: the newest published version
it has read), `chg.s`/`n`/`of` (prepared: how many of the changed graphics are built and checked),
`chg.w` (graphics whose prepared frame waits because they are on air here). G2 adds one member:

- `chg.b`: the graphics whose change is still being built or waiting to be built (at most 6
  names, with `chg.of - chg.n` the count). So for each graphic a page can say whether this output
  has the new design stored only, prepared, or on air.

What the page says (section 6 gives the tones):

- A graphic whose new design is **prepared and waiting** because it is on air: green, "Scorebug:
  new design at next Take" (O3). Today that reads amber "Waiting for clear".
- A graphic whose new design is **still being built** on some output: amber "Updating outputs",
  and if it is taken meanwhile it plays its previous design (the swapper only swaps a ready frame,
  `swap.ts` `beforePlay`) and the panel line says "Scorebug: playing its previous design on
  <output>, the new one is loading" (D1).
- An output that has **not read** the published version yet (behind, today's "Behind: showing
  v12"): amber "Updating outputs".
- A **failed** change: red, as today ("Change failed: Frost Quiz (script error)").
- `holds()` (`swap.ts:217`) keeps its meaning (every graphic holds the version); the green words
  for a waiting graphic are decided on the page from `chg.w` and `chg.s`, so an output that
  reports `waiting` is green with the O3 words, never amber.

## 6. The light (E1, O2, D4)

The page's judgement lives in a plain module, `src/control/runConfirm.ts` (Node-tested), and
`playoutStatus.ts` turns it into the light's tone and words.

### What the page tracks

- **Owed commands.** Per graphic, the newest command the page knows of: from its own send answer
  (`seq_to` and the graphic) at once, and from every command row it follows on the log, whoever
  wrote it. A command for a graphic the page's published version does not hold counts only if it
  is a Take (a Take nothing can air is still a Take that did not air). Commands from before the
  page opened are not owed; what an output still holds from then reaches the page through `h`.
- **Run reports.** Per output id, the newest `run` it has heard, from report rows and from the
  Presence entry, newest `p` winning within one epoch.
- **Judged outputs.** The expected outputs (`rememberOutputs`; never forgotten automatically, D4)
  that speak W3 (their entry carries `ready.run`, or a report row from them has).

### Confirmed

A command for graphic `g` at position `c` is **confirmed** when every judged output present has
`ran(o, g) ≥ c` in the same epoch and kind. Everything else is unknown or worse, and **green
needs every owed command confirmed** on every expected output (E1).

### Tones and words (O2)

Worst first; the existing checks (Bridge, slot, files, playback) keep their places.

| State | Tone | Header words | Panel line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not published | grey | Not published | as today |
| No output known | grey | No output | as today ("Not connected") |
| An owed command younger than 3 s not yet confirmed | grey | Confirming… | "<graphic>: sent, waiting for <output>" |
| An owed command 3 s or older not confirmed, output present | amber | Waiting for <output> | "<output> has not confirmed <graphic> (<n> s)" |
| A graphic `loading` on an output | amber | Updating outputs | "<graphic>: loading on <output>" |
| A graphic `late` (its Take held over 5 s) | amber | Updating outputs | "<graphic>: loading on <output>, it will appear without its entrance (S4)" |
| A new design still being built, or an output behind | amber | Updating outputs | as section 5 |
| An expected output with no `run` (built before this phase) | amber | Reload <output> when nothing is on air | "It cannot confirm what it runs. Reload it when nothing is on air, or press Check now." (E2's words) |
| An owed command not confirmed 15 s after it was sent | red | <output> did not confirm | "<output> has not confirmed <graphic> for <n> s" |
| A graphic `failed` on an output | red | <graphic> did not play | "<graphic> did not play on <output>: <reason>" |
| Output lost, slot lost, a change failed, a graphic that cannot play | red | as today | as today |
| Everything confirmed, every output ready | green | Ready | "Ready for playout · <n> of <n> outputs" |
| …with a prepared design waiting on air | green | Ready | "Scorebug: new design at next Take" (shown in the header instead of "Ready" while it lasts) |

Reasons in words: `unpublished` "it is not published", `load` "it did not load in 20 s",
`script` "it has a script error", `limit` "too many commands while it loaded", `typed` E1.1's
existing reason.

- **Green is only ever "every owed command confirmed by every expected output".** The Presence
  fallback (the heartbeat line when Presence is down, `describeReadiness` `fallbackView`) can no
  longer read green while a command is owed and unconfirmed; report rows still confirm while
  Presence is down. A page that cannot reach the server at all keeps today's stale words.
- **"Connected" becomes "Ready"** in the header (O2). "CasparCG ready" before a publish stays.
- **The late Take, after it appears.** Once its queue has run, the graphic is confirmed and the
  light returns to green; the output's next entry carries `lt: [{ g, ms }]` for 60 s, and the
  panel lists "<graphic> appeared on <output> without its entrance (loaded after <n> s)" for that
  time, so the operator can see what happened.
- **The cue's mark (O7).** A cue whose graphic is `loading` or `late` on any output shows a
  "Loading" mark on its rundown row until it is confirmed or fails. A mark, not a control.
- **PROGRAM's tag** (`programAir`): today "logged 14:02:11" says the log took it and "not
  confirmed here". It gains `ran`: "ran 14:02:11" once every judged output has confirmed the last
  command this page sent, with the title "Every output reported running it. A browser cannot
  prove the broadcast picture." Until then it stays "logged".
- **Timing constants**, in `runConfirm.ts`: 3 s to amber (a report row takes the 800 ms debounce
  plus a round trip, about 1 to 1.5 s, phase 2's harness to confirm), 15 s to red (the same as
  `NOT_ANSWERING_MS`), S4's 5 s, `FRAME_LOAD_MS` 20 s. The page measures its ages on its own
  clock from when it learned of the command; the output decides S4's entrance on its own clock
  from when the Take reached it, as S4 says.

## 7. Compatibility and order

- **No migration.** Both channels exist; `state` is stored as sent. No server function, policy or
  table changes. The build is one app release.
- **Old outputs, new pages.** An open output built before this phase reports no `run`. New pages
  judge it unknown: amber "Reload <output> when nothing is on air" (E2's words), never green. It
  keeps playing exactly as today. It moves to the new build by itself the way it already does: on
  the next prepare request (a publish, or Check now) once nothing is on air there
  (`prepare.ts` `newBuild`, `reloadWhenClear`).
- **New outputs, old pages.** An old page ignores `state.run`, `ready.run`, `chg.b` and `lt`
  (`readOutputReady` reads only the members it knows). A new output's reports of graphics it did
  not run stop, which removes a false "aired" from old pages' timed cues; a new output's change
  waiting on air still reports `chg.s: 'waiting'` with `w`, which old pages word as today.
- **Production copies (phase 4).** G2 reads the published payload and the log, as today, whichever
  way the `production_copies` switch is set; nothing here reads or writes a production record.
- **Chromium 71.** Everything the output runs is ES2017: no optional chaining, no nullish
  coalescing, no `Array.prototype.at`, no `Object.fromEntries` in `src/output/` and the modules it
  imports; the pure hold module is checked by the existing output-reader check
  (`scripts/check-output-reader.mjs`).
- **D1's number.** G2 does not depend on a new graphic loading in 5 s. It removes the wait before
  loading starts (the demand skips the Presence request and the 15 s spacing, which phase 2 found
  to be 22 to 25 s of the 30 to 33 s on the large fixture throttled); the download itself is still
  the whole payload until phase 7. Whatever the load takes, S4 decides what airs, and the light
  says it. The owner's D1 decision before phase 6 does not change this design.

## 8. Rules

Observable lines; the build PR repeats them under **Rules** and tests them.

- R1. Each output reports, on every report row and in its Presence entry, `run = { o, p, h, e, k }`;
  for every graphic `g`, every command of `g` at or below `ran(o, g)` (`h[g].from - 1` when listed,
  else `p`) has run on that output or been read past by R4, R6 or R8; for a listed graphic no
  command from `from` on has run. A command waiting in the stage (a loading frame's queue, the
  place-hint wait) is listed. An entry that cannot list every unsettled graphic lowers `p` so this
  still holds.
- R2. An output writes a report of a graphic only when it hosts that graphic, its frame has
  released, and the graphic has nothing held or failed; such a graphic gets no forced re-bank and
  no republish report either. So no bank of a graphic ever covers a command this output has not settled.
- R3. A Take of a graphic that is not hosted and released starts a hold; from then every command
  of that graphic waits in its held queue, in position order, once each, until the hold ends; no
  command of it reaches the stage meanwhile; other graphics are not delayed.
- R4. A command that is not a Take, for a graphic that is not hosted and holds nothing, is settled
  at once; its data and event values become that graphic's held values, which its frame receives
  first when it is hosted.
- R5. A hold of a graphic the stage does not host reads the published payload at once, without
  the Presence request or the 15 s spacing; with the graphic in it, the graphic is added to the
  stage at once beside any running preparation; without it, the hold fails `unpublished`.
- R6. On release, a held queue whose last lifecycle command is an Out (or that holds no Take)
  gives the document only its merged data, and the graphic is never seen; every command settles.
- R7. On release, a queue whose last surviving Take reached the output at most 5 s earlier (its
  own clock) runs in order with its entrance; later than that, the frame is off air while the
  queue runs and until it stands still (at most 6 s), then shows its final state with no
  entrance. Data, events, Next steps and exits apply in both cases; only an entrance a later Take
  or Out of the same queue replaces is skipped.
- R8. Deleting a graphic (a publish without it) drops its held queue and settles its commands;
  it never appears afterwards.
- R9. A hold fails (`unpublished`, `load` at 20 s, `script`, `limit` over 200 per graphic or 1,000
  per output after merging updates) and drops its queue; the failure stays in `h` until a later
  Take or Out of that graphic settles on that output.
- R10. A boot frame that releases after the stage returned on air runs its queued history off air
  and shows only its final state; a restart replays held commands from the banks.
- R11. A command is claimed once when received; a held command owns its claim; no command runs
  twice on one output, and a refill never re-holds a row.
- R12. Each output's READY change report names the graphics whose change is still building
  (`chg.b`); a graphic prepared and waiting on air reads green "<graphic>: new design at next Take";
  one still building reads amber "Updating outputs" and plays its previous design if taken.
- R13. The light is green only when every owed command is confirmed by every expected output
  that speaks W3, every expected output speaks W3, and nothing else is amber or red; an
  unconfirmed command is grey for 3 s, amber until 15 s, red after; `loading` and `late` are
  amber, `failed` is red, an output without `run` is amber "Reload <output> when nothing is on
  air". No output is forgotten automatically.
- R14. Report rows add `state.run` and nothing else to what recovery reads; the page strips it
  before noting machine state; old pages and old outputs keep working as section 7 says.

## 9. How AC-3 is tested

- **Node tests** (`scripts/`, importing `src`): the hold module (`src/output/hold.ts`, pure: the
  queue, its release plan, cancellation, merging and limits) against a small reference model
  written from R3 to R9, not a copy of the module: random sequences of Take, update, event,
  Next, Out, All out, deletion and release times, checking which commands reach the document, in
  what order, hidden or not, and what `h` reports (R3 to R9, R11). `run` and `ran(o, g)`,
  including a truncated `h` (R1). The report gate (R2). `runConfirm.ts`: confirmation per output
  and graphic, epochs, the 3 s and 15 s ages, old outputs, Presence down (R13).
  `scripts/playout-status.test.mjs` for every word in section 6's table; `scripts/readiness.test.mjs`
  for `chg.b` and the O3 green (R12); `scripts/swap-plan.test.mjs` for a held graphic counted
  off air at removal (R8).
- **A blank-page spec on the output stage** (offline `e2e/`, extended in its existing file): a
  frame held off air with its own flag while the whole stage is visible and while it is not; the
  cap always ends it; a cancelled graphic's document never receives a `play` (`data-plays`
  unchanged) and no screenshot during its load shows its pixels.
- **A configured spec with loading delayed on purpose** (extending
  `e2e/configured/per-graphic-replacement.spec.ts`; capacity checked before review): two outputs
  and a production page on the local stack. A graphic added and published while the outputs hold
  the older version; one output's payload read is held back by Playwright `route` so its frame
  loads late. Cases: a Take with a 2 s delay is first seen once loaded, with its entrance (its
  `data-plays` moves and an early screenshot shows the entrance moving); with an 8 s delay it is
  first seen at its final state without its entrance, and the light named it while it waited;
  an Out, an All out and a deletion while it loads leave it never seen; three updates, an event
  and two Nexts pressed while it loads all apply in order (the output's reported data and steps);
  a restart during the hold shows what is on air; a report row delayed by `route` keeps the light
  grey then amber, never green, until it lands; an output whose report is dropped reads red at
  15 s; an updated graphic on air reads "<graphic>: new design at next Take" with the light green;
  an output entry stripped of `run` reads "Reload <output> when nothing is on air". Every
  timing is stamped with one time origin, and first seen is a captured screenshot, as the spec's
  measuring rules say.
- **Save and send paths under delay** (lesson from phases 3 and 4): the Take is pressed with a
  mouse and with a touch, with the send's answer delayed and the page reloaded at once; the light
  never reads green before the outputs confirm, and nothing pressed is lost or run twice.
- **Not checked by the run**, for the owner's final check: a held Take on CasparCG 2.5 and 2.3
  (the output page inside CasparCG's own browser, a late frame coming on air without its entrance
  and without a flash), and the light on a studio network.

## 9a. Decisions

- **Report rows carry `run`, Presence backs them.** Presence alone is one call per 10 s per page,
  which would keep the light non-green for up to 10 s after every press; a client broadcast needs
  a new policy (no migration is authorised in this phase); the report row already exists, follows
  every command within 800 ms and reaches every page.
- **Grey "Confirming…" for the first 3 s after a press.** E1 rules out green while unconfirmed;
  amber is "attention" and would flash on every Take; grey is "nothing is wrong and nothing has
  reported yet", which is exactly the moment.
- **A Take of a graphic no output can host turns red** ("<graphic> did not play: it is not
  published"). Today it airs nothing and the light stays green, which is the owner's "everything
  looks green" case.
- **Only a Take starts a hold**; data for a graphic nobody hosts is kept and handed to its frame
  later, so a feed never turns the light amber.

## 10. Limits

- The demanded graphic still arrives with the whole published payload (phase 7 sends only what
  changed), so on a large production a held Take can wait for that download; S4 decides what
  then airs.
- A report row that fails to write is not retried; the Presence entry confirms within 10 s, and
  until then the light is amber or, after 15 s, red, which is true: the page does not know.
- The light confirms what each output ran, never the broadcast picture (E1); the PROGRAM title
  says so.
- An output that speaks W3 and is present but whose Presence and reports both fail reads red for
  that output after 15 s, as an output not answering does today.

No behaviour choice the owner has not made was needed. The light's tones and words are O2's,
E1's and E2's; the held Take's entrance rule is S4's; the "Loading" mark is O7's; the previous
design on a Take of a graphic still loading is D1's; outputs never forgotten is D4's. The defaults
chosen here (the 3 s and 15 s ages, the grey "Confirming…", the 60 s note after a late Take, red
for a Take of an unpublished graphic) are section 9a's, for the review to challenge.
