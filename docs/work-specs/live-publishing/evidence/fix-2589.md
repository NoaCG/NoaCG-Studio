# Fix #2589 evidence: the typed cue's saves coalesce, so a burst of team saves catches up

Scoped fix inside the live-publishing run (#2361). Phase 8's ten-page check found it after #2573
(merge 67d04688): with seven people each typing into their own cue of one team production every
4 s, every editor's record fell 6 to 38 texts behind and was still behind 30 s after the typing
stopped. With #2573 reverted locally all seven kept up. Nothing typed was discarded.
Review class: Risky (the cue draft's save, a state rule).
Revert: `git revert -m 1 <this pull request's merge commit>`; nothing persisted changed, so no data
step goes with it.

## Cause

Every idle save of the cue being typed (`flush` in `src/control/cueDraft.ts`) starts its own write
through the page's history queue (`writeDraft` in `src/components/home/useRundownHistory.ts`), even
while the previous one is still out. The queue runs writes one at a time, and on a team production
each draft write waits for its own confirmed team save (`commitTeamRundownEdit`, which flushes the
team save and waits while it is pending). When several people save the same production, each of
those saves takes a few refusals and merges, so it takes longer than the typing gap and the queue
grows by one stale text per typed text. The record then walks through every old text, one save at a
time, long after the typing stopped.

Before #2573 the same queue existed, but every queued write was dropped once the history's epoch
moved (`enqueue`), and with teammates saving the epoch moved on almost every draft write: `perform`
found the teammate's change and called `changedElsewhere`, which cleared the history (and
discarded the typed text: #2572). So each teammate's save emptied the queue of old texts. #2573
rightly exempted draft writes from that drop (D5, the `!draft &&` in `enqueue`), which exposed the
uncoalesced queue.

## Rule (observable; stated before the code)

- **D9.** The cue being typed has at most one save out at a time. Text typed while it is out is
  owed, and is sent as one save carrying the newest text once that save has answered, refused or
  confirmed; the older texts typed meanwhile are never sent on their own. A caller that waits for
  the save of text owed when it is called (an Update, a snap) waits for that one save. When the
  page may go, the account pauses or the session ends (leaving or switching production) while a
  save is out, the newest text is saved at once, as today.
- As today, and not changed here: a save that fails with nothing typed after it stays owed until
  the next edit, hide or save request; and a team save that fails after its four refused attempts
  ("Teammates keep saving...") is retried on the team's next good read, up to 15 s later.

D1 to D8 (#2573) and #2572's guarantee are unchanged: the cue being typed is never discarded by a
change elsewhere, and typed text is never written over a teammate's newer text in a different cue
(the team merge and its checked draft proofs are untouched).
