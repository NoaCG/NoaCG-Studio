# Fix #2655: a Take is no longer abandoned after 4 s when the backend is slow

Run issue #2361, phase 9 final check. Risky row: it changes retries, unknown outcomes and order.

## The problem

The page cuts each send attempt at 1.5 s and gives up 4 s after the press, with "Send it again"
(`src/control/failedSends.ts`, `RESEND_WINDOW_MS`). On hosted staging, after every load fix had
landed, Takes were still lost during slow spells (p95 39 s): run 1b 8 of 163, run 2 4 of 233,
five people 19 of 51 (`phase-9-staging-rerun.md`, `phase-9-load-diagnosis.md`). Typed text and
Goals held, because since #2630 they resend until they land.

## Rules (observable; stated before the code)

Revised after the first Rules-first `/so` (grade B, findings 1 to 8; first draft at 338fbe51), the
coordinator's question on All out, and the second Rules-first `/so` (grade B on 42e3a14, findings

for a replacing press, accepted by the coordinator), and round 4 graded a6c35e9 B: `snap` moved

the not-held case stated. Round 5, the Repeated-family reassessment, graded 6412349 A with the
lane model stated at the head of R7, one overclaim corrected and two clarifications.

Terms. A **press** is one call of `sendControlVerb` (one batch: its sender id, press number,
epoch and base, as `control_send_seq` receives them). A **revision command** is an item whose
`msg.t` is `update`, `play`, `stop`, `next`, `event` or `snap` and that carries no `src`: exactly
the items `control_head_effect` (0071) counts in a graphic's `rev`, `by` and `press`.

- **R1. Which presses are sent until they land.** A press is *held* (sent until it lands) when all
  of these are true: it goes on the numbered road (`control_send_seq`, protocol 2); it is not All
  out; its sender carries an epoch; and every graphic it touches has at least one revision
  command in it. A Take (update, play, cue), an Update, an Out (stop, cue), a Next and an event
  are held. Every other press (All out, a cue-only press, a press with no epoch, anything on the
  row-id road `control_send_many`) keeps today's 4 s window and today's notice. A production's
  very first press has no epoch (no head exists until it lands), so that one press keeps the 4 s
  window; every press after its answer carries the epoch it taught the page.
- **R2. The schedule.** A held press is sent again after 0.4 s, 1 s and 2 s, then every 4 s, with
  no end, while the server does not answer or answers with an error that wrote nothing: no
  connection, a gateway's 408, 429, 502 to 504 or 520 to 527, PostgREST's 503, a statement timeout
  (57014), the head's lock timeout (55P03), the burst cap (23514), a serialization or deadlock
  retry (40001, 40P01), too many connections (53300). The last four are the codes #2630 already
  resends for a bound press (`RESEND_CODES`); within today's 4 s window a press that is not held
  keeps reading them as refusals, as today. Its
  first four attempts are each cut at 1.5 s, as today; every later attempt at 8 s (the signed-in
  statement timeout), so a slow spell holds at most one copy of it per 12 s in the server's
  queue (R8). Any other answer ends it: landed, a duplicate (read as landed), or a refusal (R6a).
  The 408 and 429 apply to every press on the numbered road: a gateway that answered them never
  passed the request on.
- **R3. What else ends it.** Its own row heard back on the page's log follow (landed, as
  `heard` does today); Stop sending (R6); an All out pressed on this page (R7a); a later press of
  this page that replaces the state of one of its graphics (R7); unpublishing or closing the page.
  A later press that only modifies its graphic does not end it (R7).
- **R4. It never airs twice.** A held press's rows are inserted at most once, however many of its
  copies reach the server and in whatever order, including a copy that arrives after the
  original landed but before the page heard back, and after the head's 64-key `recent` list has
  forgotten it. Proof below; a test calls the real function on the local stack for each case.
- **R5. The correcting row of #2630 stays right.** 0125's step 7b adds, after an `update` that
  carries a bound figure, one correcting row with `src` and no command id. It is built inside
  the same call that inserts the press's own rows, so it exists exactly when they do: once. It
  carries `src`, so it is not a revision command, and a held press is judged by its own items
  only. No migration: `control_send_seq` is unchanged.
- **R6. What the page shows.** While any press of this page is out, PROGRAM's status tag reads
  "sending…", as today. Once a held press has been out longer than 4 s, the tag reads
  "still sending" with a **Stop sending** button beside it. The pressed button's mark stays
  "Sending". Nothing reads "logged", "sent" or "ran" for it until it lands; nothing reads failed
  while it is still being sent. Stop sending ends every press of this page still out, held or
  waiting (R7): each ends as today's give-up does, with "<Take> is on this monitor
  only. It may not have reached the screens or the log (sending stopped). Send it again." and
  "not confirmed" on the tag. A copy the server already took may still land once; its row then
  takes that notice down (`heard`, as today).
- **R6a. A refusal after an unanswered attempt is not "did not change".** Once any attempt of a
  held press went unanswered, an earlier copy may have landed or may still land, so any later
  answer other than a landing is not read as "nothing happened". A stale or superseded answer is
  read against the head it carries: when every graphic the press touched shows this page's
  sender with this press number or a later one, the press landed (as a duplicate does). Any
  other such answer (one of those heads, or an error such as a revoked page) means the outcome is
  unknown. An answer on R2's resend list is not an outcome: the press is sent again, as R2 says.
  When the outcome is unknown, it ends as "<Take> may have reached air; not confirmed
  (another screen changed it while it was being sent)", owed like an unanswered send, so its row
  heard later settles it, and "not confirmed" on the tag. Never "air did not change". A refusal
  of a press none of whose attempts went unanswered reads exactly as today.
- **R7. Pressing something else while a press is being sent.** (Revised after round 2: strict
  press order would air a Take stuck for 30 s as a late flash before the Out pressed after it,
  the very mistake the 4 s window was made to prevent. Accepted by the coordinator.)

  The model: each graphic has its own lane of this page's presses still out. A press that only
  modifies the graphic joins the end of its lane and leaves when everything ahead of it has
  ended. A press that replaces the graphic waits at most 1.5 s, then clears the lane (ends every
  earlier press on it, held or waiting) and leaves. All out clears every lane without waiting
  (R7a). A failure's notice is a debt per graphic, paid by the press's own row heard back or by a
  later landed press of this page that replaces that graphic; another screen's press never pays
  it. A press takes its number and base before it waits in a lane, as today. The bullets below
  are the consequences.

  A press
  that shares no graphic with an earlier press of this page still out leaves at once. For one
  that does, in the same production, it depends on what the later press does to each shared
  graphic:
  - **It replaces the graphic's state** (it carries a `play` or a `stop` for that graphic: a Take,
    a Re-take, an Out, a Take's peers taken off). Newest wins, as today: it waits for the earlier press at most 1.5 s,
    then leaves, and every earlier press of this page carrying that graphic, held or waiting,
    stops being sent. A copy of one already on its way that lands later is skipped there by the
    server (0071 step 6, `skipped`), so air ends as the newest press left it. No new copy of a
    stuck Take is sent after the Out; a copy already queued at the server that commits before
    the Out airs only until the Out commits behind it, as today. The earlier press ends as today
    with its notice, owed per graphic. The notice stays until its own row is heard, or until
    every graphic it touched has a later landed press of this page that replaces it: a Take of
    g1 that also took peer g2 off, stopped by an Out of g1 alone, stays owed on g2.
  - **It only modifies the graphic** (it carries only `update`, `next`, `event`, `snap` or `cue` for
    that graphic: an Update, a Next, a step, a Back or Reset, which the page offers only while the
    graphic is live). It waits until every earlier press of this page carrying
    that graphic, held or itself waiting, has ended (landed, refused, ended as not confirmed under
    R6a, or stopped), then leaves on top of it,
    whichever way it ended: after a refused Take it is an Update of a graphic that is not live,
    which is what it is today; it counts as sending meanwhile. The wait is transitive through waiting presses. So a Take
    followed by an Update both air, in that order.
  - A press that replaces some shared graphics and only modifies others (no product press does:
    a Take carries `play` for its graphic and `stop` for each peer) waits as a modifying press
    does, on every shared graphic, and ends no earlier press.
  - A modifying press never ends an earlier press. A later landed press that only modifies a
    graphic does not settle an earlier failed press's debt on it.
  - A modifying press waiting behind a press that is not held and not itself waiting waits at
    most 1.5 s, as today, and both are then out: that pair keeps today's behaviour inside the
    earlier press's 4 s window, order limit included.
- **R7a. All out.** All out never waits. Before it leaves, it ends every earlier press of this page
  that is still being sent or has not left yet (waiting for the press before it in `afterPress`,
  behind a held press, or for the road), as Stop sending does (R6), so no press made before the
  panic is sent, or numbered, after it. A copy of one already on its way that lands after All out is refused by the server as
  superseded (0071 step 5); one that lands before it is taken off by it. All out itself is not
  held: step 6 never refuses an All out, so once `recent` has forgotten it, a late copy arriving
  after another screen's Take of one of its graphics would take that Take off air again: a later
  press overtaken. Closing that needs a server-side check (the sender's panic mark already
  covering the press, a live-path change to `control_send_seq`), which is outside this fix and
  filed as its own issue. All out keeps today's 4 s window and today's notice.
- **R7b. A press that waited gets a fair window.** A press that is not held and waited behind a
  held one gets its 4 s window from when it leaves the queue, not from its press, so it is
  never failed with no attempt made.
- **R8. Load.** A held press costs the page one request per attempt: three in its first 5 s (at
  about 0, 1.9 and 4.4 s), the fourth near 7.9 s, then at most one new copy per 12 s (R2).
  (Corrected after coding; the first count said four in 5 s.) The page cancelling an attempt does not take its copy out
  of the server's queue, where it may wait up to the statement timeout and any pool wait.
  A copy the server takes after the original is answered as a duplicate, or refused, under the
  head lock, without writing.
- **R9. Other timers stay bounded.** The waits that bound how long one press holds the next
  (`afterPress`'s figure wait, a panel Take's guard, a restored page's settle time) keep their
  values: they say how long the NEXT press waits before it computes, never how long a press is
  sent. A restored page still settles: the page that went away stopped sending when it closed.
- **R9a. Publishing and saving are not held.** Any press counts as a send in flight
  (`sendsInFlight`, which automatic publishing and the save's pace wait for) only for its first
  4 s after the press, a press waiting behind a held one included. After that it stops counting
  there and stays visibly "still sending" (R6).

## R4, the proof

Fix a held press P: sender s, press number p, epoch E (not null), base b, touched graphics T,
every g in T carrying a revision command. Read `control_send_seq` as 0125 has it.

1. Copies of P are serialised by the head row lock (step 2, `for update`). Under read committed,
   a copy that waited reads the head as the copy before it left it.
2. When a copy of P inserts rows (step 9), step 8 has run `control_head_effect` for each of its
   items, so every g in T that was not skipped now holds `by = s`, `press = p` and a `rev` one or
   more above what it was. A g that was skipped held `by = s`, `press > p` and keeps it.
3. After that, a graphic's `by`/`press` change only through a later revision command: by s with a
   higher press number (one page load numbers its presses upward), or by another sender, or by
   an old writer (`legacy`, press 0). Within one epoch `rev` only grows. A cue row, an arm row
   and a data row (`src`) change neither. A new epoch is a new head.
4. A later copy C of P then meets, in order:
   - step 3: answered as a duplicate when its key is still in `recent`, or when every g in T
     still holds `by = s, press = p`; nothing written;
   - step 4: the epoch changed: refused as stale; nothing written (E is not null, R1);
   - step 5: an All out of s numbered above p has landed: refused as superseded;
   - step 6, for each g in T: `by = s, press > p` puts g in `skipped`. Otherwise `rev` is above
     b[g] (point 2 and 3: P's own landing raised it, and it never falls in an epoch), so g is
     stale unless `by = s` and `press < p`, which point 3 rules out. One stale g refuses the
     whole copy; nothing written.
   - So C writes only if every g is skipped, and then `v_n = 0` and it returns without inserting.
5. Hence P's rows are inserted at most once. A copy that arrives before the original commits is
   point 1: the second to take the lock is the later copy of point 4.

Why the others are not held. **All out** is never refused as stale (step 6), so a copy arriving
after another screen re-took one of its graphics, once `recent` has forgotten it, would take that
graphic off air again. **A cue-only press** moves no `rev`, so step 6 cannot tell its copy from a
new press. **A press with no epoch** skips step 4, and a republish starts the revisions again.
Each stays inside today's 4 s window, which this fix does not change.

## Rules found after coding

Listed apart from the Rules above, as the review checkpoints ask. Each has a test.

- **R7, "itself waiting" made exact** (found by the reference model, seeds 8 and 22): a press is
  waited for whole by a later modifying press when it is held, or when it waited behind such a
  press (directly or through others). A lane with no held press keeps today's release, 1.5 s
  after the press ahead LEFT (the first cut timed it from the later press). A press does not
  count as waiting behind a press it replaces: that press is ended as it is pressed.
  `scripts/take-window-model.test.mjs`, `scripts/seq-send.test.mjs`.
- **R7, the debt of a press that only modified a graphic** is paid by any later landed press of
  it, as today (an Update failed, then an Update landed); a debt of a press that replaced it
  (Take, Out) only by a later landed press that replaces it. `scripts/failed-sends.test.mjs`.
- **R3, its own row** is recognised on either road (the durable row, the seq frame or the fast
  frame) and stops further attempts and any wait between them at once (`hostedControl.ts`
  `hear`). The attempt already in flight still gets its answer (at most 8 s), because that answer
  says what the server skipped; cancelling it lost that (found by the configured "several
  batches" test). A later replacing press does the same.
- **R3, the production closing** (Codex on #2662, final `/so`): leaving the production page ends
  every press it still sends, as Stop sending does, when its playout session is disposed. An
  All out pressed before still goes.
- **R6, "still sending" first** (final `/so`): it shows ahead of "N presses unsent", so Stop
  sending is never hidden by a bound press waiting at the same time.
- **R6, a press ended while still in its lane** (Codex on #2662) never left, so it is not owed,
  whatever ended it.
- **R6, a press stopped before it ever left** (waiting for the road, or made before Stop sending
  and still in `afterPress`) cannot land, so it is not owed: it reads "<label> failed: sending
  stopped". One stopped after it left is owed, as R6 says.

- **R7, a replacing press ends an earlier press that has not been sent yet** (found by the
  offline #1697 test): a Take and an Out 50 ms apart while the road is being decided; the Out
  ends the Take before it leaves, so only the Out is sent. As R7 says, newest wins; the test now
  checks press order with a Take and an Update.
