# Fix #2671: one copy of a held press at a time while the command head is stalled

Run issue #2361, phase 9 final check. Risky row: it changes retries and unknown outcomes. The
Rules are in `fix-2655.md` (R2, R2a, R7's first bullet, R8, and the rule found after coding),
written before the code. Diagnosis: `phase-9-latency-diagnosis.md` section 3 (PR #2672).

## The rule chosen

Every attempt of a held press is cut at 8 s, the signed-in statement timeout, instead of its
first four at 1.5 s. The page sends the next copy only once the one before has ended. After a busy
head (55P03, the head's 1 s lock timeout, which rolls the copy back) the next copy goes 0.4 s
later while the press is under 10 s old. After any other ending, and later, R2's waits by attempt
number apply as before. A press that is not held keeps its 1.5 s cuts and 4 s window.

Why this one. Two causes made a stalled Take late:

- **No answer (staging).** A copy cut at 1.5 s stays in the server's queue, so four copies queued
  on the one head row in 8 s. The only way the page can avoid a second copy while the first may
  still be there is to wait until the server would have ended it: the 8 s cut. A resend gate
  layered on a 1.5 s cut gives the same timing, but it throws away answers that arrive between 1.5
  and 8 s, which R6a reads.
- **Busy head (local, disk capped).** Each copy came back 55P03 after about 1.1 s, and the growing
  waits (0.4, 1, 2, 4 s) then made the Take reach the head up to 4 s after the stall ended. The
  0.4 s resend after a busy head fixes that. Bounding it to the press's first 10 s keeps the
  long-stall rate where it was (one copy per about 5 s).

The cost: a copy lost with no answer and no error (a half-open connection) is sent again after
8.4 s instead of 1.9 s. On staging every cut copy without an answer was still in the server's
queue. Making a replacing press cancel the attempt in flight was rejected: that loses the
`skipped` answer (#2655, rules found after coding), and cancelling never recalls a copy already at
the server.

Reviews before code: three Rules-first `/so` rounds graded B (98e52f89, c16ab130, 5a0e2101, one
family: what changes once a held attempt is not cut at 1.5 s). The Repeated-family reassessment
kept the model, graded it A and gave R2a's text, used as written (184e323c).

run under the 5 minute queue cap, rounds of 40 s, every round with `auto_publish`,

only the instruments added; "after" is the same with this branch's two source files.

Two changes to the scene, so that it uses only the configured suite's accounts and no service key

Three conditions, each before and after:

- **Plain**: the setting above, nothing else.

Reading it:

- **Copies stop piling up.** With slow answers, 21 Takes before had four copies at the server at
  once; after, never more than one. With a stalled head, 23 Takes had two or three at once; after,
  one. The plain runs had one slow Take before (7.6 s, four copies, each answered 55P03) and none
  after.
- **A Take is not made slower by its own resends.** p95 press to ran falls by about 1.2 s with a
  stalled head (7.5 to 6.4 s, against a 6 s hold) and by about 2.9 s with slow answers (8.5 to
  5.6 s). The plain p95 does not get worse (673 to 608 ms with 3 people, 622 to 580 ms alone).
- With a stalled head a Take sends a little more in total (max 5 copies against 4), one at a time,
  each after the one before was rolled back: the 0.4 s resend.
- The 3-people-minus-alone gap does not separate before from after: in the stalled and slow
  conditions the injected spells dominate both modes, and in the plain runs both intervals cross
  zero. This fix is about the Take's own copies, not about what editors add (that is #2670).
