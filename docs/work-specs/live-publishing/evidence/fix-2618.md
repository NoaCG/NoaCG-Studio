# Fix #2618 evidence: a failed team save is tried again on its own timer

Scoped fix inside the live-publishing run (#2361). The phase 9 load diagnosis (PR #2619,
`phase-9-load-diagnosis.md`) found that when a team save fails, nothing schedules it again: the
retry waits for the next good read (every 15 s, backing off to 120 s while reads fail) or the next
keystroke. Locally, editor F's record stayed at its first text 40 s after the load ended; on
staging, 6 of 7 records were still behind their pages 120 s after the run. Nothing typed was lost.
Review class: Risky (retries are a state rule). Client only, no migration.

## Cause

`pushSave` in `src/backend/teamProductions.ts` records a failed save (`noteSave(true)` sets the save
backoff, the edit stays dirty, the reason line is shown) and returns. Only `refreshOnce` after a good
read, or `scheduleSave` from the next edit, starts another save.

## Rules (observable; stated before the code)

- **R1. When.** A team save that reached its send step and failed is tried again by itself, without
  waiting for a read or a keystroke. That is: no answer, an error such as a statement timeout, still
  refused after the merge attempts because teammates keep saving, or browser storage refusing the
  settled copy just before it goes up (it already counts as a failed save today). A save that hangs
  is a failure too, because every REST request of the shared client gives up when the cloud stays
  silent (`answeredFetch`, `src/backend/supabase.ts`, 75 s). Unchanged, and still waiting for the
  next edit or good read as today: a save stopped before its send step (storage refusing the
  durable writes it waits for first, no verified team revision), and a save skipped because the
  account may not author or is not the library in use.
  A failed save's outcome can be unknown: a statement timeout or a give-up may still have committed
  on the server. The retry then carries the old token, is refused, and merges onto the server's
  document as any refused save does today, so it never writes twice.
- **R2. Backoff, per production.** Each production counts its own failed saves in a row. Its retry
  waits 2 s after its first failure, doubling per failure to the 30 s ceiling, each wait spread by a
  quarter either way; a save of that production that lands resets its count, and a save of another
  production landing does not. A production's save, from a retry or an edit, never goes before its
  own wait ends, nor before the tab's save backoff ends (unchanged: any failed save holds back every
  production's next save from this tab by that backoff, as today). So with reads failing too, where
  today nothing is retried, each failing production is sent about once every 22.5 to 37.5 s at
  most, and an edit of any production never goes sooner than it would today. When several
  productions are owed and the tab's backoff ends, each sends its one save, as the read path
  already does today after a good read; their own waits are spread independently. Saves never wait
  on the read backoff. Sync now still goes at once; if it fails, it counts as one more failure and
  the next wait doubles.
- **R3. Single flight.** At most one save of a production is out at a time. The retry is that
  production's one save timer: a new edit replaces it, the read path skips a production that has a
  timer or a save out, and a timer that fires while a save is out sends nothing (the save out
  schedules what follows from its own outcome).
- **R4. What the page shows.** The same states as a retry after a good read today, only sooner.
  While the retry waits, the production stays failed: the chip reads Not synced with the failure's
  reason, and the reason line stays on the production. When the retry starts, the production is
  saving again, as when a read or an edit starts it today: the chip reads Syncing with the reason as
  detail, or Not synced "Still waiting for the cloud" once the first unconfirmed change is a minute
  old (the chip's age runs from the first failed change, so a retry does not go quiet for another
  minute). The reason line stays until a save lands; then it goes and the chip settles to Synced.
  The edit stays on the page and in the device outbox throughout.
- **R5. Stop.** Signing out or switching account clears the timers and the counts: nothing is
  retried for an account that is no longer the one signed in.

## Test

`e2e/sync-dot.spec.ts`, on the page clock:

- A team save fails with a statement timeout while the team reads hang, so no good read can come.
  Over the next two minutes it is tried again by itself 5 to 9 times (the backoff's bounds:
  2, 4, 8, 16, 30, 30 s, a quarter either way), never with two saves out at once; the chip reads Not
  synced with the reason and the reason line stays. Once the cloud answers again it lands within
  the next wait, and the line goes. Without the fix there are no attempts at all.
- Two team productions: one keeps failing while the other is edited every 4 s for two minutes. The
  failing one is still tried 5 to 9 times, its own backoff, not every 2 to 3 s as a count shared
  with the other's landed saves would allow; the other lands at least 15 of its 30 edits and its
  last text within 10 s.
- Both: the failing production is also edited during its waits; an edit never sends it sooner
  than its own wait, so the bounds hold (in the two-production case the tab's backoff alone would
  let those edits go every 2 to 3 s).
- After a later failure, signing out stops the retries: no save goes in the next 45 s.
- Each clock step waits until no save is out, so the bounds do not depend on machine speed.
