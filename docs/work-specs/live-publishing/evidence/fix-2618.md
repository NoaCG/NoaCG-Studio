# Fix #2618 evidence: a failed team save is tried again on its own timer

Scoped fix inside the live-publishing run (#2361). The phase 9 load diagnosis (PR #2619,
`phase-9-load-diagnosis.md`) found that when a team save fails, nothing schedules it again: the
retry waits for the next good read (every 15 s, backing off to 120 s while reads fail) or the next
keystroke. Locally, editor F's record stayed at its first text 40 s after the load ended; on
staging, 6 of 7 records were still behind their pages 120 s after the run. Nothing typed was lost.
Review class: Risky (retries are a state rule). Client only, no migration.

## Cause

`pushSave` in `src/backend/teamProductions.ts` records a failed save (`noteSave(true)` sets the save
backoff, the edit stays dirty, the reason line is shown) and returns. Only `refreshOnce` after a good
read, or `scheduleSave` from the next edit, starts another save.

## Rules (observable; stated before the code)

- **R1. When.** A team save that was sent and failed (no answer, an error such as a statement
  timeout, or still refused after the merge attempts because teammates keep saving) is tried again
  by itself, without waiting for a read or a keystroke. A failure before anything is sent (browser
  storage refused, no verified team revision) is unchanged: it waits for the next edit or good read,
  as today.
- **R2. Backoff.** The retry waits the existing save backoff: 2 s after the first failure, doubling
  per failure in a row to the 30 s ceiling, each wait spread by a quarter either way. One landed save
  resets it. A keystroke while the retry waits replaces the timer and still waits out the backoff.
  So a backend that keeps failing sees at most one save per production every 30 s (give or take a
  quarter) from this tab, and never a faster rate than before.
- **R3. Single flight.** At most one save of a production is out at a time. The retry is that
  production's one save timer: a new edit replaces it, the read path skips a production that has a
  timer or a save out, and a timer that fires while a save is out sends nothing (the save out
  schedules what follows from its own outcome).
- **R4. What the page shows meanwhile.** The production stays failed: the chip reads Not synced
  with the failure's reason, and the reason line stays on the production, until a save lands. The
  edit stays on the page and in the device outbox throughout. When the retry lands, the line goes
  and the chip settles to Synced. Sync now (an explicit flush) still goes at once and cancels the
  waiting timer.
- **R5. Stop.** Signing out or switching account clears the timer: nothing is retried for an
  account that is no longer the one signed in.

## Test

`e2e/sync-dot.spec.ts`: a team save fails with a statement timeout while the team reads hang, so
no good read can come. The record lands by itself within the backoff, with no read and no edit,
never with two saves out at once, and the reason line shows until it lands. While saves keep
failing, the number of attempts over two minutes stays within the backoff's bound.
