# There is one Create with AI option now, and it has no tier name

Branch `claude/cf-one-ai-harness`. This is your 2026-09-10 ask - *"we need to change the harness
names and just have one AI option ... It's a bit confusing to have to choose what model I should
use"* - done for the door. The receipt is
`docs/backlog/one-noacg-ai-harness-not-lite-and-pro.md`, which now also records what is left.

## The route, under a minute

1. `/app`, then **New graphic**.
2. Open the **Create with AI** card.
3. Press **⚙ AI settings**.

## What to look at

- **The sheet has no list of options.** Where three radio cards used to sit - NoaCG Lite, NoaCG
  Pro, Bring your own key - there is now one paragraph saying the hosted route needs no key and
  nothing chosen, and one checkbox, "Use your own AI account instead". A one-entry chooser would
  have been the same defect with fewer entries, so there is no chooser at all.
- **Nothing anywhere reads as an edition.** The step's heading is "Create with AI" whatever runs;
  the read-back beside the settings button says "NoaCG · no key needed" rather than "included";
  the entry card no longer ends with "Free with NoaCG Lite". No screen you can reach says Lite or
  Pro.
- **The under-construction line, in the sheet and under the step's heading.** It says plainly not
  to rely on the hosted route yet and that your own Claude Code or Codex is the better way today.
  I did NOT put that sentence on the entry card: that card has a measured height budget and a
  fourth line pushes the video strip below the fold. If you want it there, the card has to give up
  a line somewhere else first.
- **Tick the checkbox** and the provider, model and key fields appear exactly as before; untick it
  and you are back on the hosted route. Your own key is kept, as you asked - it is just no longer
  presented as a third edition to choose between.

## What I decided, so you can overrule it

The one hosted behaviour is the pipeline everybody already got by default. Hosted Pro's DOOR is
closed; its pipeline is untouched in the tree, because which of the two harnesses finally survives
is a measured comparison and this change deliberately does not make it. My first idea was to let
the machine pick the better pipeline per visitor, and I dropped it: the two are not two quality
levels of one thing, so the step would have changed shape between visits with nothing on screen
explaining why - your confusion, minus the label.

Your longer-term half is untouched and I want to be plain about it: *"it can create graphics like
Claude Code and Codex. It should not just be a template copy with their own text."* The hosted
route still adapts a proven catalog design. Closing a door did not move that bar.
