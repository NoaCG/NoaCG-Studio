# The landing page, in plain words

You passed the page's honesty and failed its voice. The words are rewritten. Nothing that was
true on it stopped being said, and nothing that was only selling survived.

**Route, under a minute.** Open <https://noacg.studio> and read from the top. Two minutes if you
scroll the whole thing.

**What to look at.** Whether it sounds like you. That is the only question here, and it is why
this one is yours: a gate can count words, it cannot tell you the page reads Finnish.

## What changed

- **The slogan you named is gone.** "No seat licence. No monthly bill. No edition above this one."
  The free section now says the studio is free, says the source is open, says where an account is
  actually needed, and asks for your feedback with a mail link. That is the whole section.
- **A second slogan went with it**, and this one was my call: the page used to close the states
  section with "Make it. Brand it. Animate it. Export it. Run the show." It is the same
  construction as the one you deleted, one screen further down, and it said nothing a reader could
  check. If you want that beat back, say so and it comes back in a line.
- **No countable claim is left anywhere.** "Four ways in", "One screen, four doors", "Six free
  starters", "OGraf v1" - all gone, replaced by saying the things exist. The starters card no
  longer lists which six they are either, because that list goes stale the same way a count does.
- **Hype and jargon out**: broadcast-grade, production-ready, plug-and-play, "zero extra work",
  "never a black box", "your graphics are not tenants in our cloud", "nothing phoning home",
  "Solid today, sharper every week", and "not just an animation". The tagline "Live graphics, made
  simple" is gone from the footer, from the search-result description and from the share card.
- **The page stopped repeating itself**: the opening paragraph no longer says free, open source and
  browser-based when the line right above it already says all three; the video section no longer
  restates its own card from further up; the OGraf paragraph no longer says what the three cards
  under it say.

**It is 2211 words down to 1767, and 11496 pixels tall down to 10675** on the same screen.

## What I did not touch, because you passed it

The graded claims, all three dashed **Direction** cards and the note under them, and "Bring your
own artwork" word for word.

## Two things I decided, that you can overrule in a line

- **The step numbers 01 to 05 in the walkthrough stayed.** They are not a count of what we have,
  they are wayfinding down a five-step story, and they cannot go stale. Same for the clock and the
  score inside the demo monitor: that is the graphic's own content, not a claim.
- **"Start creating - it's free" on the top button became "Start creating."** The line directly
  above the button already says free and open source, and the note beside it says no account is
  needed.

## The thing I could not fix with words

The word count fell a fifth and the scroll only a fourteenth. Most of the page's length is
screenshots and card grids. **It also tells its story twice**: the four ways-to-start cards and the
five-step walkthrough walk the same ground, each with its own screenshots. If the page still feels
long when you read it, that is the reason, and cutting one of the two tellings is a design call
with your taste in it rather than a copy edit. Filed in
`docs/backlog/public-copy-should-read-finnish-plain.md`.
