# The Quiz Show kit in three looks, and a lower third drawn in Illustrator

## What changed

The Sticker, Showtime and Arcade looks each have five more graphics: a title card, a key-facts
board (a how-to-play card), a logo bug, a countdown and a sign-off. With the quiz board, the
two-player score and the lower third that is eight, and "A whole kit" in the wizard now has a
**Quiz Show** kit that creates all eight in one of the three looks.

There is also a worked Illustrator example. `docs/svg-samples/sticker-lower-third.svg` is the
Sticker lower third rebuilt as a layered Illustrator export, and `docs/SVG_AUTHORING.md` section
6b goes through drawing it, exporting it and importing it.

## Route in under a minute

Kit: `/app` -> Start from a template -> **A whole kit** -> Quiz Show. Pick Sticker, Showtime or
Arcade under the look, and create. Open the sign-off and the title card first, they are the
biggest pieces.

Illustrator: `/app` -> New graphic -> Import graphic, and drop
`docs/svg-samples/sticker-lower-third.svg`. On the Fields step both rows are labelled from the
layer names and the panel is set to grow. Create it, type a long name in the Data tab and press
Update. Then open the same file in Illustrator and read section 6b beside it.

## What to look at

- Do the five new graphics in each look read as siblings of the quiz board you liked?
- The sign-off is a full frame. Sticker is a flat orange field with black dots, Showtime a
  burgundy curtain, Arcade a violet screen. Too much, or right for a closing card?
- Showtime's bulbs chase on the title card, the facts board and the sign-off. The logo bug has
  none on purpose, because it stays up for hours.
- Is section 6b something a student could follow without help? Is a lower third the right first
  example, or do you want the quiz board drawn the same way next?
