# Draw a custom badge and adjust its points

Pen draws open or closed shapes. Properties changes their paint and points.

## The route, about a minute

[/app](https://noacg.studio/app), after `codex/editor-r1-2b-4-pen` deploys.
Choose **+ New graphic**, **Start from a template**, search **Hairline**,
choose it, then **Skip to finish** and **Edit this graphic**.

1. Click **Pen**. Click three points above the name, then the first point to
   close a badge. Try dragging while placing one point to make a curve.
2. Change **Fill**, **Stroke** and **Stroke width** in Properties.
3. Click **Edit points**. Drag a square vertex or a round tangent handle.
   Undo, then redo. **Select** returns to moving and transforming the whole badge.
4. Draw an open accent line and press **Enter** to finish. **Escape** cancels an
   unfinished path; **Backspace** removes its last point.
5. Save, return Home and reopen the graphic. Export to your usual target.

Judge whether drawing, closing and switching between point editing and whole
shape movement feel clear. Are the points, handles and paint controls comfortable
to use at your normal desktop size?
