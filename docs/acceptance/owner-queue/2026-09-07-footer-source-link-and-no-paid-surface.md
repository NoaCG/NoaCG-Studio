# The footer links to the source, and nothing says "for the core" any more

A competitive read compared our public copy to a rival's and found six statements still describing
a paid surface your 2026-09-07 ruling had deleted, plus a page claiming to be open source with
nowhere to click. All six are fixed. Two are visible in the product.

**Route, under a minute.** Open <https://noacg.studio> and scroll to the very bottom. The footer
line now reads `contact.noacg@gmail.com · Docs · Terms · Privacy · Source · Free forever, AGPL-3.0`.
It used to end `... · Privacy · Free forever for the core`, with no link to the repository anywhere
on the page.

**What to look at.** Two things, and the first is a taste call only you can make. Does **Source**
belong in the footer, or should the repository link sit higher up where somebody deciding whether
to trust the project would actually look? The footer is where a stranger looks last, and "open
source" is one of the three claims the landing page leads with. Second, does **Free forever,
AGPL-3.0** read better or worse than the old **Free forever for the core**? The old one was wrong
after your ruling, but it was also shorter, and the licence appears **three times** on the page -
checked live 2026-09-10, not twice as this item first said. That makes the second question a
little sharper than it was written: the footer repetition is the third instance, not the second.

The new link is styled identically to its siblings, verified in the browser: same colour, same
13px, no underline, same 9px gap. It opens in the same tab, matching the other footer links.

**What changed off-screen, for the record.** `docs/IBC_LISTING_CHECKLIST.md` told you to answer
"is it really free?" with "the only paid surface is hosted AI" - you read that script at the EBU
stand on 11-14 September, four days after the ruling. It now says there is no paid surface, and
gives you the honest boundary if somebody presses: creating, previewing and exporting need no
account, while publishing a hosted show, cloud sync and the audience plane need a free signup.
`README.md` and the operating principles in `docs/GOALS.md` lost the same "for the core" hedge, and
GOALS.md's "Extreme (subscription)" AI row and its parking-lot payments line now say subsidised
rather than sold. `docs/GOALS_ARCHIVE.md` keeps the old wording on purpose.

**What was deliberately NOT changed.** The "Pro (user, a little)" row in the AI tier table. It
reads as a claim about who pays the model provider rather than about a NoaCG price, and rewriting
it would have changed a meaning this session does not own.
