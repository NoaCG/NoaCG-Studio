# Weight, alignment, spacing and long text in the new editor

Text in the new editor now takes a weight, an alignment, line and letter spacing and a choice for a
long value (R1.2b.2), and a text box's sides resize it without stretching its letters, as you chose
on 2026-10-02.

## The route, about ten minutes

After `claude/editor-r1-2b-2-typography` lands and deploys, open [/app](https://noacg.studio/app) at
1920×1080 with a real mouse and keyboard. Click **+ New graphic**, **Start from a template**, search
for **Hairline**, pick it, click **Skip to finish**, then **Edit this graphic**.

1. Select the name (**Alexandra Riva**). Below Font size, set **Weight** to Extra bold and
   **Letter spacing** to 1. Alignment and Long text are not offered there; the note under them says
   the design lays out its own lines.
2. Pick the **Text** tool and drag a box in the empty space above the name. In **Artwork text** type
   a sentence long enough to wrap and click **Apply text**. Set **Weight** to Bold, **Alignment** to
   Center and **Line spacing** to 1.3.
3. Drag the box's right side outward: the box widens and the text re-wraps at the same size. Drag a
   corner: the whole box scales, as before. Undo that.
4. Type **Alexandra Featherstonehaugh-Worthington** into **Artwork text**, Apply, and set
   **Long text** to Shrink to fit: the name stays on one row inside the box, a little smaller.
5. Save, open the graphic's control page from Home, and press Play, then Stop.

What to judge: do Weight, Alignment, the two spacings and Long text read clearly where they sit, and
does resizing a text box from its sides feel right beside its corners scaling? Known and recorded in
the [receipt](../../research/editor-r1-2b-2/README.md): italic, all caps, shadow, outline and typing
on the canvas are not in this phase; a value too long even at 55% of its size runs past a Shrink to
fit box; an imported design's slot, which has only a width, takes its Width typed rather than dragged.
