# Ease a few keys at once in the new editor

## The route, about three minutes

After `claude/editor-r1-2a-2-b82003` lands and deploys, open [/app](https://noacg.studio/app),
make a graphic with a text box, and in **Edit this graphic** animate its Position X and Opacity
with the stopwatch at 0 s and 1 s (two keys each).

1. In the timeline, click the small triangle beside the layer's name. X and Opacity get their
   own rows.
2. Drag a rectangle over the keys at 1 s from empty lane space. The toolbar reads "2 keys".
3. Choose **Easy Ease** in the toolbar dropdown, then press Play.
4. Right-click the first X key and choose **Hold**. X should stay put until 1 s, then jump.
5. Try the same menu from the keyboard: focus a key, then Shift+F10.

Do the rows, the dropdown and the right-click menu feel easy to find and predictable? This is
an asynchronous clarity and taste review, not a landing gate. The
[engineering receipt](../../research/editor-r1-2a-2/README.md) records the parity, refusals,
history and save/reopen evidence.
