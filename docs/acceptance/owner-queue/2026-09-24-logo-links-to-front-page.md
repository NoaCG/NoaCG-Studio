# The logo always goes to the front page

**Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Branch:** `claude/jolly-maxwell-6jcsme`

## What changed

The NoaCG Studio logo in the top-left corner of Home did nothing when pressed: on Home, the
graphic control page and the production dashboard it was a "go to Home" button, so on Home it
went nowhere. It is now a real link to the public front page on every surface, like it already
was in the editor and the video editor. Middle-click and Cmd-click open it in a new tab.

## The route, under a minute

1. `/app#/home` -> press the logo in the top-left corner: the front page opens.
2. Open a production: the header reads logo, **Back**, **Home**, **+ New graphic**. The logo goes
   to the front page; **Home** goes to Home.
3. Open a graphic's control panel: the logo goes to the front page, **← Home** goes Home.

## What to look at

- On the production dashboard, **Home** now sits before **+ New graphic** (it used to follow
  Back after it), because the logo is no longer the Home door there.
