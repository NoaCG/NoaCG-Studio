# Arrange a badge and two accents

Alignment, equal gaps and keyboard movement are available above the canvas.

## The route, about a minute

[/app](https://noacg.studio/app), after `codex/editor-r1-2b-5` deploys.
Choose **+ New graphic**, **Start from a template**, search **Hairline**,
choose it, then **Skip to finish** and **Edit this graphic**.

1. Draw two rectangles and a closed three-point Pen badge above the name.
2. Select their rows with Ctrl/Cmd-click in Layers & Timeline. Click **Left**,
   then **Vertical** under Gaps. Try **Align to Canvas** on one item.
3. Click the canvas and try Arrows, then Shift+Arrows. Ctrl/Cmd+Arrows change
   width or height; Shift makes the change ten pixels. Hold an arrow, release,
   then Undo. Escape cancels an unfinished hold.
4. Save and reopen the graphic. Export to your usual target.

Judge whether the references, equal-gap buttons and keyboard mapping are clear,
and whether arranging the selection feels comfortable at your desktop size.

The [engineering receipt](../../research/editor-r1-2b-5/README.md) records this
complete task, save/reopen and executed SPX, CasparCG and OGraf exports. This asks
for product judgment. Full B04 and physical receiving-host acceptance stay open.
