# Your own AI key, and your AI settings, belong to your account

**Date:** 2026-09-24

## What changed

An AI provider key you save in Settings is kept in a browser cookie for a year. That cookie did
not say whose key it was, so after you signed out, the next person to sign in on the same
computer (or anyone using it signed out) generated with YOUR key, on your bill. The key is now
sealed together with the account that saved it and only works for that account. A key saved while
signed out only works while signed out.

The small AI records on the device are per account too: the Create-with-AI setup draft (your own
words), which providers you have keys for, the AI notice you accepted, which directions you
tend to pick, your AI run history, and the remote-control pairing secret.

**Once, after this ships: enter your own AI key again.** A key saved before the change cannot be
told apart from anybody else's, so it is no longer used.

## The route, under a minute

1. On noacg.studio, signed in: Settings -> AI -> enter your provider key -> save.
2. Account menu -> Sign out. Settings -> AI shows no key of yours.
3. Sign in with the second account: no key there either. Sign back in: your key is there.

## What to look at

- That generating with your own key still works after you enter it again.
