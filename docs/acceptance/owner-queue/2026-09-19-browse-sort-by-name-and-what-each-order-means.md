# Browse sorts by name, and says what each order means

## What changed

The sort control in the template wizard's Browse step has two new orders, Name (A to Z) and
Name (Z to A). Beside the control, one sentence now says what the chosen order does, so
"Relevance" and "Simplest first" are no longer unexplained words.

## Route in under a minute

Open `/app` -> Start from a template. The sort control is on the "Showing 12 of ..." line. Pick
each of the four orders and read the sentence beside it. Then pick Quiz in the type dropdown,
choose Name (A to Z), and check the cards run alphabetically.

## What to look at

- Do the two sentences for Relevance and Simplest first say what you would expect those orders
  to do, in words a student would use?
- Is alphabetical enough, or do you also want an order such as newest first?
