# Dropping several pictures at once now says what it took

You tried to import several pictures at the same time and got one, with nothing said about the
rest: "of course, it doesn't work; it just gets one." You did not rule on what should happen, so
this was decided from ordinary practice rather than sent back to you.

**The decision: still one graphic per import, but never in silence.** The file that was used is
named, every file that was not is named, and the step says the others can come in one at a time as
their own graphics. It does not refuse the drop, because that would throw away work you already
did, and it does not fan out into several graphics, because everything from the drop zone to
Finish asks about a single artwork - one canvas, one erase pass, one field placement, one motion
choice. Importing five would mean five walks.

## The route, in under a minute

1. Open `/app`, click **Import graphic**.
2. Drag **several pictures at once** onto the drop zone - four or five PNGs is the case you hit.
3. The first is imported exactly as before. Under the drop zone there is now one amber line:
   *"Used board-a.png. Not used: board-b.png, board-c.png, board-d.png. One graphic is built from
   one design. Bring the others in one at a time - each becomes its own graphic."*
4. Now drag **a PNG and an SVG together**. The SVG wins whichever way round you grabbed them, and
   the line says why: *"The SVG was used because it is the better import: its text layers become
   fields."* That reason appears only when the drop held more than one kind of design file, so a
   ranking you cannot see actually happened. Five plain pictures get no reason, because none
   outranked another. Drop a picture with a readme and the readme is named as unreadable rather
   than offered as a graphic you could bring in later.
5. Drag **one** file. Nothing new appears at all - the ordinary path is untouched.

## What to look at

Whether being told is enough, or whether you actually wanted the four boards to become four
graphics. That third option is real and is deliberately not built here: it is a change to what the
wizard IS, not to the drop zone, so it wants its own row and your word first. If you read the line
and think "fine, I will drag the next one", this is done. If you read it and think "no, I wanted
all four", say so and it becomes a proper batch import.

Also worth your eye: the line is amber and four lines long in the narrow rail. It only ever shows
after a multi-file drop, so it adds nothing to the ordinary step - but tell me if it reads as
heavier than the thing it is reporting.
