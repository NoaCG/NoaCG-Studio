# Three playout checks on the studio laptop, about ten minutes

Phase 6 changed how every Take reaches the output (a numbered command log, stale presses refused)
and added the output health line. These three things only a person at the real playout can judge.

## The route, about ten minutes

/app on the studio laptop, a production put on air through NoaCG Bridge and CasparCG, and the
production's phone link open on your phone.

1. **Desk and phone at once.** Take a graphic on the desk and press Out on the same graphic on the
   phone within a second. One of the two is refused with a message that it "was changed from
   another screen". Air ends on whichever press the output shows.
2. **Put on Air again mid-show.** With two or three graphics up, press Put on Air again (or reload
   the CasparCG layer). The graphics come back in place, without replaying their entrances.
3. **The health line on the phone.** Read it: it should say something like
   "● 1 output · CasparCG · Chromium …" and stay steady for a few minutes.

**What to look at.** Whether the refusal's wording in step 1 makes sense to an operator in the
middle of a show, and whether anything in steps 2 and 3 would make you distrust what is on air.
