2. New graphic -> **Browse**.
3. Type **end titles**. Read the "Showing N of N" line.
4. Then **eftertexter**, then **lopputekstit**.
5. For contrast, type **thanks for watching**.

## What to look at

**"end titles" answers with 12 designs, and all 12 are credits.** Before this it answered with
17: the credits shelf plus five holding screens, because the alias had been told that end titles
happen at the sign-off moment, and a moment ADMITS designs rather than only ranking them. Someone
typing "end titles" knows exactly what they want, so the five ending cards were noise.

**"eftertexter" and "lopputekstit" now match "credits" exactly.** The Swedish and Finnish words
are supposed to reach what the English one reaches - that is the whole point of the Nordic

**"thanks for watching" still answers with 11 across two shelves.** This is the contrast worth
seeing: the vague phrase still gathers the whole family of endings, which is what the occasion
facet was built for. Only the words that NAME a shelf were narrowed.
