# Build repeated artwork in one graphic

## The route, about five minutes

[/app](https://noacg.studio/app), after the persistent-drawing slice deploys.
Choose **+ New graphic**, **Start from a template**, search **Hairline**,
choose it, then **Skip to finish** and **Edit this graphic**.

1. Choose Rectangle once and draw three badges. Try Ellipse and Text the same way.
   Pick one text row and change its content in Properties.
2. Choose Pen once. Draw an open path and press Enter, a triangle closed by its
   first point, then a curve by dragging a point before pressing Enter.
3. Begin another path and press Escape. Choose Select to move a completed badge.
   Try Edit points on a path, then Escape. Group two badges, enter the group,
   return with Composition and choose the next drawing tool.
4. Add motion with the Position X stopwatch, a second key and a Step. Save and
   reopen, then export to your usual target.

Judge whether repeated construction, choosing Select and leaving point/group
editing feel predictable while making a usable graphic. The
[engineering task and captures](../../work-specs/editor-persistent-drawing/README.md)
cover source, undo, cancellation, save/reopen and executed output packages.
Whole-editor acceptance, actual 125% zoom and physical receiving hosts remain open.
