# Output links sit beside ● SHOW, and "Share with a team…" is "Share"

Once a production is published, its links button is called **Output links**, drawn with the amber
outline and moved from the right-hand cluster to directly after the ● SHOW chip, so the URLs a
browser source needs are the first thing after the production's state. Before publishing, the
same place holds **▶ Start production**. Its panel still opens by itself right after a publish.
**Share with a team…** is now **Share**, and under 1440px only its people icon, with "Share" in
the tooltip. No buttons were added.

## The route, under a minute

1. `/app#/home/productions` - open a published production (Start production on one first, signed
   in).
2. Look along the header, left to right: logo, New graphic, Back, Home, name, ● SHOW,
   **Output links**, clock, Playout/Data/Audience, then Playout, Share, Export, All out.

**What to look at.** Whether Output links now reads as the page's main control once published,
and whether the header still fits at your laptop's width without anything wrapping. A long
production name is what shortens. Branch `claude/intelligent-gates-rlo5fp`.
