# A member removed from a team loses the team's productions

**Date:** 2026-09-24

## What changed

A team member who first published a team production kept reading it after they left or were
removed from the team: its control, output and join addresses, its data key, and what it had
staged and aired. Now a team production is visible only to the team's current members (the team
owner always is one), and a personal production only to its owner. Separately, a signed-in account
can no longer ask whether another account's storage is full.

## The route, under a minute

1. Two accounts, A (team owner) and B (member). As B, open a team production and press
   Start production so B publishes it.
2. As A, Settings -> Team -> remove B.
3. As B, reload Home -> Productions: the team production is gone, and its dashboard link no longer
   opens it.

## What to look at

- That A (and any remaining member) still sees and runs the production exactly as before.
- Anyone who kept the production's control or output LINK still has it: a link is a key by design.
  Unpublish and publish again if a departing member must lose the links as well.
