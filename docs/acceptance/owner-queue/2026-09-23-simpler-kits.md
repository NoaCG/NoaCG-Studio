# Twelve kits and the three Quiz Show looks: do they look paid-for?

"A whole kit" went from 22 kits in four looks each to twelve kits with one Style each, about ten
graphics ticked, and free editing between them. Three new quiz-show looks (Sticker, Showtime,
Arcade) each have eight graphics: quiz board, two-player score, lower third, title card, facts
board, logo bug, countdown and sign-off. Creating a kit is pinned by `e2e/wizard-kit.spec.ts`.

## The route, about five minutes

/app, **Start from a template**, **A whole kit**. Look at the twelve covers, then build **Sports**
and one of the quiz kits. In the quiz kit open the sign-off and the title card first, then take the
quiz board and press Pick C, Pick A and Reveal correct answer.

**What to look at.** Taste only: do the twelve covers read as twelve kinds of show at a glance, are
Sticker, Showtime and Arcade different enough that choosing is a real decision, and are the
full-frame sign-offs and Showtime's chasing bulbs too much for a whole show? Say if anything from
the merged kits is missed.
