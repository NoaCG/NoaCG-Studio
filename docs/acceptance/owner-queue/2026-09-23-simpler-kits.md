# Twelve kits, one Style each, and editing in any order

## What changed

"A whole kit" had 22 kits, every one of them in four looks behind a Look dropdown, with all
of its graphics ticked. After picking one you walked every graphic in order, and a question
after the first graphic asked whether its look should carry to the rest.

Now there are 12 kits: Sports, Esports, Talk Show, Newsroom, Election, Corporate Event,
Creator Stream, Stage & Awards and Worship & Ceremony, plus Sticker Quiz, Showtime Quiz and
Arcade Quiz.

- **One Style per kit, no dropdown.** Each kit has its own default Style, and each kit card
  shows its signature graphic in that Style.
- **About ten graphics ticked.** The rest of the kit's library sits under "More for <kit>", and
  every other graphic in that Style sits behind a closed "Any other graphic" section.
- **Next builds the whole set.** It lands on the kit's Finish step, which lays out every
  graphic. Click any of them to edit it.
- **Free editing.** The tray above the steps jumps between graphics on the same step. Each
  graphic keeps its own changes.
- **One Style for the whole kit.** "Apply this Style to all" copies the open graphic's
  colours, typeface, sizes and motion to the others. Their text stays.

The nine sports kits merged into Sports. Shopping and Wellness merged into Creator Stream.
Classroom merged into Corporate Event, and its quiz boards are in that kit's library.

## Route in under a minute

`/app` -> Start from a template -> **A whole kit** -> Sports -> **Build 10 graphics**. On the
hub, click the fourth graphic, change its first line, click the first chip in the tray, then
the fourth again. Go to Style, pick a palette, press **Apply this Style to all**, then
**All graphics**, and open the production.

## What to look at

- Do the twelve covers read as twelve different kinds of show at a glance?
- Is ten the right number ticked for Sports, or should the scorebug set be smaller?
- Corporate Event and Worship & Ceremony both use Mint, one in the clean minimal designs and
  one in glass. Is that different enough, or should one move?
- Anything you miss from the kits that merged? A kit can no longer be switched to another
  family's designs. Restyling now happens on the Style step. Git has the old 22-kit set if
  you want it back.
