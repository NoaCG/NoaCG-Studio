# Group a badge and edit its members

Group, Ungroup and Edit group sit beside the arrangement controls. Group editing
keeps the canvas, parent bar and local child ruler visible.

## The route, about two minutes

[/app](https://noacg.studio/app), after `codex/editor-r1-2b-6` deploys.
Choose **+ New graphic**, **Start from a template**, search **Hairline**,
choose it, then **Skip to finish** and **Edit this graphic**.

1. Draw two rectangles and a closed three-point Pen badge above the name.
2. Ctrl/Cmd-click their adjacent rows in Layers & Timeline, then click **Group**.
   Move the group with Arrows; try Rotation or Scale in Properties, then Undo.
3. Click **Edit group**. Select a member and change its Rotation. Try the
   parent bar and local ruler, then return with **Composition** above the canvas.
4. Save and reopen. Try **Ungroup**, then Undo. Export to your usual target.

Judge whether entering/leaving groups, selecting members and reading the parent
bar/local time feel clear and comfortable at your desktop size.
