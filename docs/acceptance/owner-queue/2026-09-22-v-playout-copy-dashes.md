# The playout surfaces lost their em dashes

You asked for the same sweep the wizard got on 2026-09-21, this time on the playout pages. Every
em dash an operator could read is gone from the production dashboard, the hosted control page, the
graphic's own control page, the links panel, and both downloadable controllers. Each one became a
plain sentence or a comma, never a hyphen and never a semicolon.

Two places kept a separator instead of a sentence, and I want you to look at them:

- The PROGRAM monitor heading is now **PROGRAM · ON AIR**, with the same middle dot the page
  already uses between layer names. A sentence in a two-word heading would have read oddly beside
  the plain **PREVIEW** on the monitor next to it.
- The browser tab titles of the two downloaded pages now read **<show> · production controller**
  and **<graphic> · control panel**, matching the `<show> · controller` that the controller's own
  header has always printed.

The lines you can read on the dashboard without hovering anything:

- Graphic actions: "These fire the graphic's own beats on the layer that is on air, immediately.
  They carry values from this cue, so type them above first."
- Live numbers: "One press changes the figure on the live graphic and keeps this cue in step, with
  no ✎ Update needed. Typing a value above still stages it for ✎ Update instead."

## The route, under a minute

1. Open any production's dashboard and look at the PROGRAM monitor heading, then at the two grey
   help lines under **⚡ GRAPHIC ACTIONS** and **± LIVE NUMBERS**.
2. Hover **■ Out** in the verb row ("Play <graphic> off. The other layers stay up.") and **■ All
   out** in the header ("Play every live layer off and clear the frame").
3. Open the links popover on a published production and read the Audience link's help ("This link
   is public. Share it with the room. ...") and the presenter link's.
4. Open a graphic's own control page from Home (⋯ on a library row, then Control panel). Read the
   two paragraphs above the entries list and open the easing list under Motion: it now says
   "Auto (recommended)".

## What to look at

Whether `PROGRAM · ON AIR` still reads as one heading to you, or whether you would rather it were
just `PROGRAM` with the dot gone. That is the only judgement call here; everything else is a
sentence where a dash used to be.
