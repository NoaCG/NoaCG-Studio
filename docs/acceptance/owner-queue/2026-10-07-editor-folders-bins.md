# Organize a badge and its assets

Folders organize layers without adding transforms or timing. Bins organize
bundled files. Groups keep their parent bar and local ruler, with an explicit
**Back to Composition** above the canvas.

## The route, about two minutes

Open [/app](https://noacg.studio/app) after the folders/bins slice deploys.
Choose **+ New graphic**, **Start from a template**, search **Hairline**,
choose it, then **Skip to finish** and **Edit this graphic**.

1. Draw two rectangles, select their rows together, and click **Group**.
   With that group selected, use **+ Folder**, type a name and press Enter.
2. Edit the group, select its members and create a local folder. Collapse it,
   select a member on the canvas, and try renaming the folder with a double-click
   or Enter. Escape cancels. Return with **Back to Composition**.
3. Open **Project**, import an image, and create a bin with **+ Bin**.
   Select the asset and choose the bin in Information. Rename the bin and Undo.
   Place the image, then organize its layer in a folder.
4. Save and reopen. Try **Remove folder** and Undo; the artwork stays present.

Judge whether folders, groups and bins are easy to distinguish; whether selection,
inline naming and returning to Composition feel familiar; and whether the stage
and timeline remain comfortable at your usual desktop size and zoom.
