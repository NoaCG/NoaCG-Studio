# The six briefs, as the user typed them

Briefs 1-4 are the 2026-10-02 walk's, unchanged
(`../../plugin-graphics-quality-2026-10-02/harness/briefs.md`). Briefs 5 and 6 are new for this
benchmark: a ticker (continuous motion, a list edited while on air, an urgent override) and a
full-frame result board (dense data, variable row count, staged entrance).

## 1 - news lower third

I produce the evening news for a small regional TV channel in northern Finland, "Pohjoisen Uutiset". Can you make me a lower third for NoaCG? It's for interviewees: their name, their title or role, and sometimes a location line under it (some days we use it, some days we don't). Names often have ä and ö in them and some titles are long, like "Kehitysjohtaja, Pohjois-Pohjanmaan liitto". The tone is a serious public broadcaster, nothing flashy. I don't have a NoaCG account on this machine yet, so just leave it ready for me to import.

## 2 - ice hockey scorebug

We stream our amateur ice hockey league games on YouTube and I want a scorebug for NoaCG that sits in the top left corner the whole game. Two teams as three-letter abbreviations with their colours, the score, the period (1st, 2nd, 3rd, OT), and the game clock counting down from 20:00 that I can start and stop when the ref whistles. When a team gets a penalty I want to show a power play indicator with its own two-minute countdown. I'm the only person running graphics, so it has to be quick to drive. No NoaCG account on this machine yet, so leave it ready for me to import.

## 3 - gala event title

We're livestreaming a charity gala evening, "Valon Ilta 2026" (it raises money for a children's hospital). I need an event title graphic for NoaCG for the top of the stream and between segments: the event name, a short line like "Live from Tampere Hall", and the host's name. It should feel elegant and evening-like, not corporate. No NoaCG account on this machine yet, so leave it ready for me to import.

## 4 - pub quiz

I host a monthly pub quiz that we also stream. Can you make a quiz graphic for NoaCG? It shows the round and question number, the question, and four answers A to D. I read the question out, then when time is up I press a button to reveal the right answer, and the others should dim. Sometimes I want to show a 30-second timer while people think. It should be fun but readable from the back of a pub on a TV. No NoaCG account on this machine yet, so leave it ready for me to import.

## 5 - news ticker

I run the video stream for a small community radio station, "Radio Kaiku". I need a news ticker for NoaCG along the bottom of the screen: a short label on the left (usually "UUTISET", sometimes "TIEDOTE") and headlines that run across one after another. I'll have five to ten headlines and I need to change them while it's on air without it jumping back to the start. Sometimes there's an urgent bulletin, and then I want one line to show straight away in a different colour until I clear it. It stays up for an hour at a time, so it shouldn't be tiring to look at. No NoaCG account on this machine yet, so leave it ready for me to import.

## 6 - full-frame result board

We're streaming the regional cross-country skiing championships. Between races I want a full-screen result board for NoaCG: the race name (like "Naiset 10 km vapaa"), then the top eight with rank, name, club and time, and the gap to the winner for everyone after first. Sometimes fewer than eight finish, and sometimes someone is DNF or DSQ instead of a time. Names can be long, like "Anna-Kaisa Vähäkangas-Heikkilä". It should look like proper TV sports graphics, the kind you'd see on national TV, and I want the rows to come in one after another when I take it. No NoaCG account on this machine yet, so leave it ready for me to import.
