Patterns, as the reviewer wrote them:

- The good cells commit to one idea tied to the show: the gala's "light" star, the pub marquee bulbs, the radio-wave ticker, medal-coloured ranks. The weak ones are default navy plates or cards.
- Within a brief, two cells are often near-twins (b2 Y/Z, b3 X/Y, b4 Y/Z, b5 X/Y, b6 X/Y), so the ranking turns on details.
- Doubling every field rarely broke anything: plates grow, titles wrap, b2-X truncates with an ellipsis. The one on-air clip came from a real long question (b4-Z 05).
- The most common operability failure: for any graphic with more than one row of actions, the panel puts declared buttons and live-number steppers below the 900 px fold. That hits all hockey and quiz cells; only Take/Update/Next/Out and the first action row are reachable.
- Scores, periods and question numbers are steppers rather than typed labels, which is right, but they are never reachable without scrolling.
- Long variable text inside table rows is handled inconsistently: clubs shrink (b6 X/Y) or wrap (b6 Z) instead of using a set truncation or condensed fallback.
- Hockey state logic is the weakest behaviour: in Y/Z the period lags one press after Next period, and no frame shows the power-play countdown moving.
- Fixed choices named in a brief are left as free text (the ticker's UUTISET/TIEDOTE), which forces label typing.
