## Verdict

**`revise`**

The candidate plan gets the core conceptual split right—separating **live graphic feeds** from **operator-staged cue snapshots**—and rightly drops native social integrations and Twitch work. However, **its execution plan is upside-down and contains an unresolved architectural contradiction**:
1. **Urgent student/operator value is buried behind 14 refactor phases and server infrastructure.** Issue `#1313` (unsafe message fallback onto scoreboards/poll graphics) and manual paste-to-graphic wait until Phases 21–23, behind 14 `ProductionPage.tsx` refactor phases (5 of which the spec itself marks optional) and server cron/RSS/API phases.
2. **Pool-graphic bindings and `/api/data/update` contradict cue-snapshot isolation.** In NoaCG's runtime, both `Show.bindings` (`src/components/home/ProductionPage.tsx:1654-1658`) and `POST /api/data/update` (`api/_lib/dataIngest.ts:133-141`, `supabase/migrations/0047_production_data_key.sql:95-97`) address the **pool graphic name**, not an individual cue. Promising that two cues can share one pool graphic while one is live-bound/API-updated and the other is a protected message snapshot (`docs/work-specs/audience-flow/spec.md:165-171`, Phase 16) breaks at the renderer level (`control_events`) unless pool graphic instances have explicit role separation.
3. **YouTube Video Comments (`commentThreads.list`) and Live Chat (`liveChatMessages.list`) are conflated**, and **Standings graphics require a two-field contract (`f2` rows + `f3` headers)** that single-field bindings cannot express.

Cut the refactor prerequisite to the **3 phases that actually touch data/cue editing**, fix `#1313` and ship **manual paste + native list/message staging first**, enforce **pool-graphic-level snapshot isolation**, and compress the 26 phases into a **10-phase outcome-ordered plan**.

2. **Message staging (`src/components/home/ProductionAudienceWorkspace.tsx:547-618`) is already a Cue Snapshot builder—but with broken targeting:**

#### Evaluating Two Competing Product Models

| Dimension | **Model A (Recommended): Field-Led Playout + Inline Content Drawer** | **Model B (Candidate Plan): Unified "Data" Workspace Tab + Separate "Messages" Tab** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary operator entry point** | **Inside the Playout Cue Editor (`ProductionPage.tsx:5089-5213`)** plus a slide-over/docked **Messages & Lists** tray on the Playout page. No `target="_blank"` tab switch required during a live show. | Top-bar navigation tabs (`Playout`, `Data`, `Messages`, `Manage`) that swap the entire workspace or open separate views (`docs/work-specs/data-sources/spec.md:94-110`). |

#### Our Independent Proposal Summary
1. **Two explicit modes per graphic, never mixed on the same pool graphic instance:**
   - **Snapshot Cue ("Pick / Paste & Prepare"):** Used for **Messages** (Clipboard paste, Sheet message inbox, YouTube comments/chat, Join page) and **Single-Row Table Picks** (Guest lower-third, Team match card). Produces a static `ShowCue.values` snapshot.
   - **Live Graphic Feed ("Connect Live"):** Used for **Lists** (Ticker, Schedule, Standings, End Credits from a Google Sheet tab or RSS feed) and **Live Values** (Scoreboards/Timers via Controls or `/api/data/patch`). Writes to `Show.data` under a source-owned namespace (`sources.<sourceId>.*`) and binds the target graphic's fields.
2. **Pool-Graphic Isolation Rule:** A pool graphic (`Show.graphics[]`) with live bindings (or API `/update` access) cannot simultaneously host a protected message snapshot on the same bound fields. When staging a message, NoaCG targets an **unbound** message graphic in the pool (or offers one-click **Add Message Card** / **Duplicate Graphic for Messages**).
3. **Four format-aware list adapters (`ticker`, `schedule`, `standings`, `credits`)** that work identically whether the operator **pastes cells/lines directly in the browser** (zero Google account needed) or **links a Google Sheet tab**.

## 2. Explicit Answers to the 8 Required Review Questions

### Q1. Should NoaCG use one content model or separate Data and Messages views?
- **Use one underlying data/staging model, surfaced through task-specific entry points (not two separate setup worlds, and not a forced single generic form).**
- Specifically:
  - **Under the hood**, there are only two primitives already in the codebase:
    1. **Cue Values (`ShowCue.values` in `src/model/shows.ts:172-185`):** Static key-value snapshots per rundown cue.
    2. **Bound Graphic Data (`Show.data` + `Show.bindings` in `src/model/productionData.ts:18-24`):** Live values keyed by graphic and field.
  - **In the UI**, operators think by **task**:
    - **In Playout (Cue Editor):** Every cue shows either *"Editing cue values (Snapshot)"* with buttons for **Paste Message** / **Pick Row**, or *"Connected to Live Source: [Sheet: Standings]"* with **Preview / Pause / Disconnect**.
    - **Messages Inbox (Drawer or Tab):** A focused moderation tray where operators paste a message from WhatsApp/SMS/X or pick from a Sheet/YouTube/Join inbox, edit the broadcast text/author, see the live graphic preview, and click **Add Cue to Rundown**.
    - **Live Data & Lists View:** Where operators connect a Ticker, Schedule, Standings, Credits, or Scoreboard to a Native List Editor, Google Sheet, RSS feed, or Data API.
  - Why this beats both extremes: A Google Sheet URL pasted in *either* place uses the same URL parser (`parseGoogleSheetUrl`) and fetcher, choosing only whether its rows feed a **Live Graphic** or the **Messages Inbox**.

### Q2. What is the simplest message workflow from copy/paste, Sheet, audience join, or YouTube to an on-air graphic?
1. **Arrive in one uniform candidate shape:** `{ id, body, author?, sourceLabel?, reference?, rawText }` — whether created by pasting text in the Quick-Paste box, reading a row from a Google Sheet (`Name | Message | Source | Reference`), receiving a Join page submission (`src/audience/localAudience.ts`), or clicking a YouTube comment/chat item.
2. **Select & Edit in one Moderation Card:**
   - Operator sees **Original text** (read-only reference) and edits **Broadcast Message** (required) + **Broadcast Name** (optional; one-click **Make Anonymous** clears the name field).
   - Private fields (`Reference`, phone/handle, row notes, internal IDs) are **never** mapped to graphic fields.
3. **Preview on an explicit Message Graphic:**
   - Dropdown defaults to the production's designated message graphic (`graphic.messageTarget === true` or explicit operator choice, never falling back to `show.graphics[0]`).
   - Live graphic preview renders the exact `Broadcast Message` and `Broadcast Name` before staging.
4. **Add to Rundown (Snapshot) -> Take:**
   - Clicking **Add Cue to Rundown** writes a new `ShowCue` with static `values: { [bodyField]: broadcastBody, [authorField]: broadcastName }` and records `stagedFrom: { sourceId, candidateKey, revisionHash }`.
   - Operator clicks **Preview** or **Take** on the rundown cue to put it on air.

### Q4. Where does the candidate model break for snapshots, shared graphics, live bindings, duplicate actions, or ID-less Sheets?
It breaks in **four specific places** verified in the code:
1. **Shared Pool Graphic + `/api/data/update` Overwrites Snapshot Cues:**
   - In `api/_lib/dataIngest.ts:133-141` (`resolveTargetGraphic`), `POST /api/data/update` resolves `{ cue: "cue-live" }` or `{ graphic: "g1" }` to the **pool graphic ID (`g1`)** and calls `control_data_send(p_key, p_graphic, p_data)` (`supabase/migrations/0047_production_data_key.sql:95-97`).
   - That RPC inserts `{ graphic: "g1", msg: { t: "update", data: { f0: "..." } } }` directly into `control_events`.
   - If Cue 1 (live-bound or API-targeted) and Cue 2 (staged viewer message snapshot) share pool graphic `g1`, any `/api/data/update` targeting Cue 1 or `g1` while Cue 2 is on air **immediately overwrites Cue 2's on-air text in the output renderer**!
   - **Fix:** Enforce **pool-graphic-level separation**: a pool graphic instance used for message snapshots (`messageTarget: true`) cannot have live bindings on its message/author fields and is rejected by `resolveTargetGraphic` in `/api/data/update` (`409 graphic_is_snapshot_only`). If the operator picks a graphic that already has live bindings, NoaCG clones a dedicated unbound graphic instance (`"Lower Third (Messages)"`) in `Show.graphics`.
2. **Shared Pool Graphic + `withBoundValues` Overwrites Cue Values on Take:**
   - In `src/components/home/ProductionPage.tsx:1654-1658`, `withBoundValues(graphic, values)` merges `resolvedRef.current[graphic]` over `cue.values` for **every cue** referencing `graphic`. Pool-graphic separation eliminates this conflict cleanly without per-cue binding exceptions.
3. **ID-less Sheet Reordering vs. Edits:**
   - If a Sheet has no `ID` column, using row number (`row 2`, `row 3`) breaks when a student sorts the Sheet or inserts a row at the top.
   - **Fix:** Compute `contentHash = sha256(trim(Name) + "\0" + trim(Message) + "\0" + trim(Source) + "\0" + trim(Reference))` plus an `occurrenceIndex` (`0, 1, ...` for identical duplicate rows within the same fetch).
   - When rows are **reordered or sorted**, `(contentHash, occurrenceIndex)` is unchanged—inbox status (`Staged in Cue #4`) stays attached to the right message.
   - When a row's text is **edited in the Sheet** after staging, its `contentHash` changes: the already-staged cue in the rundown keeps its snapshot untouched, and the edited Sheet row appears in the inbox with a subtle badge (`Updated row / New text`) so the operator can choose whether to update the existing cue or ignore it.
4. **Duplicate Clicks / Network Retries When Staging a Message:**
   - Today, clicking `Send to rundown` (`src/components/home/ProductionAudienceWorkspace.tsx:582-618`) always appends `newCueId = createId('cue')` (`line 592`). A double-click or retry creates duplicate cues.
   - **Fix:** Store `stagedFrom: { sourceId, candidateKey, revisionHash }` on `ShowCue`. If an operator clicks **Add to Rundown** (or retries) for a `(sourceId, candidateKey)` that already has a rundown cue in the current show, focus/update that existing cue (with an explicit secondary button **"Add as Another Cue"** if they intentionally want it twice in the rundown).

### Q6. What YouTube modes should be built now vs. deferred, and why?
Verified against current `src/audience/youtubeChat.ts:58-157` and Google's official YouTube Data API v3 specification:
- **Current code reality:** `youtubeChat.ts` (`lines 75-96`) calls `videos?part=liveStreamingDetails` and requires `activeLiveChatId`. It **fails with an error on every regular YouTube video** (`"This video has no active live chat"`). On live streams, it calls `liveChat/messages` (`5 quota units/call`), **discards the entire first page of messages** (`firstPage = true`, `lines 66, 105-108`) so any message posted before the operator clicked Connect is lost forever, and holds the API key only in React state (`src/components/home/ProductionAudienceWorkspace.tsx:161`).
- **Build Now (Mode 1 — High Priority): YouTube Video Comments (`commentThreads.list`)**
  - **Endpoint:** `GET https://www.googleapis.com/youtube/v3/commentThreads?part=snippet&videoId={videoId}&maxResults=50&order=time&textFormat=plainText`
  - **Why build now:** Costs only **1 quota unit per request** (5x cheaper than live chat), works on **any public YouTube video** (including pre-announcement videos where viewers post questions before a student show or recorded segments), requires only an API key (no OAuth), and **must return the first page immediately** as selectable message candidates.
  - **Polling model:** Manual **"Fetch / Refresh Comments"** button plus optional **30s / 60s** browser poll while open (<= 120 quota units/hour, < 1.5% of the 10,000/day default quota).
- **Build Now (Mode 2 — Contained Fix): Browser-Owned YouTube Live Chat (`liveChatMessages.list`)**
  - Keep existing browser-polled `liveChat/messages` for active live broadcasts, but **fix three bugs**:
    1. **Do NOT discard the first page (`lines 105-108`)**: load the initial page into the moderation list (marked as recent history) so operators see messages sent right before connecting.
    2. **Respect `pollingIntervalMillis` with a floor of 5,000–10,000 ms** and show a clear quota indicator (`~5 units/poll`).
    3. **Persist the video URL/ID in production config and the operator's API key in browser `localStorage`** (never in exported show JSON or unencrypted `Show` records) so a browser refresh during a live show doesn't wipe the connection.
- **Defer:**
  - **Closed-page server-polled YouTube Live Chat:** Polling `liveChatMessages.list` every 5s from a server costs **3,600 quota units/hour** per stream (exhausting a 10,000/day project quota in 2.7 hours) and requires server-side encrypted user credential vaults. And because a human operator **must have the browser open to moderate, preview, and Take messages anyway** ("Nothing a viewer sends should air automatically"), closed-page background ingestion of YouTube chat adds server complexity and quota burn with almost zero practical benefit.
  - **All new Twitch work:** Keep existing `src/audience/twitchChat.ts` untouched so existing users aren't broken; defer any persistent Twitch source work.

### Q7. How should Pause, empty content, failures, and server ownership work without races?
1. **Failed Reads Hold Last Good Content:**
   - If a Sheet fetch (`429`, `5xx`, network error, or HTML sign-in page instead of CSV) or RSS fetch fails, the runner records `{ status: 'error', lastError, lastFailedAt }` on source health and **does not patch `Show.data`**. On-air graphics keep their last valid values.
2. **Valid Empty Live Content Clears Bound Fields (Fixing the `[]` / `null` SQL Trap):**
   - **Codebase bug found:** In `src/model/productionData.ts:242-254` (`formatValue`) and `supabase/migrations/0060_operator_data_patch.sql:29-41, 91-102`, an empty array `[]` formats to `null`, and a `null` patch deletes the key from `v_after`. Because `0060_operator_data_patch.sql:97-100` uses `from production_data_resolve(v_after, v_bindings) n left join ... o`, deleting a key produces **zero rows in `n`** and therefore emits **no `control_events` update**—leaving stale text stuck on air!
   - **Fix:** Without changing legacy `[]` or `null` behavior for external callers, managed list formatters (`rowsToFields`) must emit **`""` (empty string)** (not `[]` or `null`) to their bound path (`sources.<id>.f0 = ""`) when a Sheet tab or RSS feed is validly empty (0 data rows). Because `""` is a non-null string in `production_data_resolve(v_after, v_bindings)`, the SQL diff (`o.val is distinct from n.val`) cleanly emits `{ t: 'update', data: { f0: '' } }` to clear the on-air graphic!
3. **Pause Across Polled Sources, `/api/data/patch`, and `/api/data/update` Without Races:**
   - **Why the candidate plan's held-write merge is risky:** `POST /api/data/patch` mutates `control_shows.data` (JSON tree), whereas `POST /api/data/update` (`control_data_send` in `supabase/migrations/0047_production_data_key.sql:66-99`) writes raw graphic field IDs (`{ f0: "val" }`) directly into `control_events` without touching `control_shows.data`. Holding both in separate structures during Pause makes ordering between `/patch` and `/update` ambiguous on Resume.
   - **Cleanest Race-Free Contract:**
     - **For Polled Sources (Sheet, RSS):** Pause simply stops applying fetched snapshots to `Show.data` (or stores the latest fetched value in `source.heldSnapshot` and applies it in one `patchProductionData` call on Resume).
     - **For Data API (`/patch` and `/update`):** Store `data_paused: boolean` and `held_data_patch: jsonb` on `control_shows`.
       - While `data_paused = true`, `control_data_patch` merges incoming patches into `held_data_patch` using the exact same `jsonb_merge_patch` function (`supabase/migrations/0060_operator_data_patch.sql:7-26`), preserving partial patches and explicit `null` deletions in arrival order, and returns `{ ok: true, paused: true, held: true }` without touching `control_shows.data` or `control_events`.
       - For legacy `POST /api/data/update` (`control_data_send`), also store `held_graphic_updates: jsonb` (`{ "<graphic>": { "<fieldId>": "<value>" } }`, merged per graphic in arrival order) and apply `held_data_patch` followed by `held_graphic_updates` atomically inside one database transaction on `Resume`.
       - **Manual operator edits and scoreboard controls** (`patchProductionData` via authenticated operator session / `control_command`) continue to apply immediately to `control_shows.data` even while external API ingest is paused.
4. **Persisting Connection Changes Without a Hidden Republish Step:**
   - Currently, `control_shows.data_bindings` is only updated when the operator runs a full publish (`src/control/live.ts:249`).
   - Add a lightweight authenticated operator RPC `control_update_data_config(p_show_id, p_bindings, p_sources, p_data)` that updates `control_shows.data_bindings` (and `data`) immediately whenever an operator connects, edits, pauses, or disconnects a source on a published production—no full graphic re-bundle/republish required.

### W7. Edge Case: No Compatible Message Graphic in the Production
1. **Scenario:** A student production only has a `Scoreboard` (`sb01`) and a `Poll / Vote Board` (`poll01`). The operator opens the Message Tray and tries to paste a WhatsApp message.
2. **What happens (Replacing Issue `#1313`):**
   - NoaCG does **not** select `Scoreboard` or `Poll / Vote Board`.
   - Instead, the Destination Graphic area shows: *"No message graphic in this production yet (Scoreboards and Vote boards cannot display text messages)."*
   - Right below is a single primary button: **"+ Add Message Lower Third to Production"**.
3. **Recovery:** Clicking that button adds a pre-configured, unbound Message Lower Third graphic to `show.graphics`, previews the pasted message in it immediately, and lets the operator click **Add to Rundown**. Total recovery time: **1 click (< 5 seconds)**.

### W8. Edge Case: Two Prepared Messages Using One Graphic Template
1. **Scenario:** Operator stages Message A (`"Great show! — Pekka"`) into Cue #3, and immediately stages Message B (`"Who plays next? — Liisa"`) into Cue #4, both using `Message Lower Third`.
2. **What happens:**
   - Because `Message Lower Third` is an **unbound snapshot graphic**, Cue #3 stores `{ f0: "Great show!", f1: "Pekka" }` and Cue #4 stores `{ f0: "Who plays next?", f1: "Liisa" }`.
   - Taking Cue #3 airs Pekka's message. While Cue #3 is on air, the operator can edit Cue #4 in the Cue Editor (or Preview Cue #4) without altering Cue #3 on Program output. Taking Cue #4 transitions cleanly to Liisa's message.
3. **What if the operator later tries to attach a Live Sheet Ticker/Feed to `Message Lower Third`?**
   - NoaCG detects that `Message Lower Third` is used by prepared message snapshot cues (Cue #3 and Cue #4) and prompts: *"Cue #3 and Cue #4 use this graphic for prepared messages. Create a separate graphic copy for this live connection?"* — preventing live bindings from ever clobbering Cue #3 or Cue #4.

### W9. Edge Case: Source Changes or Disappears After Staging or While On Air
1. **Scenario:** Operator stages Row 4 from a Google Sheet (`"Can you play Taylor Swift? — Anna"`) into Cue #5 and Takes Cue #5 to air. Five seconds later, a student helper deletes Row 4 in Google Sheets (or rewrites it to `"NEVER MIND"`).
2. **What happens on air:** **Nothing changes on Program output.** Cue #5 is a static snapshot in `ShowCue.values` on an unbound graphic; no live binding or `control_events` update fires.
3. **What the operator sees in the Message Tray:**
   - If Row 4 was **deleted** in the Sheet: Cue #5 in the rundown remains completely intact and on air; in the Message Tray, the staged receipt notes *"Staged in Cue #5 (removed from source Sheet)"*.
   - If Row 4 was **edited** in the Sheet: Cue #5 on air remains untouched; the Message Tray shows the new text with an explicit action **"Update Cue #5 with new text"** or **"Add as new cue"** so changes only happen when the operator chooses.

## 5. Ranked Most Consequential Risks & Contradictions

### Risk 1 (Critical): Graphic-Addressed Live Bindings and `/api/data/update` Will Overwrite Prepared/On-Air Message Snapshots Sharing a Pool Graphic
- **Failure Example:** An operator has one lower-third graphic `g1` (`"Lower Third"`) in `Show.graphics`. Cue 1 uses `g1` with a live binding (or an external script sends `POST /api/data/update` with `{ "graphic": "Lower Third", "data": { "f0": "Host Name" } }` or `{ "cue": "cue-1", "data": { "f0": "Host Name" } }`). The operator stages a viewer message into Cue 2 on the same graphic `g1` and Takes Cue 2 to air. Two seconds later, the external sender (or live binding resolution) fires: **Cue 2's on-air viewer message is immediately replaced by `"Host Name"` on the broadcast output.**
- **Code & Spec Evidence (`7313cd859b1ac0d6b6fb18d12526c2d149128586`):**

### Risk 3 (High): Clearing a Live Sheet or RSS Feed Cannot Clear On-Air Graphics Because `[]` and `null` Emit No `control_events` Update in SQL
- **Failure Example:** An operator links a Google Sheet to an on-air Ticker (`f0`). During the show, they clear the data rows in the Sheet so the ticker text is empty. The Sheet poller sends `[]` or `null` to `control_data_patch`. **The old headlines remain stuck on the on-air Ticker forever.**
- **Code & Spec Evidence (`7313cd859b1ac0d6b6fb18d12526c2d149128586`):**
  - `src/model/productionData.ts:242-254`: `formatValue([])` returns `null` when `items.length === 0`.
  - `supabase/migrations/0060_operator_data_patch.sql:29-41`: `production_data_format_value('[]'::jsonb)` returns `null`.
  - `supabase/migrations/0060_operator_data_patch.sql:91-102`: `from public.production_data_resolve(v_after, v_bindings) n left join public.production_data_resolve(v_before, v_bindings) o ...`. When a path is `[]` or deleted via `null`, `production_data_resolve(v_after, v_bindings)` omits that field row (`where val is not null`, `line 70`), so the `left join` from `n` to `o` produces **no row** for the cleared field and emits **no `update` event** to `control_events`!
- **Smallest Fix / Design Decision:** Keep legacy `[]` and `null` behavior unchanged for existing API callers, but have all managed list formatters (`rowsToFields` for Ticker, Schedule, Standings, Credits) write **`""` (empty string)** to their bound `Show.data` path when a live source returns 0 valid items. Because `""` is a non-null string in JSONB, `production_data_resolve(v_after, v_bindings)` outputs `val = ""`, which is distinct from `o.val` and emits `{ t: 'update', data: { f0: '' } }` to clear the on-air graphic.
- **Verification Case:** Populate a bound Ticker via `control_data_patch` with 3 items, then patch the source's formatted path with `""`; verify `control_events` receives `{ t: 'update', data: { f0: '' } }` and `resolveProductionBindings` resolves `{ f0: '' }`.

## 6. Prototype & User-Trial Plan (Before Expensive Infrastructure)

Before building server schedulers, database migrations, or 14 refactor phases, run a **2-day clickable UI prototype trial** (using local browser state and `/api/data/sheet-fetch`) with **3–5 students or non-technical operators**.

### 6.1 Critique & Refinement of the Proposed Timing Targets
The candidate specs (`docs/work-specs/audience-flow/spec.md:190-191`, `docs/work-specs/sheet-data-flow/spec.md:274-275`) propose:
- **Clipboard message to air in `< 2 minutes`**
- **Sheet message to air in `< 5 minutes`**

**Critique:**
1. **`< 2 minutes` for a pasted message is too loose for live broadcast playout** (where a host asks for a viewer reaction and needs it in 30–60 seconds), **and it fails to distinguish first-time setup from repeat operation** or test what happens when no message graphic is in the show yet.
2. **`< 5 minutes` for a Sheet message conflates Google account/Sheet creation time with NoaCG UI time.** If a student already has a link-shared Sheet URL, taking 4 minutes inside NoaCG means the UI is still too confusing.
3. **Neither target tests error recovery or snapshot safety** (the exact failure modes that hurt students on air).

## 7. Remaining Decisions, Measured Facts vs. Assumptions, Confidence & Limitations

### 7.1 Explicit Remaining Decisions (With Recommended Defaults)

1. **Where should the Message Tray live during a show?**
   - **Recommendation:** Make it a **collapsible drawer/panel inside the Playout workspace** (in addition to being reachable from the top bar), rather than only a full-screen tab replacement.
   - *Tradeoff:* Uses ~360px of horizontal space when open on Playout, but lets a single operator paste/moderate a message while still seeing their Program/Preview monitors and rundown.
2. **How should pool-graphic snapshot protection handle a production where the operator only has one lower-third graphic used for both live-bound host names and viewer messages?**
   - **Recommendation:** Auto-clone a second pool graphic instance (`"Lower Third (Messages)"`, unbound) on one click when setting up Message Staging.
   - *Tradeoff:* Adds one entry to `Show.graphics`, which completely eliminates every runtime collision between `Show.bindings`, `/api/data/update`, renderer iframes, and static message cues.
3. **How should 5-second Google Sheet polling be hosted when the operator's browser tab is closed?**
   - **Recommendation:** In Phase R5, run 5-second polling from any active operator browser **or** active cloud output renderer (`/output` page) via a cached `/api/data/sheet-fetch` proxy. Defer standalone server-cron background polling until Phase R10+.
   - *Tradeoff:* Avoids building a sub-minute server scheduler across all published shows, while still working with the operator's control page closed because the **output renderer (`src/output/`) is always open in OBS/vMix while a show is on air**!

### 7.2 Measured Repository Facts vs. Unmeasured Operational Assumptions

- **Verified Facts (from `7313cd859b1ac0d6b6fb18d12526c2d149128586` & Primary Docs):**
  - `Show.bindings` (`src/model/productionData.ts:24`) and `POST /api/data/update` (`api/_lib/dataIngest.ts:133-141`, `supabase/migrations/0047_production_data_key.sql:95-97`) are graphic-addressed, not cue-addressed.
  - Empty arrays `[]` and `null` patches in `supabase/migrations/0060_operator_data_patch.sql:29-41, 91-102` do not emit a clearing `update` event to `control_events`; `""` (empty string) does.
  - `src/components/home/ProductionAudienceWorkspace.tsx:609-618` falls back to `show.graphics[0]` and its first `textfield` when no message field matches (`#1313`), and `pollFieldMap` (`lines 508-534`) misses poll graphics without standard option field names.
  - `src/audience/youtubeChat.ts:66, 105-108` only supports `liveChat/messages` (5 quota units/call) and discards the first page of messages; `commentThreads.list` costs 1 quota unit/call and works on standard YouTube videos.
  - Standings templates (`src/templates/competition/results/shared.ts:153-233`) require coordinated updates to both `f2` (rows) and `f3` (column headers); End Credits (`src/templates/endCredits/shared.ts:192-196`) require preserved interior blank lines for section breaks; Tickers (`src/templates/tickers/shared.ts:145`) split on `: ` into kicker badges.
- **Unmeasured Assumptions (Must Be Measured in Phase R5/R7 Acceptance):**
  - **Google Sheets `/export?format=csv` 5-second polling latency and rate limits:** The "<10s visible update over 20 real edits and 1 hour of 5s polling" target is an unmeasured hypothesis; Google CDN caching and `429` thresholds on unauthenticated `/export?format=csv` URLs must be benchmarked with a real harness before promising "<10s" in user-facing copy.
  - **Operator completion times (`<= 90s` paste, `<= 4m` Sheet):** Must be validated with 3–5 real students in the prototype trial (Section 6.2).
