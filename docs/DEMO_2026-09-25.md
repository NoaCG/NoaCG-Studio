# The 2026-09-25 session - the script, and the gap list it is

**What this is.** The ordered beats of the 25 September session: students and YLE people trying
NoaCG, hands on. For every beat: who drives, the exact route in the product, and the evidence that
it works today or the word GAP with the next step named. §7 lists every beat that is not WORKS,
with what closing it costs and who closes it; the two weeks before the date are planned from that
list. It is written to be read cold by the owner on a phone, and by a session on the morning of
the 25th.

**The owner's brief** is the 2026-09-09 ruling in `docs/OWNER_RULINGS.md`, paraphrased from
speech, so the intent binds and the wording does not. The 12th is no longer the pressure. The
date is the 25th. We owe one presentation and one step-by-step guide over two capabilities: SVG
import ending in their own graphics playing on their systems, and the CLI taking a graphic from
Codex or Claude Code to the NoaCG player in minutes.

**Status legend, used in every table.** `docs/PROMISE_AUDIT.md` grades what the product may
claim; this column grades a BEAT of this session, which is a different question: can it be run on
the day, and has a person seen it run.

| word | meaning | who closes it |
|---|---|---|
| WORKS | on `main`, pinned by a spec, and a person has driven it on the surface the beat uses | nobody |
| UNSEEN (eyes) | on `main` and pinned; the owner has not looked at it on this surface since the code last moved | a walk (`docs/acceptance/OWNER_QUEUE.md`, `kind: walk`) |
| UNSEEN (box) | on `main` and pinned; never run on the real playout hardware the beat names | a box (`kind: hardware`) |
| GAP | not there, or nothing evidences it | the row in §7 says |
| PARTLY | driven on the surface the beat uses, and something IN it is broken or unmeasured | the row in §7 says |

**Three notes on the words, all learned on 2026-09-10.** First, "a person has driven it" excludes
a green spec and a green build; it does NOT exclude a session that ran the real commands on the
real surface. That is how B5 became WORKS, and how R2.1, R2.4 and R2.5 stopped being UNSEEN
(eyes) - they were never really eyes rows, because what they were missing was a RUN, not the
owner's taste. **UNSEEN (eyes) is for a beat whose open question is whether he likes what he
sees**, and no session's run closes one of those. Second, PARTLY exists because the day needs the
difference between "we have not looked" and "we looked and found something": a PARTLY beat runs in
the room with a caveat its §7 row spells out. Third, a GAP normally has a §7 row, but **a GAP whose
cell says OFF THE LEDGER has none**, because the owner has told us to stop tracking it; B0 is the
only beat in that state.

Every beat table has the same six columns: number, beat, who drives, route, evidence, status.
On a phone the tables read one column at a time, which is why the who column is never pushed
into a heading. **The status column is hand-maintained.** Nothing in the repo rewrites it when an
acceptance box is ticked or an owner-queue file is deleted, so each UNSEEN and GAP row names, in
§7, the mechanism that really settles it, and §8 says who edits the cell.

## 0. The shape, and the six calls it rests on

The brief says "trying NoaCG", so this is a follow-along session and not a demonstration. That
one word decides most of the shape. The owner-queue item for this script carries each call with
the sentence that reverts it.

1. **They drive.** Every beat a participant can do on their own laptop is theirs. We drive only
   the beats that need a playout box or a coding-agent subscription some of them will not have.
2. **Both roads end at the same place, so it is taught once.** An imported SVG and an agent-made
   graphic both become a library record, both go into a production, and both air through one
   output URL. Road 1 and road 2 stop at the library; §5 takes the production to air once, for
   both.
3. **Road 1 goes first.** It needs no terminal, no subscription and no account until the
   production is published, so the whole room can do it. Road 2 needs Node and, for its full
   form, an agent subscription.
4. **On the day, air stops at the NoaCG player in the cloud** (owner, 2026-09-10). A graphic is
   imported and played on our own hosted player, and seeing it work there is the proof the room
   gets. No OBS on student laptops, no CasparCG box, no playout configuration of any kind. Real
   playout is a separate studio session later. The bring-your-box beats stay written in §5 so one
   can be run if somebody asks, but none is on the clock and none gates the day. This replaces
   the earlier call, which prepared four targets and chose between them on the day.
5. **The step-by-step guide is `/docs`, not a second document.** A guide that lives beside the
   product drifts, and the encore under §3 names a pair that already do. What is printed is a
   one-page index into the pages, in beat order.
   **Two versions of that page** (owner, 2026-09-10): one for the students, written to be handed
   out on the day and shared afterwards as a file, and one for the owner, which is the running
   order with the beats and who drives each. Same page, two audiences; G2 builds both.
6. **The room draws in small groups, and finishes at home** (owner, 2026-09-10). The drawing is
   done in Illustrator, in small groups rather than one file per student. Each group takes away
   two pieces to finish in their own time and send in: **a lower-third quiz template and a
   scoreboard**. What happens inside the session is deliberately smaller - one simple graphic, a
   lower third, imported and played - because the session proves the road works and the homework
   is where the artwork gets made.

**Two sibling rows of the 2026-09-09 day wave were running while this was written**, and both
landed the same day. `claude/a-cli-minutes-to-air` (pull request 190) timed the CLI path from an
empty folder to a graphic on air; its number is in R2.5. `claude/d-import-road-guide` (pull
request 191) walked the SVG import road as a stranger and wrote the end-to-end `/docs` page; it is
`/docs#first-graphic`, in G1. The deck, `docs/presentation-2026-09-25/NoaCG-2026-09-25.pptx`,
was built from this file after both had landed, and rebuilt on 2026-09-10 against calls 4, 5 and
6 when they made three of its slides wrong (O2).

**The 2026-09-12 student production** (quiz and scoreboard, drawn by students) still happens, and
it is the dress rehearsal for road 1. It is no longer the day the hardware rows get ticked. That
was the plan until 2026-09-10, when the owner put those ticks at "a few weeks at least"
(`docs/OWNER_RULINGS.md`), which is on or past the 25th itself. **So plan §5 as if no box is
ticked before the day**: §7 rows 3 and 10 each say what happens without one, and nothing waits
on the 12th to find out. What the 12th still settles is row 11, the owner's eyes on his own quiz
board. Row 2, the output URL on a real CasparCG, left the list on 2026-09-10 for the opposite
reason: a session ran it on both servers on this laptop, so A4 is WORKS.

**Timing.** About ninety minutes, re-cut 2026-09-10 for calls 4 and 6: 10 opening, 20 road 1 (a
simple lower third, imported and played on the NoaCG player), 25 drawing in small groups, 25 road
2, 10 on air and the close. Air is short now because it ends at our own player instead of at four
targets, and the time that frees goes to the groups. Whoever runs the clock cuts a beat that runs
long at the next beat's boundary and never stretches it.
