# The 2026-09-25 session - the script, and the gap list it is

**What this is.** The ordered beats of the 25 September session: students and YLE people trying
NoaCG, hands on. For every beat: who drives, the exact route in the product, and the evidence that
it works today or the word GAP with the next step named. §7 lists every beat that is not WORKS,
with what closing it costs and who closes it; the two weeks before the date are planned from that
list. It is written to be read cold by the owner on a phone, and by a session on the morning of
the 25th.

**The owner's brief** is the 2026-09-09 ruling in `docs/OWNER_RULINGS.md`, paraphrased from
speech, so the intent binds and the wording does not. The 12th is no longer the pressure. The
date is the 25th. We owe one presentation and one step-by-step guide over two capabilities: SVG
import ending in their own graphics playing on their systems, and the CLI taking a graphic from
Codex or Claude Code to the NoaCG player in minutes.

**Status legend, used in every table.** `docs/PROMISE_AUDIT.md` grades what the product may
claim; this column grades a BEAT of this session, which is a different question: can it be run on
the day, and has a person seen it run.

| word | meaning | who closes it |
|---|---|---|
| WORKS | on `main`, pinned by a spec, and a person has driven it on the surface the beat uses | nobody |
| UNSEEN (eyes) | on `main` and pinned; the owner has not looked at it on this surface since the code last moved | a walk (`docs/acceptance/OWNER_QUEUE.md`, `kind: walk`) |
| UNSEEN (box) | on `main` and pinned; never run on the real playout hardware the beat names | a box (`kind: hardware`) |
| GAP | not there, or nothing evidences it | the row in §7 says |

Every beat table has the same six columns: number, beat, who drives, route, evidence, status.
On a phone the tables read one column at a time, which is why the who column is never pushed
into a heading. **The status column is hand-maintained.** Nothing in the repo rewrites it when an
acceptance box is ticked or an owner-queue file is deleted, so each UNSEEN and GAP row names, in
§7, the mechanism that really settles it, and §8 says who edits the cell.

## 0. The shape, and the five calls it rests on

The brief says "trying NoaCG", so this is a follow-along session and not a demonstration. That
one word decides most of the shape. The owner-queue item for this script carries each call with
the sentence that reverts it.

1. **They drive.** Every beat a participant can do on their own laptop is theirs. We drive only
   the beats that need a playout box or a coding-agent subscription some of them will not have.
2. **Both roads end at the same place, so it is taught once.** An imported SVG and an agent-made
   graphic both become a library record, both go into a production, and both air through one
   output URL. Road 1 and road 2 stop at the library; §5 takes the production to air once, for
   both.
3. **Road 1 goes first.** It needs no terminal, no subscription and no account until the
   production is published, so the whole room can do it. Road 2 needs Node and, for its full
   form, an agent subscription.
4. **"Their systems" is four targets, prepared in advance, chosen on the day.** A browser source
   loading the output URL is the one every student can do at their desk and it is the one the
   evidence is strongest on. CasparCG 2.3 with the URL, CasparCG with a file, and an OGraf
   renderer are bring-your-box beats; each carries its own risk in §5.
5. **The step-by-step guide is `/docs`, not a second document.** A guide that lives beside the
   product drifts, and the encore under §3 names a pair that already do. What is printed is a
   one-page index into the pages, in beat order. The owner asked for "a step-by-step guide"; this
   reads that as the pages plus the index, and the queue item says how to say otherwise.

**Two sibling rows of the 2026-09-09 day wave were running while this was written**, and both
landed the same day. `claude/a-cli-minutes-to-air` (pull request 190) timed the CLI path from an
empty folder to a graphic on air; its number is in R2.5. `claude/d-import-road-guide` (pull
request 191) walked the SVG import road as a stranger and wrote the end-to-end `/docs` page; it is
`/docs#first-graphic`, in G1. The deck, `docs/presentation-2026-09-25/NoaCG-2026-09-25.pptx`,
was built from this file after both had landed (O2).

**The 2026-09-12 student production** (quiz and scoreboard, drawn by students) still happens. It is
the dress rehearsal for road 1, and every UNSEEN (box) row in §5 gets its first tick there or not
at all before the 25th.

**Timing.** About ninety minutes: 10 opening, 30 road 1, 25 road 2, 20 on air, 5 close. Whoever
runs the clock cuts a beat that runs long at the next beat's boundary and never stretches it.
