# Create with AI step restructure, 2026-10-14

The `wizard-ai` plan (`docs/work-specs/wizard-ai/spec.md`) moved the AI step's run chain into
`src/ai/creation.ts`, quarantined the Pro markup in `ProPanel.tsx` and moved the step from
`src/components/wizard/steps/` to `src/components/wizard/ai/`. These are its sizes before and
after. Phase evidence: `docs/work-specs/wizard-ai/evidence/phase-1.md` to `phase-5.md`.

edited file's folder, LF-normalised, plus 2 bytes per join. Target: 40,960 bytes
(`docs/METRICS.md`). A1 to A3 changed no contract (spec D8), so "before" is the A4 builder's
measurement on `a5992ed97`.
