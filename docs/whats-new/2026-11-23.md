## Playout and Bridge

- OSC cues: A rundown cue sends an OSC message to a lighting desk or show controller, through Bridge 0.13.
- Clip timeline: Pick the part of a clip that plays on its waveform, with CasparCG and Bridge 0.13.
- Normalize audio: Set a clip or the whole production to one loudness target.
- Folder delays: In an All together folder, each graphic and OSC cue can start after its own delay.
- CasparCG 2.3: Output plays on CasparCG 2.3 servers again.

## Editor and templates

- Template library: One page previews every SVG template, each one click from download.
- More templates: New SVG templates for votes, alerts, award reveals, standings, quiz shows, crawls, word clouds and speaking timers.
