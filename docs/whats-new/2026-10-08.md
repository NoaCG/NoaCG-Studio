## Playout and Bridge

- Simpler production page: One status with one action, and a switch to turn CasparCG output on.
- Clear rehearsal: Before the first publish, the production page shows that you are rehearsing.
- Instant shortcuts: Cue shortcuts take effect at once and can use any key.
- Reliable All out: All out clears everything the server reports as on air.
- Quieter saving: Routine saves show in the header instead of a notice.

## Editor and templates

- Simpler Home: Newest productions come first, with a compact Sync control and less text.
- Fewer interruptions: NoaCG asks about unsaved changes only when replacing would lose work.
