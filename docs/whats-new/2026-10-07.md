## Playout and Bridge

- Graphics can play sounds on In, Out, steps and other changes. Attach them once to a graphic in
  a production, set each level and listen before going on air.
- A graphic cue can run on a timer: it stays up for the time you set, then does what you chose,
  on a draft or a published production.
- The rundown has Undo and Redo for moves, pastes, duplicates, deletions and folder edits. Undoing
  never changes what is on air.
- Playout settings reads which channels your CasparCG server has, shows each one's video mode and
  warns about a saved channel the server does not have.
- Picture cues offer Fit and Stretch. Fit keeps the whole picture with black bars and is the
  default; it needs the new Bridge and CasparCG 2.5 or later.

## Editor and templates

- A Pen tool draws custom shapes and lines, and Properties edits their points, curves, fill and
  stroke.
- Layer folders and asset bins keep a graphic's parts organised, and they are kept when you save
  and reopen.
- Place, replace and import images with Undo, align artwork, and move it with the keyboard. Text
  gains typography and fit controls.
- The template wizard has a Community packs shelf, starting with a pub quiz.

## AI workflows

- The CLI and the MCP server render any state of a graphic: after operator presses, at a chosen
  moment, and over a video-like background.
- The skill leaves the look to your agent and teaches the fields and behaviour an operator needs.
  Critique and NoaCG's design guidelines are opt-in.
- The NoaCG MCP server is in the official MCP Registry, and the plugin installs in Claude Code
  with one command.
