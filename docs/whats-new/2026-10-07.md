## Playout and Bridge

- Sounds for graphics: Add sounds to In, Out, steps and other graphic changes, with volume
  controls and preview.
- Timed cues: Set how long a graphic stays on air and what happens when the timer ends.
- Safer rundown editing: Undo and redo rundown changes without affecting the current output.
- Better CasparCG setup: NoaCG now detects server channels and video modes and warns about missing
  saved channels.
- Better picture playback: Picture cues now support Fit and Stretch. Fit keeps the whole image
  visible and is now the default.

## Editor and templates

- Custom drawing: Draw shapes and lines and edit their points, curves, fill and stroke.
- Better organisation: Group layers into folders and assets into bins that stay with the graphic.
- Faster editing: Improved image placement, alignment, keyboard movement, Undo and text controls.
- Community templates: The wizard now includes Community packs, starting with Pub Quiz.

## AI workflows

- More realistic previews: CLI and MCP can render graphics after interactions, at specific moments
  and over video-like backgrounds.
- More flexible AI design: The skill teaches the agent how the graphic should behave without
  dictating its visual style.
- Easier setup: NoaCG MCP is in the official MCP Registry, and the Claude Code plugin installs with
  one command.
