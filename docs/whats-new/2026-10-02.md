## Playout and Bridge

- Bridge download: NoaCG Bridge is a Windows download that pairs in one click and reconnects to
  your last CasparCG server.
- Rundown folders: Folders step through cues or play clips through, and All out clears everything
  on air.
- Clip settings: Set each clip's ending, fades, volume and trim, and loop a folder until Out.
- SPX exports: Exports play on SPX 1.2.1 and 1.4.1, with a ready SPX project holding your cues.
- Team productions: Members of a team share a production and can run it while its creator is
  signed out.

## Editor and templates

- New editor in Alpha: Edit text, shapes and colours, and set animation keys, steps and the exit
  on a timeline.
- Quiz Show kit: Three new looks, and standings whose rows trade places as the points change.
- SVG import: Nested artwork, styled text and slanted shapes import intact, and layer names are read
  in more languages.
- Simpler kits: Twelve kits with one style each, and you can edit a kit's graphics in any order.

## AI workflows

- Your own agent: Create with AI starts with your coding agent, such as Claude Code or Codex, and
  the NoaCG CLI.
