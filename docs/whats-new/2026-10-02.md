## Playout systems

- SPX exports now play on real SPX 1.2.1 and 1.4.1 servers. The production export includes a
  ready SPX project to copy into SPX, its rundown holding your cues.
- In OBS, an exported overlay now plays its entrance when its scene goes on program, and its
  control panel can run inside OBS as a Custom Browser Dock.
- Rundowns have folders. A folder can step through its cues one by one or play its clips
  through, and All out clears everything on air.
- A production can belong to a team: members add graphics from their own libraries, and a member
  can run the show while its creator is signed out.

## Editor and templates

- The new editor is open to try, marked Alpha: edit text, shapes and colours on the canvas, and
  set animation keys, steps and the exit on a timeline.
- A Quiz Show kit in three new looks, and a standings graphic whose rows trade places as the
  points change.
- SVG import keeps nested artwork, styled text and slanted shapes intact, and reads layer names
  written in more ways and in four more languages.
- Kits are simpler: twelve kits with one style each, and you can edit the graphics of a kit in
  any order.

## AI workflows

- Create with AI now starts with your own coding agent, such as Claude Code or Codex, and the
  NoaCG CLI. The built-in NoaCG agent is one click away.

## NoaCG Bridge

- NoaCG Bridge is its own Windows download. Double-click it, and pairing your browser is one
  click on the page it opens.
- Server clips have their own settings: what happens at the end, fades, sound level and trim.
  Play next runs them back to back, and a folder can loop until Out.
- The Bridge remembers your CasparCG servers and connects to the last one by itself after
  pairing.
