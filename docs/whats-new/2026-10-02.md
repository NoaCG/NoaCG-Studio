## Playout and Bridge

- Bridge download: Download NoaCG Bridge for Windows. It pairs in one click and remembers your
  CasparCG server.
- Rundown folders: Folders step through cues or play clips in a row. All out clears everything
  on air.
- Clip settings: Set each clip's ending, fades, volume and trim, and loop a folder until Out.
- SPX exports: Exports play in SPX 1.2.1 and 1.4.1, and include an SPX project with your cues.
- Team productions: Everyone in a team can open and run a shared production, even when its
  creator is away.

## Editor and templates

- Editor in Alpha: Edit text, shapes and colours, and animate In, steps and Out on a timeline.
- Quiz Show kit: New looks, and standings whose rows swap places as the points change.
- SVG import: More designs import intact, including nested groups, styled text and slanted
  shapes.
- Simpler kits: Each kit has one style, and you can edit its graphics in any order.
