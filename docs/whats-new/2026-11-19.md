## Playout and Bridge

- Clips in vMix: Bridge 0.12.0 plays clips, stills and audio in vMix from rundown cues, with the time left.
- OBS scenes: Mark a rundown folder as an OBS scene; its graphics follow your cuts through its own link.
- Scene check: The Playout panel shows whether each scene's link is loaded and seen in OBS.
- Live dock: Run the production from a narrow dock inside OBS.
- Safer typing: A cue you are typing survives a page reload and your teammates' changes.

## Editor and templates

- SVG templates: Download a set of layered SVG graphics to import, from quiz and scoreboard to end credits.
- Credits roll: Imported credits roll as one text, with the show title first.
