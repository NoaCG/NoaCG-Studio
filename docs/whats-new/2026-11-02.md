## Playout and Bridge

- Live data: Drive graphics from pasted rows, a Google Sheet, a news feed or another system.
- Viewer messages: Collect Sheet rows, YouTube comments and live chat, then choose what airs.
- Pinned controls: Keep several graphics' live controls on one screen, whichever cue is selected.
- On-air adjustments: Change place, size, type and colour on Preview, then send them to air.
- Imported OGraf: Add other makers' OGraf packages to the library and play them on browser outputs.

## Editor and templates

- SVG clocks: Imported artwork can run a match clock or count down to a set time.
- SVG credits: Imported credits can show roles and names in the artwork's own layers.
- Safer saving: Saving and leaving the editor protect your unsaved work better.
- Cleaner type: Bundled fonts set Š and Ž in their own face, with no fake bold.

## AI workflows

- One save: Your agent delivers a set of graphics in one step, optionally as a ready production.
- Clear delivery: Each save says which account received it and whether that was confirmed.
- Your brand: Agents suggest what to make first and handle your colours, fonts and logo consistently.
