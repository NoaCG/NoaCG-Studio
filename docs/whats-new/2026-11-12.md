## Playout and Bridge

- Load into vMix: Bridge 0.11.0 loads a production's graphics into vMix as a Browser input on an overlay.
- Audience questions: Ask multiple choice, open and word questions from a production's Interaction tab.
- Phone and chat: Viewers answer on their phones by link or QR code, or vote in YouTube and Twitch chat.
- Results on air: Add a question's results to the rundown as bars, a donut or a word cloud.
- One operator page: Old control links now open the production page.

## Editor and templates

- Loops: Repeat or yoyo a property or a whole layer; it keeps moving while on air.
- Animated pivots: Key the pivot of artwork, and copy and paste timeline keys.
- Your own vote board: Draw one as SVG; named layers become the bars, counts and Join QR code.
- Steady scores: A score keeps its drawn size when it gains a digit.
- Truer SVG import: Font weights and rotated Illustrator plates now match the artwork.
